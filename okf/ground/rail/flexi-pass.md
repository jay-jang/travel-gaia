> A Flexi Pass is a type of rail pass that grants a fixed number of travel days that the holder may use freely on any non-consecutive dates within a defined validity period. Unlike a continuous (consecutive) rail pass that activates a fixed block of consecutive days, a Flexi Pass allows the traveller to choose which days to travel, making it suited to itineraries that combine rail travel with city stays, sea crossings, or other modes. Eurail Global Flexi Pass and Interrail Global Flexi Pass are the most widely distributed examples.

A Flexi Pass defines two key parameters: the number of travel days (e.g., 5, 7, 10, or 15 days) and the validity period within which those days must be used (e.g., one or two months). The validity period begins from the date of first activation and runs to the calendar date that exhausts the stated period (e.g., a 1-month pass activated on 18 August expires on 17 September). On each day the pass holder wishes to travel, they activate the pass for that date — historically by writing the date in a paper pass and now by activating it digitally in the Eurail or Interrail app. Flexi Passes are sold for single countries and for multi-country global networks; price varies by travel-day count, validity period, and pass class (1st or 2nd).

**한국어 / Korean** — **유연 패스(Flexi Pass)** — Flexi Pass(유연 패스)는 정해진 유효 기간 내에서 비연속적인 날짜에 자유롭게 사용할 수 있는 고정 여행 일수를 부여하는 철도 패스 유형이다. 연속형 철도 패스와 달리 여행자가 여행할 날짜를 자유롭게 선택할 수 있어, 도시 체류·해상 이동·기타 교통수단과 조합하는 여정에 적합하다. Eurail Global Flexi Pass와 Interrail Global Flexi Pass가 가장 널리 유통되는 사례이다.

Flexi Pass는 여행 일수(예: 5·7·10·15일)와 유효 기간(예: 1개월·2개월)의 두 핵심 매개변수를 정의하며, 소지자는 여행하는 날 해당 날짜에 패스를 활성화한다. 유효 기간은 최초 활성화일부터 명시된 기간이 소진되는 달력 날짜까지 이어진다.

**Aliases:** `Flexible Pass`, `Select Pass`, `Non-Consecutive Pass`

# Distinctions
- **Flexi Pass** vs [Eurail Pass](/ground/rail/eurail-pass.md) — A Eurail Pass is the umbrella product for rail passes sold to non-EU/EEA residents; a Flexi Pass is a specific type within that range allocating non-consecutive travel days within a validity period, as opposed to the continuous Eurail Pass that grants travel on every consecutive day within a fixed block.
- **Flexi Pass** vs [Season Ticket](/ground/rail/season-ticket.md) — A Season Ticket allows unlimited travel between designated stations for a fixed calendar period; a Flexi Pass allows a fixed number of travel days across a broad network within a longer validity period — each day counts against the allocated total, so travel is not unlimited.
