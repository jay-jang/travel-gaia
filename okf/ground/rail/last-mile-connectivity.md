> Last-mile connectivity (also called first/last-mile connectivity) refers to the final leg of a passenger journey that links a major transportation hub — such as a railway station, airport, or bus terminal — to the traveller's actual final destination, or equivalently the initial leg from origin to the first major hub. In the rail and multimodal transport context it encompasses walking, cycling, micromobility, shared taxis, feeder buses, and other short-distance modes that bridge the gap between mass transit and the door-to-door trip.

Last-mile connectivity is one of the most frequently cited barriers to shifting passenger travel from private cars and short-haul aviation to rail. Distribution implications include: (1) OSDM enables multi-leg itinerary booking that includes first/last-mile segments alongside main rail legs; (2) Mobility as a Service (MaaS) platforms aggregate last-mile options into a single journey plan and payment; (3) Rail and Fly products address the airport-to-station last-mile gap by bundling train tickets with a flat-rate airport access product.

The European Commission's 2022 action plan for long-distance and cross-border passenger rail and the EU TEN-T Regulation explicitly target first/last-mile infrastructure investments at major nodes.

**한국어 / Korean** — **라스트 마일 연결(Last-Mile Connectivity)** — 라스트 마일 연결(퍼스트/라스트 마일 연결이라고도 함)은 철도역·공항·버스 터미널 등 주요 교통 허브와 여행자의 실제 최종 목적지를 잇는 여정의 마지막 구간을 가리킨다.

라스트 마일은 자가용이나 단거리 항공에서 철도로의 전환에서 가장 자주 언급되는 장벽 중 하나다. OSDM은 주요 철도 구간과 함께 퍼스트/라스트 마일 구간을 포함한 다구간 여정 예약을 가능하게 하고, MaaS 플랫폼은 자전거 공유·스쿠터·택시 등을 단일 여정 계획 및 결제로 통합한다.

# Distinctions
- **Last-Mile Connectivity** vs [Multimodal Transport](/ground/rail/multimodal-transport.md) — Multimodal Transport describes the combination of two or more distinct transport modes to complete a journey (train + bus, air + rail, etc.); Last-Mile Connectivity specifically refers to the short access or egress segments at either end of a multimodal trip that bridge mass transit to the precise origin or destination.
- **Last-Mile Connectivity** vs [Mobility as a Service](/ground/car/mobility-as-a-service.md) — Mobility as a Service (MaaS) is a platform concept that integrates multiple transport modes into a single digital journey plan and payment; Last-Mile Connectivity is the physical transport problem that MaaS platforms specifically aim to solve at the access/egress ends of longer rail or air trips.
