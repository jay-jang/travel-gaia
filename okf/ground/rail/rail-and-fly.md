> Rail and Fly is an intermodal travel product that integrates a rail journey to or from an airport with a connecting air ticket, typically sold under a single commercial arrangement or jointly marketed tariff. The rail leg transports the passenger between a city railway station and the departure or arrival airport, forming a seamless surface-to-air itinerary. Deutsche Bahn and participating Lufthansa Group airlines offer the most established example in Europe.

The classic Rail and Fly product works as follows: a passenger buys an air ticket from a Lufthansa Group airline (or other participating carrier) and receives, as a bundled benefit or optional add-on, a Deutsche Bahn train ticket valid on ICE, IC, and EC services across Germany on the day before, day of, or day after the flight. The rail ticket is priced separately or included in the airfare, carries its own fare conditions, and allows the passenger to reach any German airport in the network. Similar products exist in other European countries (Austrian Railways ÖBB+Austrian Airlines, SNCF in France, and others). Rail and Fly reduces road congestion at airports, supports intermodal sustainability goals, and is encouraged by EU transport policy promoting rail as a substitute for short-haul flights.

**한국어 / Korean** — **레일 앤 플라이(Rail and Fly)** — Rail and Fly는 공항을 오가는 철도 여정과 연결 항공편을 하나의 상업 협약 또는 공동 마케팅 운임 하에 통합하는 복합 운송 상품이다. 철도 구간이 도시 철도역과 출발·도착 공항을 연결하여 지상-항공 일관 여정을 형성한다. 유럽에서는 Deutsche Bahn과 Lufthansa Group 참여 항공사가 가장 잘 알려진 사례를 제공한다.

대표적인 Rail and Fly 상품은 다음과 같이 작동한다. 승객이 Lufthansa Group 항공사(또는 참여 항공사)의 항공편을 구매하면 독일 내 ICE·IC·EC 열차를 항공편 전날·당일·익일에 이용할 수 있는 Deutsche Bahn 기차표를 번들 혜택 또는 선택 부가 서비스로 받는다. 철도 티켓은 항공권에 포함되거나 별도 가격이 매겨지며 자체 운임 조건을 가지고, 승객은 네트워크 내 독일 전역 공항에 도달할 수 있다. 유사한 상품이 다른 유럽 국가에도 있다(오스트리아 ÖBB+Austrian Airlines, 프랑스 SNCF 등). Rail and Fly는 공항 도로 혼잡을 줄이고, 복합 운송 지속가능성 목표를 지원하며, EU 교통 정책이 단거리 항공편 대안으로 철도를 장려하는 방향과 일치한다.

# Distinctions
- **Rail and Fly** vs [Multimodal Transport](/ground/rail/multimodal-transport.md) — Multimodal transport is the general concept of combining two or more transport modes under a single contract of carriage with a single transport operator responsible for the whole journey; Rail and Fly is a specific commercial product combining rail and air segments, but the two carriers typically remain under separate contracts without a single-operator through-carriage obligation.
- **Rail and Fly** vs [Through-Ticketing](/ground/rail/through-ticketing.md) — Through-ticketing is a single ticket covering a journey across multiple operators, typically with joint liability and rebooking rights; Rail and Fly may or may not be a single through-ticket — it is often two co-marketed but legally separate tickets, without the full rebooking protections of an integrated through-ticket.
- **Rail and Fly** vs [Codeshare](/air/air-partner/codeshare.md) — A codeshare is an arrangement between airlines for one carrier to place its designator code on another's flight within the air distribution system; Rail and Fly integrates a rail leg with an air leg — an entirely different mode — and is typically a commercial partnership agreement rather than a codeshare.
