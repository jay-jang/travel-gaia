> A Passenger Service Obligation (PSO) is a legally defined requirement imposed by a competent public authority on a transport operator — in rail, air, or road — to maintain a service that the market would not provide on a commercial basis alone, in exchange for state compensation. In EU rail, PSOs are governed by Regulation (EC) No 1370/2007 on public passenger transport services by rail and by road, which establishes the framework for public service contracts (PSCs), competitive tendering, and the net-cost principle limiting permissible compensation.

A PSO applies where commercial viability alone would not sustain a transport service — remote communities, thin regional rail lines, or socially necessary services at low-demand hours. EU Regulation 1370/2007 is the primary legal instrument for rail PSOs, requiring a formal Public Service Contract (PSC) specifying routes, timetables, quality standards, and compensation, with compensation limited to the net cost of the obligation plus a reasonable profit. Competitive tendering is generally mandatory. Aviation PSOs under EU Regulation 1008/2008 Articles 16–18 allow member states to designate a single carrier for uneconomic remote routes. PSOs determine which routes are market-operated (open access) and which are publicly funded.

**한국어 / Korean** — **여객 서비스 의무(PSO)** — 여객 서비스 의무(PSO)는 권한 있는 공공당국이 운송 사업자(철도, 항공, 도로)에게 법적으로 부과하는 의무로, 시장이 상업적으로 단독 제공하지 않을 서비스를 국가 보상을 조건으로 유지하도록 요구한다. EU 철도에서는 EU 규정(EC) 1370/2007이 PSO를 규율하며, 공공 서비스 계약(PSC), 경쟁 입찰, 허용 보상을 제한하는 순비용 원칙의 틀을 수립한다.

PSO는 상업적 실행 가능성만으로 운송 서비스가 유지될 수 없는 상황—오지 지역사회, 얇은 지역 철도 노선, 수요가 낮은 시간대의 사회적 필수 서비스—에 적용된다. EU 규정 1370/2007은 노선·시간표·품질기준·보상을 명시하는 공식 공공 서비스 계약(PSC)을 요구하며, 보상은 PSO 이행의 순비용과 합리적 이윤으로 제한된다. PSO는 어떤 노선이 시장 경쟁(개방형 접근)이고 어떤 노선이 공적 자금 지원(PSO)인지를 규정한다.

# Distinctions
- **Passenger Service Obligation** vs [Open Access Operator](/ground/rail/open-access-operator.md) — An open access operator enters the rail market commercially on a chosen route without public funding; a PSO operator is contracted by a public authority to provide specific services in exchange for state compensation on routes that would not be commercially viable without it.
- **Passenger Service Obligation** vs [Rail Passenger Rights Regulation (EU) 2021/782](/ground/rail/rail-passenger-rights-regulation-eu-2021-782.md) — The Rail Passenger Rights Regulation defines passenger rights against operators when disruptions occur; a PSO is a public-authority contract determining which rail services must operate and how operators are compensated for uneconomic routes — PSO and passenger rights address different aspects of rail governance.
