> A walk-up rail ticket valid only during designated off-peak hours — typically outside weekday morning and evening rush periods — priced below an Anytime ticket. In the UK National Rail network, Off-Peak is one of the three primary walk-up fare families (alongside Anytime and Advance) and includes a cheaper Super Off-Peak tier with tighter time restrictions.

Off-Peak fares are the rail industry's primary mechanism for spreading demand away from busy commuter periods. Valid times vary by route: on UK National Rail, most Off-Peak tickets allow travel from 09:30 on weekdays (09:00 in some areas) and all day on weekends. Where two off-peak price points exist, the cheaper is labelled Super Off-Peak and carries the most restrictive time conditions. Off-Peak tickets are typically open (not train-specific) within permitted times, making them more flexible than Advance tickets but priced higher. Railcard discounts generally apply to Off-Peak and Super Off-Peak fares. Revenue managers do not control Off-Peak inventory (they are available unless restricted) but actively manage the threshold between peak and off-peak to smooth demand curves.

**한국어 / Korean** — **비성수 시간대 요금(Off-Peak Fare)** — 평일 아침·저녁 러시아워 등 지정 성수 시간대를 피한 시간대에만 유효한 walk-up 철도 승차권으로, Anytime 승차권보다 저렴하게 책정된다. 영국 National Rail에서 Off-Peak는 Anytime·Advance와 함께 세 가지 주요 walk-up 요금 패밀리 중 하나이며, 더 엄격한 시간 제한을 가진 더 저렴한 Super Off-Peak 등급을 포함한다.

Off-Peak 요금은 붐비는 통근 시간대에서 수요를 분산시키기 위한 철도 업계의 주요 메커니즘이다. 유효 시간은 노선마다 다르다. 영국 National Rail의 경우 대부분의 Off-Peak 승차권은 평일 09:30(일부 지역은 09:00) 이후와 주말 전일 이용이 가능하다. 두 가지 off-peak 가격대가 존재할 경우, 더 저렴한 것을 Super Off-Peak라 하며 가장 제한적인 시간 조건을 갖는다. Off-Peak 승차권은 허용 시간 내에서 열차 지정 없이 이용 가능한 오픈 승차권으로, Advance보다 유연하지만 가격은 더 높다. Railcard 할인은 일반적으로 Off-Peak 및 Super Off-Peak 요금에 적용된다. 수익 관리자는 Off-Peak 재고를 직접 관리하지 않지만(제한이 없는 한 이용 가능) 수요 곡선 완화를 위해 성수·비성수 시간대 구분을 능동적으로 관리한다.

**Aliases:** `Off Peak Ticket`, `Super Off-Peak Fare`, `Off-Peak Return`, `Off-Peak Single`

# Distinctions
- **Off-Peak Fare** vs Anytime Fare — An Anytime fare is valid at any hour including peak periods; an Off-Peak fare is valid only outside designated peak windows and is priced lower as a result.
- **Off-Peak Fare** vs [Advance Fare](/ground/rail/advance-fare.md) — An Advance fare must be purchased before the travel day, is tied to a specific train service, and is typically non-refundable; an Off-Peak fare is a walk-up ticket bought any time (including at the station), valid on any permitted train within off-peak hours, and usually exchangeable for a fee.
- **Off-Peak Fare** vs [Season Ticket](/ground/rail/season-ticket.md) — A Season Ticket provides unlimited travel between two stations over a set period (week, month, year) suited to regular commuters; an Off-Peak fare is a single-journey (or return) ticket purchased per trip with no unlimited travel benefit.
