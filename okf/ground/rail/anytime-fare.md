> A fully flexible, walk-up rail ticket that places no restrictions on departure time, allowing travel on any permitted train on the valid date including peak hours. It is the highest-priced of the three UK National Rail walk-up fare families (Anytime, Off-Peak, Advance) and is typically open for a day or as a return within a month.

Anytime fares are designed for passengers who need maximum travel flexibility — typically business travellers, last-minute passengers, or commuters whose schedule may change. Unlike Off-Peak tickets, Anytime fares are valid during the busiest peak hours (typically weekday mornings and evenings) and are therefore priced at a premium. Most Anytime tickets are not train-specific: an Anytime Single is valid on any service between origin and destination on the date shown, and an Anytime Return permits outbound travel on the day shown and return within one month. On many inter-city routes, Anytime fares are governed by the National Rail Conditions of Travel (NRCoT) and must be purchased from a licensed retailer. Railcard discounts may apply but are less common than for Off-Peak tickets. For regular travellers, a Season Ticket will almost always be more economical than repeated Anytime purchases.

**한국어 / Korean** — **무제한 시간 요금(Anytime Fare)** — 출발 시간에 어떠한 제한도 없어 성수 시간대를 포함해 유효일 내 임의의 허가된 열차로 이용할 수 있는 완전 유연형 walk-up 철도 승차권이다. 영국 National Rail의 세 가지 walk-up 요금 패밀리(Anytime·Off-Peak·Advance) 중 가장 비싸며, 보통 당일 또는 한 달 내 왕복으로 유효하다.

Anytime 요금은 최대의 이용 유연성이 필요한 승객, 즉 전형적으로 비즈니스 여행자, 막차 탑승객, 또는 일정이 바뀔 수 있는 통근자를 위해 설계되었다. Off-Peak 승차권과 달리 Anytime 요금은 가장 붐비는 성수 시간대(통상 평일 아침·저녁)에도 유효하므로 할증 가격이 책정된다. 대부분의 Anytime 승차권은 열차 지정이 없다. Anytime Single은 표시된 날짜에 출발지에서 목적지까지 임의 서비스로 유효하고, Anytime Return은 표시된 날 출발, 한 달 내 귀환을 허용한다. 많은 도시 간 노선에서 Anytime 요금은 National Rail Conditions of Travel(NRCoT)에 의해 규율되며 공인 소매업체에서 구매해야 한다. Railcard 할인이 적용될 수 있지만 Off-Peak 승차권에 비해 덜 일반적이다. 정기 이용자에게는 Season Ticket이 반복적인 Anytime 구매보다 거의 항상 경제적이다.

# Distinctions
- **Anytime Fare** vs Off-Peak Fare — An Off-Peak fare restricts travel to designated quiet periods and costs less; an Anytime fare is valid at all hours including peak periods, at a higher price, providing the flexibility to board the earliest or busiest trains.
- **Anytime Fare** vs [Advance Fare](/ground/rail/advance-fare.md) — An Advance fare must be booked before the day of travel and locks the passenger to a specific train; an Anytime fare requires no advance purchase, can be bought at the gateline seconds before boarding, and is valid on any permitted service.
- **Anytime Fare** vs [Season Ticket](/ground/rail/season-ticket.md) — A Season Ticket covers unlimited journeys between two fixed points over a set period for a recurring commuter; an Anytime fare is a single-journey (or one-month return) ticket per trip, offering route and date flexibility rather than unlimited frequency.
