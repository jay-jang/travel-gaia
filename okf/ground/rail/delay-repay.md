> A mandatory UK rail passenger compensation scheme under which train operators must offer proportional refunds of the ticket price when a journey is delayed beyond a defined threshold — typically 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes — regardless of the cause. The Office of Rail and Road (ORR) sets minimum standards; individual Train Operating Companies (TOCs) implement the scheme and may exceed the minimum entitlements.

Delay Repay was introduced in the UK in 2008 and progressively tightened, with most TOCs now operating on a "Delay Repay 15" basis, meaning compensation begins at 15 minutes of delay. The standard entitlements are: 15–29 minutes: 25% refund; 30–59 minutes: 50% refund; 60–119 minutes: 100% refund (single journey equivalent); 120+ minutes: 100% plus an additional 25% in some schemes. Compensation is calculated on the single-leg fare, not the full return fare, unless both legs are delayed. Passengers may claim online, by post, or via the TOC's mobile app, usually within 28 days of travel. Force majeure events — extraordinary circumstances outside the operator's control — do not exempt UK operators from Delay Repay, unlike the EU Rail Passenger Rights Regulation which permits such exemptions. Delay Repay is distinct from the EU's Rail Passenger Rights Regulation (EU) 2021/782 (PRR), which applies to cross-border and some domestic services on the continent, and from UK Regulation 261, which applies to air passengers.

**한국어 / Korean** — **지연 환급 제도(Delay Repay)** — 열차 지연이 정해진 임계값을 초과할 경우 운행 원인과 무관하게 승객에게 운임의 일정 비율을 환급하도록 강제하는 영국의 철도 승객 보상 제도. ORR이 최소 기준을 설정하고 TOC가 시행한다.

2008년 도입되어 점진적으로 강화된 제도로, 현재 대부분의 TOC는 15분 지연부터 보상하는 "Delay Repay 15" 기준을 적용한다. 표준 보상액은 15–29분 25%, 30–59분 50%, 60–119분 100%, 120분 이상 일부 제도에서 125%이며, 영국 운영사는 불가항력 사유에도 보상 의무를 면제받지 못한다.

# Distinctions
- **Delay Repay** vs [Rail Passenger Rights Regulation (EU) 2021/782](/ground/rail/rail-passenger-rights-regulation-eu-2021-782.md) — The EU PRR applies to cross-border and some domestic rail services in EU member states and permits force majeure exemptions from compensation; Delay Repay is a UK-specific domestic scheme enforced by the ORR that offers no such exemption and activates at a lower threshold (15 minutes).
- **Delay Repay** vs [UK Regulation 261](/common/disruption/uk-regulation-261.md) — UK Regulation 261 is the post-Brexit aviation passenger rights rule governing compensation for flight delays and cancellations; Delay Repay is the equivalent rail-sector scheme administered by rail TOCs under ORR oversight.
