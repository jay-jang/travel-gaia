> An excess waiver (also marketed as Super CDW, SCDW, or excess-reduction cover) is a product offered by car rental companies at the counter or pre-trip online that eliminates or substantially reduces the renter's residual financial liability — the excess or deductible — that persists even when a CDW or LDW is already in place. With an excess waiver, the renter faces no out-of-pocket cost for covered damage or theft, provided the rental terms are not breached.

An excess waiver is distinct from CDW and LDW: CDW/LDW limit the renter's liability to the excess amount; the excess waiver then removes (or halves) that residual amount. The product is typically sold at the counter as a daily surcharge, or alternatively purchased pre-trip through third-party car hire excess insurance policies — which work differently in that they reimburse the renter after the rental company has already charged the excess, rather than preventing the charge entirely. Counter-sold excess waivers cover the same vehicle damage events as the underlying CDW/LDW; standard exclusions (lost keys, roof and undercarriage damage, tyre sidewalls, misfuelling) usually continue to apply. ACRISS vehicle coverage codes include Super CDW (SCDW) codes in supplier catalogues, enabling consistent product exchange across distribution channels.

**한국어 / Korean** — **자기부담금 면제 상품(Excess Waiver)** — 자기부담금 면제 상품(Excess Waiver, Super CDW라고도 함)은 렌터카 회사가 카운터 또는 온라인으로 제공하는 상품으로, CDW나 LDW가 있어도 남아 있는 이용자의 재정적 책임(excess 또는 deductible)을 소멸하거나 대폭 줄여 준다. 이 상품을 구매하면 이용자는 렌털 조건을 위반하지 않는 한 면책 대상 손해·도난 사고에 대해 자기부담금 없이 보호받는다.

자기부담금 면제 상품은 CDW, LDW와 구별된다. CDW/LDW는 이용자의 책임을 excess 금액으로 한정하고, excess waiver는 그 잔여 금액을 없애거나 절반으로 줄인다. 이 상품은 보통 카운터에서 일일 추가 요금으로 구매하거나, 사전에 제3자 car hire excess insurance 보험을 통해 구입할 수도 있다. 카운터에서 구매하는 excess waiver는 CDW/LDW가 보장하는 차량 손해 이벤트와 동일한 범위를 커버하며, 차키 분실·루프·하부·타이어 사이드월·혼유 등 표준 제외 조항은 excess waiver를 구매해도 계속 적용된다.

**Aliases:** `Super CDW`, `Super Collision Damage Waiver`, `SCDW`, `Excess Reduction`, `Car Hire Excess Insurance`

# Distinctions
- **Excess Waiver** vs [Excess](/ground/car/excess.md) — The excess is the renter's residual financial liability even when CDW is in place; the excess waiver is the product purchased to remove that residual liability.
- **Excess Waiver** vs [Collision Damage Waiver](/ground/car/collision-damage-waiver.md) — CDW limits the renter's liability for collision damage to the excess amount; the excess waiver then eliminates that remaining amount. CDW alone still leaves the renter exposed to the excess.
- **Excess Waiver** vs [Loss Damage Waiver](/ground/car/loss-damage-waiver.md) — LDW bundles collision and theft waiver into one product but still leaves an excess/deductible; the excess waiver removes that remaining deductible.
