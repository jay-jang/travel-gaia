> A cross-border restriction is a standard condition in rental car agreements that prohibits the renter from driving the vehicle across an international border without advance written authorisation from the rental company. Taking a vehicle across a border without authorisation typically voids all counter protection products (CDW, LDW, SLI) and exposes the renter to full financial liability for any damage, theft, or loss.

Cross-border restrictions exist because vehicle registration, road regulations, insurance requirements, and liability frameworks differ between countries, and most standard rental agreements are written to a specific national or regional jurisdiction. Where cross-border travel is allowed — for example within the Schengen Area or between the US and Canada for some fleet categories — it typically requires a formal cross-border authorisation letter issued by the rental company (sometimes at an additional fee), confirmation of adequate third-party liability coverage under the destination country's rules, and in some cases a specific Cross-Border Insurance certificate. Renters should always check the specific terms for the collection country and each destination country before travel, as restrictions vary by company, fleet, and country pair.

**한국어 / Korean** — **국경 이동 제한(Cross-Border Restriction)** — 국경 이동 제한(Cross-Border Restriction)은 임차인이 렌터카 회사의 사전 서면 승인 없이 차량을 국경 너머로 운행하는 것을 금지하는 표준 렌탈 계약 조건이다. 승인 없이 국경을 넘으면 일반적으로 모든 카운터 보호 상품(CDW, LDW, SLI)이 무효가 되어 임차인이 모든 손해·도난·손실에 대해 전액 재정적 책임을 지게 된다.

국경 이동 제한이 적용되는 이유는 나라마다 차량 등록 요건, 도로 규정, 보험 의무, 배상 책임 체계가 다르며, 표준 렌탈 계약은 특정 국가 또는 지역 법체계를 기준으로 작성되기 때문이다. 셴겐 지역 내 이동이나 일부 차량 종류에 대한 미국-캐나다 이동처럼 국경 이동이 허용되는 경우에도, 일반적으로 렌탈 회사가 발급하는 공식 국경 이동 승인서(때로는 추가 비용 부과), 도착국 규정에 따른 적절한 제3자 배상책임 보장 확인, 경우에 따라 특정 국경 이동 보험 증명서가 필요하다. 제한 사항은 회사·차량·국가 조합마다 다르므로 여행 전에 반드시 출발국 및 각 목적국의 구체적인 조건을 확인해야 한다.

# Distinctions
- **Cross-Border Restriction** vs [Rental Agreement](/ground/car/rental-agreement.md) — The rental agreement is the master contract governing the whole rental transaction; the cross-border restriction is a specific clause within that agreement that restricts geographic use of the vehicle.
- **Cross-Border Restriction** vs [Collision Damage Waiver](/ground/car/collision-damage-waiver.md) — CDW waives the renter's liability for collision damage when the vehicle is used within the authorised territory; an unauthorised border crossing voids CDW, converting the waiver into full renter liability.
- **Cross-Border Restriction** vs [International Driving Permit](/ground/car/international-driving-permit.md) — An International Driving Permit is a document authorising a driver to operate a vehicle in foreign countries under the 1949 or 1968 Geneva Conventions; a cross-border restriction is the rental company's separate contractual prohibition — an IDP does not by itself grant permission to take the rental vehicle across a border.
