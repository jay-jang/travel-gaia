> Ride-hailing is an on-demand ground transportation service in which passengers request a trip in real time through a mobile application that matches them with a nearby driver who uses their own vehicle. Major platforms include Uber, Lyft, Grab, DiDi, Bolt, and Ola. Regulators in most jurisdictions classify these platforms as Transportation Network Companies (TNCs), a legally distinct category from metered taxis and traditional licensed hire cars.

Ride-hailing platforms operate a two-sided marketplace: the app aggregates supply (driver partners and their personal vehicles) and demand (passengers) through GPS matching, dynamic surge pricing, in-app payment, and driver rating systems. Unlike traditional taxis, which may be hailed on-street and are subject to regulated meter fares, ride-hailing trips are pre-booked through the app, priced algorithmically (often including a surge multiplier during peak demand), and paid cashlessly. From a travel distribution standpoint, ride-hailing is significant as a last-mile connectivity solution for airport access and as a component of Mobility as a Service (MaaS) bundles. Global platforms (Uber, Lyft) compete with regional champions (Grab in Southeast Asia, DiDi in China, Bolt in Europe and Africa, Ola in India). The International Transport Forum (OECD ITF) frames ride-hailing and broader "app-based mobility services" as a distinct regulatory category requiring dedicated licensing frameworks, minimum wage protections for drivers, and data-sharing obligations.

차량 호출 플랫폼은 GPS 매칭·동적 급증 요금·인앱 결제·평가 시스템을 통해 공급과 수요를 연결하는 양면 시장으로 운영된다. 노상 호출이 가능하고 규제 미터 요금이 적용되는 전통 택시와 달리, 차량 호출은 앱을 통해 사전 요청되고 알고리즘으로 가격이 책정된다. 여행 유통 관점에서 공항 접근을 위한 라스트 마일 수단 및 MaaS 번들의 구성 요소로 중요하다.

# Distinctions
- **Ride-Hailing** vs [Mobility as a Service](/ground/car/mobility-as-a-service.md) — MaaS is the integration layer that aggregates ride-hailing alongside public transit, car rental, bike-share, and other modes into a single digital offer; ride-hailing is one of the constituent supply modes that MaaS platforms orchestrate. A ride-hailing platform is a single-mode marketplace; a MaaS platform is a multi-modal intermediary that may include ride-hailing as a component.
- **Ride-Hailing** vs [Airport Transfer](/tours/tour-product/airport-transfer.md) — An airport transfer is a pre-arranged, point-to-point ground transportation service booked in advance as part of a travel itinerary, often as a tour or hotel ancillary; ride-hailing is an on-demand service requested in real time through an app, with no pre-arrangement required and dynamic algorithmic pricing. Airport transfers are a booking product sold by DMCs and OTAs; ride-hailing is a platform marketplace.
