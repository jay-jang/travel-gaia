> A time-based motorway permit — sold as a physical windscreen sticker or digital registration — that authorises a vehicle to use designated toll roads in countries that operate vignette schemes, including Austria, Switzerland, Czech Republic, Hungary, and others. Car rental companies are required to ensure their vehicles carry valid vignettes for the countries in the rental itinerary, and customers may face fines for non-compliance.

Vignette systems are distinct from per-use tolling: a vignette grants unlimited access to covered roads for a fixed duration (e.g., 10-day, 2-month, annual) regardless of distance driven. Major European vignette countries include Austria (ASFINAG digital vignette since 2022), Switzerland (annual CHF 40 vignette for all vehicles), Czech Republic (e-vignette), Hungary (e-matricula), Slovenia, Bulgaria, and North Macedonia. For car rental, the vignette responsibility is usually built into the rental tariff or offered as an add-on. Electronic vignettes are linked to the vehicle registration plate and are enforced by automated licence plate readers.

**한국어 / Korean** — **비네트(Vignette)** — 비네트 제도를 운영하는 국가(오스트리아·스위스·체코·헝가리 등)에서 지정된 유료 도로 통행을 허가하는 기간제 허가증으로, 실물 앞유리 스티커 또는 디지털 등록 형태로 판매된다.

비네트 제도는 사용별 통행료와 구별된다. 비네트는 주행 거리와 무관하게 정해진 기간 동안 대상 도로에 무제한 통행을 허용한다. 렌터카의 경우 비네트 비용은 보통 렌탈 요금에 포함되거나 부가 옵션으로 제공된다.

# Distinctions
- **Vignette (Road Toll)** vs [Electronic Toll Service](/ground/car/electronic-toll-service.md) — An Electronic Toll Service (e.g., a transponder or OBU) is charged per use each time the vehicle passes through a toll point; a vignette grants unlimited access to covered roads for a fixed period with a single upfront purchase.
