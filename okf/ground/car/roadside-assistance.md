> Roadside Assistance (also marketed as Roadside Protection or Emergency Roadside Service) is an optional or included benefit offered by car rental companies that covers emergency help when a rental vehicle becomes immobile on a public road due to a mechanical breakdown, flat tyre, dead battery, fuel exhaustion, or lockout. When purchased, the rental company dispatches or arranges assistance at no additional call-out charge; without it, the renter may bear all third-party recovery costs.

Roadside assistance coverage typically includes towing to the nearest authorised repair facility (often up to a specified distance), battery jump-start, flat tyre change (using the vehicle's spare), emergency fuel delivery (fuel cost charged separately), and lockout assistance when keys are locked inside the vehicle. Some programmes additionally cover trip interruption benefits — hotel and meal expenses if the breakdown strands the renter overnight — and 24-hour telephone assistance lines. The product is usually sold per rental day alongside collision and theft waivers; premium programmes may extend coverage regardless of fault, while basic agreements exclude coverage if the breakdown is caused by renter misuse, off-road driving, or violation of the rental agreement terms. Major rental companies market the product under proprietary names: Hertz offers "Premium Emergency Roadside Service", Enterprise uses "Roadside Assistance Protection", and Avis offers "Emergency Saver Service". Fleet-level roadside assistance is also a key component of car rental company agreements with breakdown and recovery networks such as AA, RAC (UK), ADAC (Germany), and AAA (US).

긴급 출동 서비스 보장은 일반적으로 가장 가까운 공식 정비소까지의 견인(흔히 지정 거리 이내), 배터리 점프 스타트, 타이어 펑크 교체(차량 스페어 타이어 사용), 긴급 연료 공급(연료비는 별도 청구), 차량 잠김 시 잠금 해제 지원을 포함한다. 일부 프로그램은 추가로 여행 중단 혜택(고장으로 렌터가 하룻밤 발이 묶인 경우 호텔·식사비)과 24시간 전화 지원을 포함한다. 이 상품은 일반적으로 충돌·도난 면책 상품과 함께 렌탈 일 단위로 판매된다. 프리미엄 프로그램은 과실 여부와 무관하게 보장하는 반면, 기본 약관은 렌터의 부주의, 비포장 도로 주행, 렌탈 계약 위반으로 인한 고장은 보장에서 제외한다. 주요 렌터카 회사는 고유 명칭으로 판매한다. Hertz는 "Premium Emergency Roadside Service", Enterprise는 "Roadside Assistance Protection", Avis는 "Emergency Saver Service"를 제공한다. 차량 단위 긴급 출동 서비스는 렌터카 회사의 영국 AA·RAC, 독일 ADAC, 미국 AAA 등 고장 구난 네트워크와의 계약에서도 핵심 구성 요소다.

# Distinctions
- **Roadside Assistance** vs [Collision Damage Waiver](/ground/car/collision-damage-waiver.md) — Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) reduces or eliminates the renter's financial liability for damage to the rental vehicle caused by a collision or accident; Roadside Assistance covers the cost of emergency recovery services when a vehicle becomes immobile. A breakdown that is not caused by a collision is covered by Roadside Assistance, not CDW; and CDW does not pay for towing or lockout services.
- **Roadside Assistance** vs [Excess](/ground/car/excess.md) — The Excess (also called excess liability or deductible) is the maximum amount a renter pays out-of-pocket for an insured damage event covered under CDW or similar waivers; Roadside Assistance is a separate, fee-based benefit for emergency recovery situations that is not linked to the excess amount and operates independently of damage waivers.
