> An optional rental add-on that enables electronic toll payment during the rental period through an in-vehicle transponder or license-plate recognition, billed as a daily flat administrative fee plus the actual tolls incurred.

Electronic toll services address a significant friction point in car rental: without enrolment, a renter who drives through an all-electronic or cashless toll lane risks receiving a toll-violation notice weeks after return, to which the rental company typically adds an administrative fee of USD 15–25 per incident. With the service activated, an in-vehicle transponder pre-registered to the rental company's toll account is read at each plaza, and the charges (plus the daily service fee, typically USD 3–5) appear on the final rental invoice. The service usually covers all standard toll facility types — highway, bridge, tunnel — across multiple states or countries, though some emerging zones (e.g. NYC Congestion Relief Zone) may be billed separately after return.

**한국어 / Korean** — **전자 통행료 서비스(Electronic Toll Service)** — 렌탈 기간 동안 차량 내 트랜스폰더 또는 번호판 인식을 통해 전자 통행료를 납부할 수 있게 하는 선택형 렌탈 부가 서비스. 일 단위 정액 관리 수수료와 실제 발생 통행료를 합산해 청구된다.

전자 통행료 서비스는 렌탈 차량의 주요 불편 사항을 해소한다. 서비스 활성화 시 렌탈사의 통행료 계정에 등록된 트랜스폰더가 각 톨게이트에서 감지되고, 요금(일 서비스 수수료 USD 3~5 포함)이 최종 청구서에 표시된다. 서비스는 통상 여러 주 또는 국가의 고속도로·교량·터널 통행료를 포함하나, 일부 신규 구역은 반납 후 별도 청구될 수 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Electronic Toll Service** vs [Concession Recovery Fee](/ground/car/concession-recovery-fee.md) — A concession recovery fee is a fixed daily charge that helps the rental company recoup airport concession costs and appears on every airport rental regardless of toll usage; the electronic toll service is an opt-in add-on covering actual toll payments, charged only when enrolled.
