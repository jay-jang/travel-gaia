> A membership-based or pay-per-use short-term vehicle rental service accessed via mobile app or key-card — without a staffed rental counter — in which members book vehicles for minutes or hours rather than days. Car-sharing (e.g., Zipcar, Share Now, Communauto) is structurally distinct from traditional car rental in its access model, vehicle placement, and pricing unit.

Car-sharing operates in two principal models: (1) station-based (round-trip), where a vehicle is picked up and returned to the same designated pod/station, and (2) free-floating, where a vehicle can be picked up and dropped off anywhere within a defined operating zone. Pricing typically includes a per-minute and/or per-hour rate covering fuel, insurance, and a distance allowance. Car-sharing integrates with Mobility as a Service (MaaS) platforms and public transit ticketing systems. The shared-mobility distinction from ride-hailing is that the member drives themselves (self-drive), eliminating a commercial driver.

**한국어 / Korean** — **카셰어링(Car-Sharing)** — 멤버십 또는 사용별 요금 기반의 단기 차량 이용 서비스로, 직원이 있는 렌탈 카운터 없이 모바일 앱 또는 키카드로 접근한다.

카셰어링은 거점 기반(왕복형)과 자유 이동형(free-floating)의 두 가지 주요 모델로 운영된다. 요금은 연료·보험이 포함된 분당·시간당 구조이며, MaaS 플랫폼 및 대중교통 시스템과 통합해 복합 도시 모빌리티 생태계를 구성한다.

**Aliases:** `Carsharing`, `Car Share`, `Shared Car`, `Short-Term Car Rental`

# Distinctions
- **Car-Sharing** vs [Ride-Hailing](/ground/car/ride-hailing.md) — Ride-hailing (e.g., Uber, Grab) provides a vehicle with a commercial driver for a point-to-point trip; car-sharing provides a self-drive vehicle to a member who operates it independently for a flexible duration.
- **Car-Sharing** vs [Rental Agreement](/ground/car/rental-agreement.md) — A traditional car rental is accessed at a staffed counter, priced by the day, and governed by a formal rental agreement; car-sharing is activated digitally via app, priced by the minute or hour, and has no counter interaction — the membership terms replace the per-rental agreement.
