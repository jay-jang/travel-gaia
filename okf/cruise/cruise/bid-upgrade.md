> A revenue-management programme in which a cruise line invites already-booked guests to submit a per-person cash bid for a higher cabin category from unsold inventory, with the line awarding upgrades to the highest-value accepted bids.

Bid upgrade programmes are typically triggered after the final payment date, when remaining unsold inventory is unlikely to sell at rack rate. Guests receive an email invitation with a bidding interface showing eligible upgrade categories and a suggested minimum bid per person. Bids are blind — guests do not see competitors' bids. If the line accepts a bid, the guest's card is charged the bid amount per person (usually based on double occupancy), and the original stateroom is released. The programme monetises inventory that would otherwise sail empty while preserving higher rack-rate visibility in early distribution. Programme names include Royal Caribbean's RoyalUp, Norwegian's Upgrade Advantage, and Celebrity's MoveUp.

**한국어 / Korean** — **비드 업그레이드(Bid Upgrade)** — 크루즈 선사가 이미 예약한 승객에게 원래 구매한 선실 등급보다 높은 등급을 위해 1인당 추가 현금 입찰을 제출할 기회를 제공하는 수익 관리 프로그램.

비드 업그레이드 프로그램은 일반적으로 최종 결제일 이후 잔여 미판매 재고가 정가에 판매될 가능성이 낮아질 때 자동으로 활성화된다. 승객은 적격 업그레이드 등급과 권장 최소 입찰가가 담긴 이메일 초대를 받는다. 입찰은 블라인드 방식이다. 선사가 입찰을 수락하면 1인당 입찰 금액이 청구된다.

# Distinctions
- **Bid Upgrade** vs [Guarantee Cabin (GTY)](/cruise/cruise/guarantee-cabin-gty.md) — A Guarantee Cabin (GTY) booking means the guest pays the minimum fare for a category and accepts any stateroom the line assigns, sometimes receiving a complimentary upgrade; it is a rate/booking type chosen at reservation. A Bid Upgrade is a post-booking, opt-in auction where a guest who already has a specific cabin pays extra to compete for a higher category.
