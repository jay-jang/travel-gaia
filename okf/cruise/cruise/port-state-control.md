> Port State Control (PSC) is the inspection of foreign ships in national ports by the maritime authority of the port state to verify that the ship, its equipment, and its manning and operation comply with the requirements of international maritime conventions (primarily SOLAS, MARPOL, MLC 2006, and STCW). PSC acts as a safety-net enforcement layer supplementing flag-state oversight, coordinated through regional Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) — principally the Paris MOU (Europe/North Atlantic) and the Tokyo MOU (Asia-Pacific). MOUs publish annual ship-performance statistics that determine flag-state white/grey/black listings.

Port state control is the second-tier enforcement layer for international maritime conventions: the flag state bears primary responsibility for its ships' compliance, but every port state may inspect foreign ships calling at its ports and take corrective measures, including detention, when serious deficiencies are found. Inspectors check certificates (ISM Safety Management Certificate, ISPS International Ship Security Certificate, MARPOL certificates, MLC 2006 Maritime Labour Certificate, STCW crew certificates), the ship's physical condition, crew familiarization, and emergency equipment. Deficient ships receive a deficiency notice; ships with serious deficiencies are detained until corrected. The Paris MOU and Tokyo MOU coordinate inspection data across member states, enabling risk-based targeting of higher-risk vessels and flag-state performance scoring.

**한국어 / Korean** — **항만국통제(PSC)** — 항만국통제(PSC)는 항만국 해양당국이 자국 항구에 입항한 외국 선박을 검사하여 선박·설비·선원의 상태와 운항이 국제 해사협약(주로 SOLAS, MARPOL, MLC 2006, STCW)을 준수하는지 확인하는 제도다. PSC는 기국 감독을 보완하는 두 번째 안전망 역할을 하며, 지역별 양해각서(MOU)를 통해 조율된다.

항만국통제는 국제 해사협약의 2차 이행 층위이다. 기국이 선박 준수에 대한 1차 책임을 지지만, 항구에 입항하는 모든 항만국은 외국 선박을 검사하고 중대한 결함 발견 시 억류 등 시정 조치를 취할 수 있다. 검사관은 증서·선박 물리적 상태·선원 숙련도·비상 설비를 점검한다. Paris MOU와 Tokyo MOU는 회원국 간 검사 데이터를 조율하여 고위험 선박을 우선 대상으로 하고 기국 실적 점수를 산정한다.

# Distinctions
- **Port State Control** vs [Flag State](/cruise/cruise/flag-state.md) — The flag state is the state in which a ship is registered and is the primary enforcer responsible for ensuring the ship meets international standards; port state control is the secondary inspection layer where the state whose port the ship enters can inspect and enforce the same standards independently.
- **Port State Control** vs [ISM Code](/cruise/cruise/ism-code.md) — The ISM Code defines the safety management standards ships and companies must meet; Port State Control is the enforcement mechanism — the port inspections that verify a ship holds a valid Safety Management Certificate and operates in accordance with ISM requirements.
