> Prepaid Gratuities is a cruise industry commercial offering that allows passengers to pay their expected onboard service charges or gratuities before the voyage begins, typically at the cruise line's published per-person, per-night rate. By prepaying, passengers settle a recurring onboard charge in advance, providing budget certainty and often a promotional incentive when offered as part of a fare package.

Most major cruise lines automatically assess a daily service charge (variously called daily gratuities, crew appreciation, or automatic gratuity) per passenger per night for stateroom attendants, dining staff, and other crew. This charge appears on the onboard folio unless pre-purchased. Prepaid Gratuities programs allow guests to purchase this total amount before embarkation — either as a standalone purchase at booking, as part of a promotional package (e.g., "beverage package + prepaid gratuities"), or through a travel agent incentive. The prepaid amount is credited to the passenger's onboard account so that the automatic daily charge is covered for the duration of the voyage. Cruise lines frequently include prepaid gratuities as a value-add in wave-season promotions, which simplifies budgeting for passengers and increases ancillary revenue recognition at booking time for the line.

**한국어 / Korean** — **선납 봉사료(프리페이드 그래추이티)** — 선납 봉사료(Prepaid Gratuities)는 승객이 크루즈 항해 시작 전에 선박 내 서비스 요금이나 봉사료를 미리 지불할 수 있도록 하는 크루즈 업계의 상업적 서비스다. 일반적으로 크루즈 선사가 공시한 1인당 1박 요금으로 책정되며, 승객은 선납을 통해 반복적인 선박 내 요금을 사전에 정산하고 예산 확실성을 확보한다.

주요 크루즈 선사 대부분은 객실 담당 승무원, 식음료 직원 및 기타 승무원을 위해 승객 1인당 1박 기준으로 일일 서비스 요금을 자동 부과한다. 이 요금은 사전 구매하지 않으면 선내 폴리오에 반영된다. 선납 봉사료 프로그램은 게스트가 승선 전에 이 총액을 미리 구매할 수 있도록 한다. 선납 금액은 승객의 선내 계정에 크레딧으로 적립되어 항해 기간 동안 자동 일일 요금이 충당된다. 크루즈 선사는 파도 시즌 프로모션에서 선납 봉사료를 부가 가치로 포함시키는 경우가 많다.

# Distinctions
- **Prepaid Gratuities** vs [Daily Service Charge](/cruise/cruise/daily-service-charge.md) — Daily Service Charge is the automatic per-person, per-night fee posted to the onboard account each day of the voyage to compensate crew; Prepaid Gratuities is the option to pay that same charge in full before the voyage begins. Prepaid Gratuities eliminate the daily folio debit but cover the same underlying compensation.
- **Prepaid Gratuities** vs [Onboard Credit (OBC)](/cruise/cruise/onboard-credit-obc.md) — Onboard Credit (OBC) is a monetary credit the passenger can spend on any onboard purchase (drinks, spa, excursions, shopping); Prepaid Gratuities is a targeted pre-payment covering only the service charge, not a general-purpose credit.
