> Two or more consecutive cruise voyages on the same ship, where passengers remain aboard (or reboard) through the turnaround port call, effectively sailing a longer combined itinerary made up of individual voyage segments. Lines often offer back-to-back passengers a per-voyage discount and simplified embarkation procedures at the intermediate turnaround.

A back-to-back cruise arises when a ship completes a voyage (e.g., a 7-night Western Caribbean) and then immediately begins a second consecutive voyage (e.g., a 7-night Eastern Caribbean) from the same homeport. Passengers who book both voyages are referred to as "back-to-back guests." At the turnaround, they are usually required to go ashore briefly for customs clearance, receive new key cards, and reboard before general boarding opens, giving them priority access to their cabin. Back-to-back bookings may be made as two separate reservations or, where a cruise line offers a combined sailing, as a single booking with a single voyage number. They are distinct from World Cruises, which are sold as one comprehensive voyage. Back-to-back cruising is common on ships with circular itineraries and is used by travellers seeking extended time at sea without repositioning.

**한국어 / Korean** — **연속 크루즈(Back-to-Back Cruise)** — 동일 선박에서 연속으로 이어지는 두 개 이상의 항해를 말하며, 승객은 중간 기항지(환승항)를 거칠 때 하선하지 않고(또는 재승선하여) 개별 항해 일정이 조합된 더 긴 여정을 항해한다.

연속 크루즈는 선박이 한 항해를 마치고 동일 기항지에서 즉시 두 번째 항해를 시작할 때 발생한다. 통관을 위한 잠시 하선 후 우선 재승선 혜택이 주어지며, 두 개의 별도 예약이거나 선사가 통합 항해로 제공하는 경우 단일 예약으로 처리될 수 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Back-to-Back Cruise** vs [Repositioning Cruise](/cruise/cruise/repositioning-cruise.md) — A Repositioning Cruise moves the ship to a different homeport between seasons; a Back-to-Back Cruise leaves from and returns to the same homeport on each successive voyage — the ship does not relocate.
- **Back-to-Back Cruise** vs [World Cruise](/cruise/cruise/world-cruise.md) — A World Cruise is marketed and priced as a single around-the-world voyage; a Back-to-Back Cruise is a passenger-initiated combination of two or more separately sold voyages that happen to be consecutive.
