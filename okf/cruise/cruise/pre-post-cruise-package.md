> Hotel accommodation, airport and pier transfers, and optional land touring sold to cruise passengers as an extension immediately before embarkation (pre-cruise) or after disembarkation (post-cruise) at the homeport or turnaround city, extending the vacation and easing same-day travel logistics.

Pre/post-cruise packages are sold both by cruise lines directly and by specialist tour operators. CLIA research found that close to two-thirds of cruise passengers book some form of pre- or post-cruise hotel arrangement. Major cruise lines offer branded programmes (e.g., Celebrity Cruises' 'Hotels by Celebrity', Norwegian Cruise Line's pre/post hotel programme), while third-party operators such as Avanti Destinations provide dedicated microsites for travel advisors. Packages are typically fully commissionable to travel advisors and bundle hotel at negotiated rates, private transfers between airport and cruise terminal, and optional sightseeing. They help guests avoid the risk of missing embarkation due to flight delays and allow deeper exploration of the embarkation or disembarkation city. Pre-cruise packages are particularly high-value for fly-cruise passengers and for itineraries that begin in less-familiar international ports.

**한국어 / Korean** — **크루즈 전후 패키지(Pre/Post-Cruise Package)** — 승선(pre-cruise) 직전 또는 하선(post-cruise) 직후에 출발 항구 또는 환승 도시에서 크루즈 승객에게 판매되는 호텔 숙박·공항-부두 이동·선택적 육상 투어로 구성된 연장 패키지. 여행의 가치를 높이고 당일 이동의 불편함을 줄여 준다.

Pre/post-cruise 패키지는 선사 직판(예: Celebrity Cruises의 "Hotels by Celebrity", Norwegian Cruise Line의 Pre/Post 호텔 프로그램)과 전문 투어 오퍼레이터(예: Avanti Destinations의 여행사 전용 마이크로사이트) 두 채널을 통해 판매된다. CLIA 조사에 따르면 크루즈 승객의 약 3분의 2가 어떤 형태로든 Pre/Post 호텔 상품을 예약한다. 패키지는 일반적으로 여행사에 전액 커미션이 지급되며, 협의된 요금의 호텔, 공항-크루즈 터미널 간 전용 이동, 선택적 관광을 묶어 제공한다. 항공 지연으로 인한 승선 불가 위험을 줄이고 출발·도착 도시를 더 깊이 탐험할 수 있게 한다.

# Distinctions
- **Pre/Post-Cruise Package** vs [CruiseTour](/cruise/cruise/cruisetour.md) — A CruiseTour is a seamlessly packaged combination of a cruise segment and a multi-day land touring itinerary, sold as a single product by the cruise line; a pre/post-cruise package is a separate hotel and transfer add-on at the embarkation or disembarkation city and does not involve extended land touring.
- **Pre/Post-Cruise Package** vs [Fly-Cruise](/cruise/cruise/fly-cruise.md) — Fly-Cruise is the combination of a flight and a cruise sold together; a pre/post-cruise package is the hotel and land extension at the port before or after the cruise and may be sold alongside a fly-cruise booking or independently.
