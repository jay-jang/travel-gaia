> The International Safety Management Code (ISM Code) is an IMO mandatory standard requiring all shipping companies and ships to develop, implement, and maintain a Safety Management System (SMS). Adopted by IMO Resolution A.741(18) and made compulsory under SOLAS Chapter IX, it applies to passenger ships on international voyages. A compliant company receives a Document of Compliance (DOC) and each ship is issued a Safety Management Certificate (SMC), both subject to periodic external audit by the flag state or its recognized organization (RO).

The ISM Code establishes an international standard for the safe management and operation of ships and for pollution prevention. It requires every shipping company to develop a Safety Management System (SMS) covering: a safety and environmental-protection policy; company and shipboard procedures; levels of authority and lines of communication; procedures for reporting accidents and non-conformities; emergency preparedness; and internal auditing and management review. Companies receive a Document of Compliance (DOC) valid for five years with annual verification; each ship is issued a Safety Management Certificate (SMC) valid for five years with an intermediate audit. PSC inspectors verify the SMC during port inspections. The Code was first adopted in 1993 and became mandatory for passenger ships from 1 July 1998.

**한국어 / Korean** — **국제안전관리규정(ISM Code)** — 국제안전관리규정(ISM Code)은 모든 선박회사와 선박이 안전관리시스템(SMS)을 개발·이행·유지할 것을 요구하는 IMO 의무 표준이다. IMO 결의 A.741(18)로 채택되어 SOLAS 제IX장에 따라 국제 항해에 종사하는 여객선에 의무화되었다. 적합 회사는 적합증서(DOC)를, 각 선박은 안전관리증서(SMC)를 발급받으며, 모두 기국 또는 인정기관(RO)의 정기 외부 심사를 받는다.

ISM Code는 선박의 안전한 운항과 오염 방지를 위한 국제 표준을 수립한다. 회사는 안전관리시스템(SMS)을 개발해야 하며, SMS에는 ① 안전·환경 보호 방침, ② 회사·선상 절차, ③ 권한 수준 및 통신 계통, ④ 사고·부적합 보고 절차, ⑤ 비상 상황 준비·대응 절차, ⑥ 내부 심사 및 경영 검토가 포함된다. 회사는 적합증서(DOC, 5년 유효)를, 각 선박은 안전관리증서(SMC, 5년 유효)를 발급받는다. 1993년 채택되어 1998년부터 여객선에 의무 적용되었다.

# Distinctions
- **ISM Code** vs [SOLAS](/cruise/cruise/solas.md) — SOLAS is the overarching international convention covering ship construction, fire safety, life-saving appliances, and security; the ISM Code is a specific code mandatory under SOLAS Chapter IX that focuses narrowly on safety management systems — the organizational and procedural framework companies and ships must have in place.
- **ISM Code** vs [ISPS Code](/cruise/cruise/isps-code.md) — The ISM Code governs safety management — preventing accidents and managing emergencies at sea; the ISPS Code governs security management — preventing unlawful acts (terrorism, piracy) against ships and port facilities. Both are mandatory under SOLAS but address different domains.
