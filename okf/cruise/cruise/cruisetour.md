> A single packaged vacation product sold by a cruise line that combines a cruise voyage with a pre- or post-cruise overland tour itinerary, offering guests a seamless land-and-sea experience under one booking. CruiseTours are most prominently marketed for Alaska (cruise + Denali / Canadian Rockies rail touring) and other regions where independent scenic areas complement the cruise route.

CruiseTours bundle the cruise component (sailing itinerary, stateroom, meals, onboard activities) with a structured land program that typically includes motorcoach or rail transportation, hotel accommodation, guided excursions, and meals ashore. The land portion may precede or follow the cruise. Pricing is expressed as a combined cruise-plus-land rate and the booking is managed as a single reservation file. CLIA classifies CruiseTours as a distinct product category. Major operators include Princess Cruises (Alaska and Europe), Holland America Line (Alaska land-sea journeys), and Silversea (expedition extensions). CruiseTours differ from independently booked land packages in that the cruise line assumes operational responsibility for the land program and guarantees seamless transfers.

**한국어 / Korean** — **크루즈투어(CruiseTour)** — 크루즈 항해와 출발 전 또는 도착 후 육상 투어 일정을 결합하여 단일 예약으로 제공하는 크루즈 라인 판매 패키지 휴가 상품.

크루즈투어는 크루즈 구성요소(항해 일정·선실·식사·선내 활동)에 코치·철도 교통, 호텔 숙박, 가이드 관광을 포함한 구조화된 육상 프로그램을 결합한다. CLIA는 크루즈투어를 별도 상품 범주로 분류하며, 크루즈 라인이 육상 프로그램의 운영 책임을 지고 원활한 이동을 보장한다.

# Distinctions
- **CruiseTour** vs [Land Package](/tours/tour-product/land-package.md) — A land package is a standalone ground-only arrangement (hotel, transfers, tours) that a guest may combine independently with a cruise; a CruiseTour is a single, cruise-line-managed product integrating both the cruise voyage and the land program under one reservation.
- **CruiseTour** vs [Repositioning Cruise](/cruise/cruise/repositioning-cruise.md) — A repositioning cruise moves the ship between regions at the end of a season and is typically a longer ocean passage with fewer port calls; a CruiseTour integrates an overland extension with a cruise and is sold as an experience product rather than a transit voyage.
