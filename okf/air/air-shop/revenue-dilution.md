> Revenue dilution is the loss of potential revenue that occurs when passengers purchase tickets at fare levels below what they would have been willing to pay, typically because availability controls are too loose, fare fences are insufficient, or lower fare classes are inadvertently made accessible to market segments intended for higher fares. It is the inverse problem to spill (turning away high-yield demand); dilution fills the aircraft but at sub-optimal revenue.

Revenue dilution arises from two main sources: (1) **Structural dilution** — when fare fences (advance purchase requirements, non-refundability, Saturday-night stays) are too weak to segment the market and business travellers buy leisure fares; (2) **Control dilution** — when booking limits or protection levels are set too low, allowing cheap fare classes to remain open when late-booking high-yield demand is expected to materialise.

Airlines quantify dilution by comparing actual revenue per seat to the theoretical maximum obtainable under perfect market segmentation; the gap is the dilution cost. Revenue management systems counteract control dilution by dynamically raising booking limits as departure approaches and high-fare demand signals emerge.

**한국어 / Korean** — **수익 희석(Revenue Dilution)** — 수익 희석(revenue dilution)은 여객이 지불 의향 수준보다 낮은 운임으로 항공권을 구매함으로써 발생하는 잠재 수익의 손실이다. 일반적으로 가용성 통제가 지나치게 느슨하거나, 운임 울타리가 불충분하거나, 낮은 운임 클래스가 의도치 않게 개방될 때 발생한다.

수익 희석은 구조적 희석(운임 울타리 부족)과 통제 희석(예약 한도·보호 수준 과소 설정)으로 구분된다. RM 시스템은 출발일에 근접하면서 고운임 수요 신호가 감지되면 예약 한도를 동적으로 높여 통제 희석에 대응한다.

# Distinctions
- **Revenue Dilution** vs [Spill and Spoilage](/air/air-shop/spill-and-spoilage.md) — Spill and spoilage measure the twin errors of turning away demand (spill) or flying with empty seats (spoilage); revenue dilution is a different error where the aircraft is full but filled with passengers paying lower fares than they would have been willing to pay — it is a pricing/segmentation failure, not a capacity-utilisation failure.
- **Revenue Dilution** vs [Revenue Management](/air/air-shop/revenue-management.md) — Revenue Management is the overarching discipline; revenue dilution is one of the key failure modes it aims to prevent by correctly calibrating fare availability, booking limits, and protective levels across the booking curve.
