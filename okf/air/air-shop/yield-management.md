> Yield management is the original aviation term for the practice of dynamically controlling seat inventory and pricing to maximize revenue from a perishable, fixed-capacity flight. Pioneered by American Airlines in the mid-1980s with its DINAMO system, it centers on selling the right seat to the right customer at the right price at the right time by opening and closing booking classes (RBDs) according to demand forecast models. The term is now largely superseded by the broader "Revenue Management" but remains in use for the fare/inventory-control dimension of airline and hotel pricing.

Yield management was developed to solve the airline's core commercial problem: a seat that departs empty is revenue lost forever, yet selling all seats cheaply sacrifices the high fares that late-booking business travelers will pay. The solution is a nesting structure of booking classes (RBDs), where lower-priced classes are closed as demand builds, protecting seats for higher-paying passengers. American Airlines' DINAMO (Dynamic Inventory and Maintenance Optimizer) system, deployed in 1985, is widely cited as the first large-scale computerized yield-management implementation; it provided a competitive response to the low-fare entry of People Express. The yield-management concept migrated rapidly from aviation into hotels (where it became "revenue management" combining rate and availability control) and then into car rental, rail, and hospitality broadly. In airline economics "yield" has a specific meaning — revenue per Revenue Passenger Kilometre (RPK), expressed in cents or pence per km — and yield management's objective is to maximize this figure.

**한국어 / Korean** — **수익률 관리(Yield Management)** — 수익률 관리(Yield Management)는 항공사가 고정 용량의 소멸성 좌석 재고와 요금을 동적으로 통제하여 수익을 극대화하는 원래의 항공 용어다. 1980년대 중반 American Airlines의 DINAMO 시스템으로 개척되었으며, 수요 예측 모델에 따라 예약 클래스(RBD)를 개방·폐쇄함으로써 적절한 좌석을 적절한 고객에게 적절한 가격에 적시에 판매하는 것을 핵심으로 한다.

수익률 관리는 항공사의 핵심 상업적 문제를 해결하기 위해 개발되었다. 빈 자리로 출발하는 좌석은 영구히 손실된 수익이지만, 모든 좌석을 저렴하게 판매하면 고운임을 포기하게 된다. 해결책은 예약 클래스(RBD)의 중첩 구조로, 수요가 증가함에 따라 저가 클래스를 폐쇄하여 고가 좌석을 보호한다. American Airlines의 DINAMO 시스템은 최초의 대규모 전산화 수익률 관리 구현으로 널리 인용된다.

# Distinctions
- **Yield Management** vs [Revenue Management](/air/air-shop/revenue-management.md) — Yield management is the original, narrower term focused on inventory-class nesting to protect high-yield seats on a flight leg; Revenue Management is the modern, broader discipline that encompasses O&D control, pricing strategy, group revenue, network effects, and ancillary revenue optimization beyond simple inventory nesting.
- **Yield Management** vs [Dynamic Pricing](/air/air-shop/dynamic-pricing.md) — Dynamic pricing refers to algorithmically adjusting the price itself in real time based on demand signals; yield management manages inventory availability (opening and closing booking classes) rather than the price, though the two mechanisms are now often combined in modern RMS platforms.
