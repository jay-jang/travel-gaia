> An airport check-in touchpoint dedicated exclusively to the physical acceptance and tagging of checked baggage for passengers who have already completed the check-in process off-site (web, mobile app, or kiosk). At a bag drop desk or automated bag drop (ABD) unit, the agent or machine scans the boarding pass, verifies travel documents, weighs and tags the bag, and routes it into the baggage system — without repeating the full check-in interview.

Bag drop decouples the administrative check-in (name verification, seat assignment, boarding pass issuance) from the physical baggage acceptance step, enabling airports and airlines to process high volumes of pre-checked-in passengers quickly. A staffed bag drop desk typically verifies identity and travel documents, weighs the bag, prints and attaches the bag tag, and introduces the bag into the baggage make-up system. Automated Bag Drop (ABD) units extend this to self-service: the passenger scans their boarding pass, the unit weighs the bag and prompts the passenger to attach a self-print or machine-printed tag, then opens a conveyor belt to accept it. Bag drop is a key component of the IATA Fast Travel programme concepts, designed to reduce airport congestion and staffing costs.

**한국어 / Korean** — **수하물 위탁 데스크(Bag Drop)** — 웹·모바일 앱·키오스크 등에서 사전 체크인을 완료한 승객이 위탁 수하물을 신속하게 맡기기 위한 공항 전용 창구. 직원 또는 ABD(자동화 수하물 위탁) 장비가 탑승권을 스캔하고 여행 서류를 확인한 뒤 수하물을 계량·태깅하여 수하물 처리 시스템으로 전달한다.

수하물 위탁 데스크는 행정적 체크인을 물리적 수하물 접수 단계와 분리해, 공항과 항공사가 사전 체크인을 완료한 대량의 승객을 신속하게 처리할 수 있도록 한다. ABD(자동화 수하물 위탁) 장비는 이 과정을 셀프서비스로 확장하여, 승객이 탑승권을 스캔하면 장비가 수하물 무게를 측정하고 태그 부착을 안내한 뒤 컨베이어 벨트가 열려 수하물을 접수한다. 많은 공항에서 기내 수하물만 가진 승객은 해당 구역을 완전히 건너뛰므로, bag drop은 기능적으로 전통 체크인 카운터보다는 전용 수하물 서비스 창구에 가깝다.

# Distinctions
- **Bag Drop** vs [Departure Control System (DCS)](/air/air-ops/departure-control-system-dcs.md) — The DCS is the back-end platform that manages check-in, boarding, load control, and messaging between the airline host and airport; a bag drop desk or ABD is the passenger-facing hardware and workflow touchpoint that interfaces with the DCS to accept the bag.
- **Bag Drop** vs [Boarding Pass](/air/air-ops/boarding-pass.md) — A boarding pass is the document (paper or mobile) confirming a passenger's check-in and gate entitlement; a bag drop desk is the physical location where that boarding pass is scanned so checked baggage can be accepted against it.
