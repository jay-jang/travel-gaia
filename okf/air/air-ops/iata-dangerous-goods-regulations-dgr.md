> The IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) is an annual publication that provides the authoritative technical and regulatory requirements for the safe transport of dangerous goods by air, translating the ICAO Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air (ICAO Doc 9284) into operationally usable guidance for airlines, ground handlers, shippers, freight forwarders, and airport authorities worldwide.

The DGR is updated annually and carries regulatory authority through ICAO Annex 18 (The Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air), which incorporates the ICAO Technical Instructions by reference. The publication classifies dangerous goods into nine UN hazard classes (explosives; gases; flammable liquids; flammable solids; oxidisers and organic peroxides; toxic and infectious substances; radioactive material; corrosives; miscellaneous), assigns UN numbers, specifies packaging instructions (PI), quantity limits, labelling/marking requirements, and the documentation that must accompany shipments (Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods). Airlines may issue an Operator Variations list to impose restrictions beyond the IATA minimums. Key role holders include the shipper (responsible for classification and packaging), the acceptance agent (inspects and verifies documentation), and the pilot-in-command (notified via the NOTOC — Notification to Captain). The DGR also governs dangerous goods carried by passengers (e.g., lithium batteries in carry-on versus hold limits, restrictions on aerosols). Non-compliance carries criminal liability in most jurisdictions and can result in revocation of IATA operational certificates.

**한국어 / Korean** — **위험물 항공 운송 규정(DGR)** — IATA 위험물 항공 운송 규정(DGR, Dangerous Goods Regulations)은 ICAO 기술 지침(ICAO Doc 9284)을 운영에 활용 가능한 형태로 번역·정리한 IATA의 연간 간행물로, 항공으로 위험물을 안전하게 운송하기 위한 권위 있는 기술·규제 요건을 제공한다. 항공사·지상 조업사·화주·화물 운송 주선업자·공항 당국이 전 세계적으로 준수한다.

DGR은 매년 개정되며, ICAO Annex 18(위험물 항공 운송의 안전)을 통해 규제 효력을 가진다. 위험물을 UN 위험성 분류 9개 클래스(폭발물·가스·인화성 액체·인화성 고체·산화제 및 유기 과산화물·독성 및 감염성 물질·방사성 물질·부식성 물질·기타)로 분류하고, UN 번호·포장 지시(PI)·수량 한도·표시·표찰 요건, 선하 서류(위험물 신고서)를 규정한다. 항공사는 IATA 최소 요건 이상의 제한을 Operator Variations 목록으로 부과할 수 있다. 주요 역할 주체는 화주(분류·포장 책임)·접수 대리인(서류 검사·확인)·기장(NOTOC — 기장 통보를 통해 고지)이다. 또한 승객이 기내 또는 위탁 수하물로 운반하는 위험물(리튬 배터리 제한·에어로졸 수량 등)도 규율한다. 위반 시 대부분의 관할권에서 형사 책임이 발생하며, IATA 운영 인증이 취소될 수 있다.

# Distinctions
- **IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR)** vs [NOTAM](/air/air-ops/notam.md) — A NOTAM is an operational notice issued to pilots and flight operations staff about temporary changes to airspace, navigation aids, or airport facilities; the DGR is a permanent, annually revised publication that establishes the rules for classifying, packaging, documenting, and accepting dangerous goods before they are loaded onto the aircraft. The DGR governs pre-flight acceptance; a NOTAM may restrict specific dangerous-goods shipments during a particular operational period.
- **IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR)** vs [General Declaration](/air/air-ops/general-declaration.md) — The General Declaration (GenDec) is the aircraft document listing cargo, passengers, and operational details filed with authorities at departure and arrival; the DGR is the multi-volume manual that defines how dangerous goods must be classified, packaged, and declared before they are included in that cargo manifest. Compliance with the DGR produces the Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods, which in turn feeds into the GenDec cargo information.
