> A co-terminal is a group of two or more airports serving the same metropolitan area that IATA designates as interchangeable for the purposes of air fare construction, ticketing, and routing. A passenger may depart from or arrive at any airport within the co-terminal group without incurring a stopover charge or triggering an open-jaw fare construction, provided all other routing rules are met.

IATA maintains the official list of co-terminal airport groups in its Ticketing Handbook and TACT Rules. Well-known examples include the New York group (JFK / LGA / EWR), London (LHR / LGW / LCY / STN / LTN / SEN), Chicago (ORD / MDW), Paris (CDG / ORY / BVA), and Tokyo (NRT / HND). The co-terminal rule has two key practical effects: (1) Fare construction — a round-trip where outbound is from JFK and return is into EWR is priced as a single round-trip, not as an open-jaw; the lowest applicable round-trip fare applies. (2) Connections — the minimum connecting time (MCT) for connections at co-terminal airports may differ from intra-airport MCTs and requires ground transfer time to be factored in.

**한국어 / Korean** — **공동터미널(Co-terminal)** — 공동터미널(co-terminal)은 동일한 대도시권을 서비스하는 두 개 이상의 공항으로, IATA가 운임 산출·발권·라우팅 목적상 상호 교환 가능한 것으로 지정한 그룹이다. 승객은 다른 라우팅 규칙을 준수하는 한, 해당 그룹 내 어느 공항에서든 출발하거나 도착할 수 있으며 경유 추가 운임이나 Open-jaw 운임 산출 없이 처리된다.

IATA는 공식적인 공동터미널 공항 그룹 목록을 Ticketing Handbook과 TACT 규정에 유지한다. 주요 사례는 뉴욕(JFK / LGA / EWR), 런던(LHR / LGW / LCY / STN / LTN / SEN), 시카고(ORD / MDW), 파리(CDG / ORY / BVA), 도쿄(NRT / HND)이다.

# Distinctions
- **Co-terminal** vs [Open Jaw](/air/air-ops/open-jaw.md) — An Open Jaw is a fare construction where the departure and arrival city differ (creating a V-shape routing) and carries an additional charge; a co-terminal round-trip uses different airports within the same city group and is treated as a regular round-trip with no open-jaw surcharge.
- **Co-terminal** vs [City Code](/common/codes/city-code.md) — A City Code (metropolitan area code) represents the broader city area and may encompass several airport codes; co-terminals are the specific airports that IATA formally links under one city code or as interchangeable points for fare-construction purposes.
