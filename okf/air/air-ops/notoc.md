> A written or printed notification provided by the aircraft operator to the pilot-in-command before departure that lists all dangerous goods loaded as cargo and specifies the emergency response information to be used in the event of an in-flight incident. The NOTOC is mandated by the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) and ICAO Technical Instructions, and must be delivered to the cockpit crew before engine start.

The NOTOC must state, for each dangerous goods shipment: the proper shipping name, UN number, hazard class or division, packing group (where assigned), number and type of packages, net quantity, and aircraft stowage location. For radioactive material it must additionally include the Transport Index. The pilot-in-command uses the NOTOC to brief crew and to communicate hazard information to emergency services in the event of fire, smoke, or an accident. Ground handling agents generate the NOTOC as part of the dangerous goods acceptance and load process; the original is handed to the cockpit crew and a copy is retained at the departure station. Many operators now accept an electronic NOTOC (e-NOTOC) in place of paper, subject to regulatory approval and operator procedures.

**한국어 / Korean** — **기장 통보(NOTOC)** — NOTOC(Notification to Captain, 기장 통보)는 출발 전 항공기 운항자가 기장에게 제공하는 서면 또는 인쇄 통보로, 화물로 적재된 모든 위험물 목록과 기내 사고 발생 시 사용할 비상 대응 정보를 명시한다. IATA 위험물 항공 운송 규정(DGR) 및 ICAO 기술 지침(Doc 9284)에서 의무화하며, 엔진 시동 전에 조종석 승무원에게 전달해야 한다.

NOTOC에는 각 위험물 화물별로 정식 운송 명칭, UN 번호, 위험성 등급 또는 구분, 포장 등급(해당 시), 포장 수 및 유형, 순량, 기내 적재 위치가 포함되어야 한다. 방사성 물질의 경우 운반 지수(Transport Index)도 추가로 표시해야 한다. 기장은 NOTOC를 이용해 승무원에게 사전 브리핑하고, 화재·연기·사고 발생 시 비상 서비스에 위험 정보를 전달한다. 지상 조업사가 위험물 접수·탑재 과정의 일환으로 NOTOC를 생성하며, 원본은 조종석 승무원에게 전달하고 사본은 출발 스테이션에 보관한다.

# Distinctions
- **NOTOC** vs [General Declaration](/air/air-ops/general-declaration.md) — The General Declaration (GenDec) is the aircraft's full departure document submitted to customs and immigration authorities, covering passengers, crew, cargo, stores, and health; the NOTOC is a cockpit-specific notification listing only the dangerous goods on board with their emergency response information for the pilot-in-command, and it is not submitted to authorities.
- **NOTOC** vs [IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR)](/air/air-ops/iata-dangerous-goods-regulations-dgr.md) — The DGR is the comprehensive annual rulebook that governs classification, packaging, marking, labelling, documentation, and acceptance of dangerous goods for air transport; the NOTOC is one specific document produced within that framework — it is the final flight-deck notification required when dangerous goods are loaded, not the regulatory publication itself.
