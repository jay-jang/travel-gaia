> An intermediate landing made by an aircraft for operational purposes — principally fuel replenishment, crew change, or maintenance — at which no commercial passengers are boarded or disembarked. It corresponds to the ICAO Second Freedom of the Air and is a key concept in long-haul flight planning and Air Service Agreements.

A technical stop is expressly recognized by the ICAO Second Freedom of the Air: a contracting state must permit airlines of other ICAO member states to make technical landings for non-traffic purposes (refueling, maintenance, crew rest). Unlike a stopover — where a passenger intentionally breaks their journey — or a diversion, where weather or emergency forces a landing at an unplanned airport, a technical stop is pre-planned, crew and aircraft oriented, and does not allow any commercial activity. Passengers remain onboard (or may temporarily disembark in a transit area) but cannot join or leave the aircraft at that point. Technical stops were historically common on ultra-long-haul routes (e.g., Pacific crossings through Anchorage or Honolulu before aircraft range improved) and remain relevant for freighters, charter flights, and some ultra-long-haul passenger routes. They must be declared in the aircraft's filed flight plan and are typically covered by the bilateral Air Service Agreement or IASTA between the countries involved.

**한국어 / Korean** — **기술적 기착(Technical Stop)** — 연료 보급, 승무원 교대, 정비 등 운항상의 필요로 항공기가 중간 지점에 착륙하되, 어떠한 상업 여객도 탑승하거나 하기하지 않는 기착. ICAO 항공의 자유 중 2자유에 해당하며, 장거리 노선 계획 및 항공서비스협정(ASA)의 핵심 개념이다.

기술적 기착은 ICAO 항공의 자유 중 2자유에 명시적으로 인정된 개념이다. 협약 체결국은 다른 ICAO 회원국 항공사가 비상업적 목적(연료 보급, 정비, 승무원 휴식)을 위해 기술적 착륙을 할 수 있도록 허용해야 한다. 여객이 여정을 의도적으로 중단하는 스톱오버나, 기상 또는 비상 상황으로 계획되지 않은 공항에 착륙하는 항공편 회항(diversion)과 달리, 기술적 기착은 사전 계획되고 승무원·기재 위주이며 상업 활동을 허용하지 않는다. 여객은 기내에 남아 있거나 일시적으로 트랜짓 구역에 하기할 수 있으나, 그 지점에서 항공기에 탑승하거나 하기할 수 없다.

**Aliases:** `Tech Stop`, `Non-traffic Stop`, `Fuel Stop`

# Distinctions
- **Technical Stop** vs [Stopover](/air/air-shop/stopover.md) — A stopover is a passenger-initiated, deliberate break in a journey exceeding the ATPCO/IATA time threshold (typically 24 hours internationally); a technical stop is an airline-initiated operational pause where no passengers board or deplane, regardless of duration.
- **Technical Stop** vs [Flight Diversion](/common/disruption/flight-diversion.md) — A technical stop is a pre-planned operational landing at an intermediate point with no passenger traffic; a diversion is an unplanned redirection of a flight to an alternate airport, typically due to weather, emergency, or airport closure.
