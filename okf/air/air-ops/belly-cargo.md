> Revenue freight transported in the lower-deck (belly) holds of passenger aircraft alongside passenger baggage. Airlines sell belly cargo capacity to freight forwarders, integrators, and shippers, typically on a per-kilogram basis by route, generating ancillary revenue from hold space that would otherwise carry only passenger bags.

Belly cargo occupies the lower-deck compartments of widebody and narrowbody passenger aircraft that are not filled by passenger baggage. Airlines publish belly cargo rates and capacity through IATA's TACT (The Air Cargo Tariff & Rules) and allocate space via the cargo management system alongside passenger load. Revenue from belly cargo is a significant contributor to passenger airline economics, particularly on wide-body routes. Belly cargo competes with all-cargo freighter capacity on price and speed; it is booked through cargo reservations systems and is tendered under Air Waybills governed by IATA Cargo regulations. Unit Load Devices (ULDs) are used on wide-body belly holds, while narrow-body aircraft use bulk loading.

**한국어 / Korean** — **복부 화물(Belly Cargo)** — 여객기의 하부 화물칸(belly hold)에 여객 수하물과 함께 탑재되어 운송되는 수익 화물. 항공사는 킬로그램 단위·노선별로 화물 포워더·통합업체·화주에게 belly 화물칸 여유 공간을 판매하여 부가 수익을 창출한다.

복부 화물은 여객 수하물로 채워지지 않은 광동체·협동체 여객기의 하부 격실을 활용한다. 항공사는 IATA TACT를 통해 belly 화물 요율과 용량을 공시하고, 화물 관리 시스템을 통해 여객 탑재와 병행해 공간을 배분한다. 복부 화물 수익은 여객 항공사 경제에서 중요한 부분을 차지하며, 특히 광동체 노선에서 두드러진다.

# Distinctions
- **Belly Cargo** vs [Charter Flight](/air/air-partner/charter-flight.md) — A charter flight is a dedicated aircraft hired for a specific cargo or passenger mission; belly cargo occupies the spare lower-deck hold space on scheduled passenger flights without displacing the primary passenger service.
- **Belly Cargo** vs [Unit Load Device](/air/air-ticket/unit-load-device.md) — A ULD is the physical container or pallet used to load cargo into the belly hold of widebody aircraft; belly cargo is the commercial concept of selling that hold space for freight revenue.
