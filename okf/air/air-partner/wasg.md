> The globally accepted framework for the neutral, transparent, and non-discriminatory allocation and management of runway and terminal capacity slots at coordinated (Level 3) and schedules-facilitated (Level 2) airports. The WASG is jointly published by IATA, ACI World, and WWACG under the Worldwide Airport Slot Board (WASB), and is the primary reference for slot coordinators worldwide.

The WASG (Edition 4, effective 1 August 2025) defines the three coordination levels (Level 1 = uncoordinated, Level 2 = schedules facilitated, Level 3 = fully coordinated) and the obligations they impose on airlines and coordinators. Core allocation rules include: historic ("grandfather") priority for existing slot holders; the 80/20 use-it-or-lose-it rule (an airline must operate at least 80% of a series to retain it the following equivalent season); new-entrant quotas (minimum 50% of any pool offered to new entrants); and the slot pool for redistribution. The WASG also governs neutrality requirements for coordinators, monitoring and enforcement mechanisms, and the force-majeure waiver framework applied during major disruptions. Jurisdiction-specific law (e.g., EU EC Regulation 95/93 as amended) implements the WASG principles with binding effect in each territory.

**한국어 / Korean** — **전 세계 공항 슬롯 가이드라인(WASG)** — WASG는 조정 대상(레벨 3) 및 스케줄 조율 대상(레벨 2) 공항에서 슬롯의 중립적·투명·비차별적 배분 및 관리를 위한 전 세계적으로 통용되는 체계이다. IATA·ACI World·WWACG가 세계 공항 슬롯 위원회(WASB) 거버넌스 하에 공동 발행하며, 전 세계 슬롯 조정관들의 주요 참조 문서이다.

WASG(4판, 2025년 8월 1일 발효)는 3단계 조정 수준과 항공사·조정관 의무, 과거 운항 실적 우선권, 80/20 사용 않으면 상실 규정, 신규 진입자 쿼터, 슬롯 풀을 포함한 핵심 배분 규칙, 조정관 중립성 요건, 모니터링·시행 메커니즘 및 불가항력 면제 체계를 정의한다.

# Distinctions
- **Worldwide Airport Slot Guidelines (WASG)** vs [Slot](/air/air-partner/slot.md) — A slot is the unit of allocation — the permission for one aircraft movement at a specific time; the WASG is the multi-volume guideline framework that governs how slots at coordinated airports are classified, allocated, monitored, and exchanged globally.
- **Worldwide Airport Slot Guidelines (WASG)** vs [Bilateral Air Service Agreement](/air/air-partner/bilateral-air-service-agreement.md) — A Bilateral Air Service Agreement grants governments the right for their airlines to operate routes; the WASG governs the operational slot-allocation process at airport level for all carriers, regardless of the bilateral that created their route rights.
