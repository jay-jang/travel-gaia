> One of the two annual periods — Northern Summer (last Sunday of March through last Saturday of October) and Northern Winter (last Sunday of October through last Saturday of March) — used by IATA and the global aviation industry as the standardized time frame for airline schedule planning, slot coordination, and interline agreement applicability.

The two IATA scheduling seasons provide a globally synchronized calendar for airline operations. The Northern Summer season (S-season) starts on the last Sunday of March and ends on the last Saturday of October; the Northern Winter season (W-season) starts on the last Sunday of October and ends on the last Saturday of March. The bi-annual IATA Schedules Conferences set the season boundaries and coordinate schedule submissions: airlines request and confirm slots for the upcoming season, typically attending the Northern Summer conference in June (for the following summer) and the Northern Winter conference in November (for the following winter). The season framework is also referenced in the IATA Standard Schedules Information Manual (SSIM) as the time unit for schedule messages (SSMs, ASMs, SCR) and in the WASG as the unit for historic precedence (slots from one summer season provide precedence for the next summer season, not the intervening winter). Flight schedules, fare rules, and interline agreements routinely cite IATA season identifiers (e.g., S25, W25) for validity periods.

**한국어 / Korean** — **IATA 스케줄링 시즌** — IATA와 글로벌 항공 산업이 항공사 스케줄 계획, 슬롯 조정, 인터라인 협정 적용 기간의 표준화된 시간 단위로 사용하는 두 연간 기간 중 하나. 북반구 하계(3월 마지막 일요일~10월 마지막 토요일)와 북반구 동계(10월 마지막 일요일~3월 마지막 토요일)로 구성된다.

IATA 스케줄링 시즌 체계는 항공사 운항을 위해 전 세계적으로 동기화된 캘린더를 제공한다. IATA는 연 2회 스케줄 컨퍼런스를 열어 시즌 경계를 확정하고 스케줄 제출을 조율한다. 항공사는 통상 6월에 다음 하계 시즌, 11월에 다음 동계 시즌에 대한 슬롯 요청 및 확정에 참여한다. 항공편 스케줄, 운임 규정, 인터라인 협정은 유효 기간을 표시할 때 IATA 시즌 식별자(예: S25, W25)를 통상적으로 사용한다.

# Distinctions
- **IATA Scheduling Season** vs [Standard Schedules Information Manual (SSIM)](/air/air-ops/standard-schedules-information-manual-ssim.md) — The IATA Scheduling Season is the biannual time period — the calendar unit of slot coordination and schedule validity; the SSIM is the technical manual that defines the message formats and data standards (SSM, ASM, SCR) used to communicate schedules and slot requests during and between seasons.
- **IATA Scheduling Season** vs [Slot](/air/air-partner/slot.md) — A slot is the individual allocated permission for an aircraft movement; the IATA scheduling season is the time-window unit to which that slot is assigned and within which it must be utilized to qualify for historic precedence.
