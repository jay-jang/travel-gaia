> An Interline Baggage Agreement is a bilateral or multilateral arrangement between two or more airlines under which each carrier agrees to accept and through-check baggage for passengers traveling on itineraries that involve more than one airline, forwarding it to the final destination without the passenger reclaiming and re-checking bags at intermediate points. IATA Resolution 302 sets the global standard conditions governing interline baggage proration, liability, and claims; lost or delayed bags on multi-carrier itineraries are traced through the WorldTracer system.

Interline baggage through-checking lets a passenger check bags once at origin and have them transferred to connecting flights on other airlines. IATA Resolution 302 is the foundational standard, setting the proration formula for dividing baggage costs among carriers and liability limits consistent with the Montreal Convention 1999; the last carrier in the itinerary bears primary passenger-facing liability. IATA's standard 10-digit baggage identification tag enables tracking across carriers via WorldTracer. Not all interline agreements include baggage through-check — the parties must specifically agree; many LCCs opt out, requiring passengers to reclaim and re-check bags at each transfer.

**한국어 / Korean** — **인터라인 수하물 협정** — 인터라인 수하물 협정은 둘 이상의 항공사 간의 양자 또는 다자간 협정으로, 각 항공사가 여러 항공사가 관여하는 여정의 승객 수하물을 인수·연계 위탁하여 최종 목적지까지 전달하도록 합의한다. IATA 결의 302가 인터라인 수하물 프로레이션, 책임, 클레임에 관한 글로벌 표준 조건을 정하며, 분실·지연 수하물은 WorldTracer 시스템으로 추적된다.

인터라인 수하물 연계 위탁은 승객이 출발 공항에서 한 번만 위탁하면 다른 항공사의 연결편으로 이송되도록 한다. IATA 결의 302는 항공사 간 수하물 비용 분배와 몬트리올 협약 1999와 일치하는 책임 한도를 정하는 기초 표준이며, 여정의 마지막 항공사가 승객에 대한 1차 책임을 진다. 모든 인터라인 협정이 수하물 연계 위탁을 포함하는 것은 아니며, LCC는 종종 미참여한다.

# Distinctions
- **Interline Baggage Agreement** vs [Interline](/air/air-partner/interline.md) — Interline is the broader commercial agreement for accepting passengers, tickets, and traffic documents across multi-carrier itineraries; an Interline Baggage Agreement is the specific component that must be separately agreed and that governs the acceptance, transfer, liability, and proration of checked baggage. An airline can have an interline ticket agreement without having a baggage through-check agreement.
- **Interline Baggage Agreement** vs [Inter-Airline Through Check-In (IATCI)](/air/air-partner/inter-airline-through-check-in-iatci.md) — IATCI is the operational IT standard enabling electronic check-in across all legs of a multi-carrier itinerary and boarding pass printing at the first point; an Interline Baggage Agreement is the commercial contract enabling the passenger's bags to also be through-checked. Both are needed for a seamless connection: IATCI handles boarding passes; the baggage agreement handles the bags.
