> A set of nine traffic rights, codified by ICAO on the basis of the 1944 Chicago Convention, that define what commercial aviation activities one country may permit an airline of another country to conduct. The first five freedoms are universally recognized and form the backbone of bilateral air service agreements and open-skies treaties; the additional freedoms (6–9) address more liberal cabotage and beyond-rights arrangements.

The nine freedoms are cumulative traffic rights: 1st — overflight without landing; 2nd — technical stop (fuel/maintenance) without carrying commercial traffic; 3rd — carry outbound traffic from home country to a treaty partner; 4th — carry inbound traffic from a treaty partner to the home country; 5th — carry traffic between two foreign countries as part of a service originating or ending in the home country; 6th — carry traffic between two foreign countries via the airline's home country (the so-called '6th freedom' hub model used by many Middle East and Asian carriers); 7th — operate flights entirely between two foreign countries without touching the home country; 8th — consecutive cabotage (operate between two points within a foreign country as an extension of an international service from the home country); 9th — stand-alone cabotage (operate wholly within a foreign country). The 1st and 2nd freedoms are guaranteed by the International Air Services Transit Agreement (IASTA, 1944); the 3rd and 4th by bilateral Air Service Agreements (ASA); the 5th through 9th only by specific treaty negotiation. Open Skies agreements typically grant the first seven freedoms liberally between the signing states.

**한국어 / Korean** — **항공의 자유(Freedoms of the Air)** — 1944년 시카고 협약에 기반하여 ICAO가 성문화한 9가지 교통권으로, 한 국가가 타국의 항공사에게 허용할 수 있는 상업 항공 활동의 범위를 정의한다. 1~5의 자유는 보편적으로 인정되며 양자항공협정(ASA) 및 오픈스카이 협약의 근간을 이룬다. 6~9의 자유는 보다 자유화된 연속·이원·국내 운항권 등을 다룬다.

9개 자유는 누적적 교통권이다: 1자유 — 착륙 없는 영공 통과; 2자유 — 상업 여객 운송 없이 기술적 착륙(연료 보급·정비); 3자유 — 자국 출발 여객을 협정 상대국으로 운송; 4자유 — 협정 상대국에서 자국으로 여객 운송; 5자유 — 자국 출발·도착 편의 일부로 두 외국 간 여객 운송; 6자유 — 자국을 경유하여 두 외국 간 여객 운송(중동·아시아 다수 항공사가 활용하는 허브 모델); 7자유 — 자국을 경유하지 않고 두 외국 간에서만 운항; 8자유 — 연속 국내운항권(자국 출발 국제편과 연결하여 외국 내 두 지점 간 운항); 9자유 — 독립 국내운항권(외국 영내에서만 순수 운항). 1·2자유는 1944년 국제항공운송통과협정(IASTA)으로, 3·4자유는 양자항공협정(ASA)으로, 5~9자유는 별도 조약 협상을 통해서만 부여된다. 오픈스카이 협정은 서명국 간에 보통 1~7자유를 폭넓게 허용한다.

# Distinctions
- **Freedoms of the Air** vs [Open Skies Agreement](/air/air-partner/open-skies-agreement.md) — The Freedoms of the Air are the ICAO-defined framework of traffic rights; an Open Skies Agreement is a specific bilateral or multilateral treaty that grants most of those freedoms (typically 1–7) liberally between two or more countries.
- **Freedoms of the Air** vs [Codeshare](/air/air-partner/codeshare.md) — A freedom of the air is a sovereign traffic right a government grants to a foreign airline; a codeshare is a commercial arrangement between two airlines to market each other's flights and does not by itself confer traffic rights — the underlying services must already be authorized by the relevant freedom.
