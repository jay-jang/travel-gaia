> A Bilateral Air Service Agreement (BASA) — also called a Bilateral Air Transport Agreement — is a treaty between two sovereign states that grants each country's designated airlines the right to operate commercial air services between them. BASAs define the routes, frequencies, capacity entitlements, pricing freedoms, and ownership requirements within which airlines may operate, forming the legal basis for the international route network.

Historically, most BASAs follow the restrictive Bermuda I model (1946), which predates liberalisation and tightly caps capacity, designates specific routes and airlines, and requires airline ownership by nationals of the designated country. The modern counterpart is the Open Skies Agreement, a BASA type that removes these restrictions. ICAO Doc 9626 (Manual on the Regulation of International Air Transport) provides model clauses and guidance for states negotiating BASAs. A BASA typically specifies: (1) Designated airlines — which carriers may operate the routes; (2) Route schedules (5th Freedom, route tables); (3) Capacity and frequency controls (or an open-sky clause removing them); (4) Tariff arrangements (double-approval, dual-disapproval, or free pricing); (5) Exchange of commercial rights and ownership/control criteria. Codeshare, interline, and slot access provisions may be incorporated. Where no BASA exists, airlines cannot operate scheduled international services between those countries without special governmental authorisation.

**한국어 / Korean** — **이중 항공업무협정(BASA)** — BASA(Bilateral Air Service Agreement, 이중 항공업무협정)는 두 국가 간 조약으로, 각국의 지정 항공사에 그 사이를 운항하는 상업 항공 서비스 권리를 부여한다. BASA는 항공사가 운항할 수 있는 노선, 운항 빈도, 수용 인원, 가격 자유도, 소유권 요건을 정의하며, 국제 노선망의 법적 근거를 형성한다.

역사적으로 대부분의 BASA는 자유화 이전의 제한적인 버뮤다 I 모델(1946)을 따르며, 수용 인원을 엄격히 제한하고, 특정 노선과 항공사를 지정하며, 지정 국가의 국민에 의한 항공사 소유권을 요구한다. 현대적 대응 개념은 이러한 제한을 철폐하는 BASA 유형인 Open Skies Agreement이다. ICAO Doc 9626(국제항공운송 규제 매뉴얼)은 BASA 협상 국가를 위한 모델 조항과 지침을 제공한다. BASA는 일반적으로 (1) 지정 항공사; (2) 노선 일정표(5th Freedom, 노선 테이블); (3) 수용 인원 및 빈도 통제(또는 오픈스카이 조항); (4) 운임 협약; (5) 상업권 교환 및 소유권 기준을 명시한다. 코드셰어, 인터라인, 슬롯 접근 조항도 포함될 수 있다. BASA가 없는 경우 항공사는 특별 정부 승인 없이는 정기 국제선을 운항할 수 없다.

# Distinctions
- **Bilateral Air Service Agreement** vs [Open Skies Agreement](/air/air-partner/open-skies-agreement.md) — An Open Skies Agreement is a liberalised type of bilateral air service agreement that removes restrictions on routes, capacity, frequencies, and pricing; a BASA is the general legal instrument — an Open Skies Agreement is a BASA, but not all BASAs are Open Skies.
- **Bilateral Air Service Agreement** vs [Freedoms of the Air](/air/air-partner/freedoms-of-the-air.md) — The Freedoms of the Air are the conceptual building blocks defining what commercial rights aircraft may exercise internationally (overfly, land, carry traffic); a BASA is the bilateral treaty that grants or denies those specific freedoms between two countries.
- **Bilateral Air Service Agreement** vs [Antitrust Immunity](/air/air-partner/antitrust-immunity.md) — Antitrust Immunity (ATI) is a government grant allowing competing airlines to coordinate pricing and capacity on specific routes without violating competition law; a BASA is the traffic-rights treaty between states — ATI is a separate domestic competition-law exemption that airlines apply for, often linked to a joint venture, not the BASA itself.
