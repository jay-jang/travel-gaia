> The right of an airline to retain a series of airport slots in the next equivalent IATA scheduling season (summer-to-summer or winter-to-winter) because it held and operated those slots in the previous equivalent season at or above the WASG 80% utilization threshold. Commonly called "grandfather rights", it is the primary mechanism by which incumbents preserve their schedule positions at capacity-constrained airports.

Historic slot precedence is the cornerstone of the IATA WASG slot allocation framework and is codified in applicable legislation such as EU Council Regulation (EEC) No 95/93 (as amended). An airline may claim historic precedence for a series of slots if: (1) the slots were allocated in the previous equivalent season (summer-to-summer or winter-to-winter — not consecutive seasons); (2) the slots were operated as cleared by the coordinator for at least 80% of the time during the period (the 80:20 use-it-or-lose-it rule); and (3) the slots formed a valid series of at least five flights at the same time on the same day of the week. Slots not meeting the 80% threshold — absent a force-majeure waiver — fall into the slot pool and are no longer eligible for historic precedence. At Level 3 (fully coordinated) airports, historic precedence effectively limits the supply of slots available to new entrants and is the primary reason that slots at congested airports (e.g., Heathrow, Frankfurt, Tokyo Haneda) have substantial commercial value. The WASG mandates that at least 50% of any slot pool must be offered to new entrants before incumbents may claim the remainder under non-precedence rules.

**한국어 / Korean** — **슬롯 과거 운항 실적 우선권(Historic Slot Precedence)** — 항공사가 이전 동등 IATA 스케줄링 시즌(하계→하계 또는 동계→동계)에 해당 슬롯을 보유하고 WASG 80% 활용 기준 이상으로 운항했기 때문에 다음 동등 시즌에도 동일한 공항 슬롯 시리즈를 유지할 권리. 흔히 "조부 권리(grandfather rights)"라고 불리며, 혼잡 공항에서 기존 항공사가 스케줄 포지션을 보전하는 1차적 메커니즘이다.

슬롯 과거 운항 실적 우선권은 IATA WASG 슬롯 배분 체계의 핵심이며, EU 이사회 규정(EEC) No 95/93(개정판) 등 관련 법령에 명문화되어 있다. 항공사가 과거 운항 실적 우선권을 주장하려면 다음 조건을 충족해야 한다: (1) 이전 동등 시즌(연속 시즌이 아닌 하계→하계 또는 동계→동계)에 슬롯이 배분되었을 것; (2) 해당 기간 동안 조정관이 승인한 대로 최소 80%를 운항했을 것(80:20 사용하지 않으면 상실 규정); (3) 슬롯이 동일 요일·시간대에 최소 5회 비행으로 구성된 유효한 시리즈였을 것.

# Distinctions
- **Historic Slot Precedence** vs [Series of Slots](/air/air-partner/series-of-slots.md) — A series of slots is the precondition — the required grouping of recurring allocations — that makes an airline eligible for historic precedence; historic slot precedence is the resulting right to retain that series in the next equivalent season.
- **Historic Slot Precedence** vs [Slot](/air/air-partner/slot.md) — A slot is a single permitted aircraft movement at a coordinated airport; historic slot precedence is the multi-season entitlement to retain a recurring series of those individual slots.
