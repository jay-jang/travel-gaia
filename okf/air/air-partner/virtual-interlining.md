> Virtual interlining is a booking arrangement in which a passenger combines two or more separately purchased tickets on carriers without a mutual interline agreement to form a connecting journey. A third-party platform assembles the combination and typically presents a blended price, but each ticket remains legally independent — the carriers bear no rebooking obligation if the traveler misses the onward leg after completing a self-transfer.

Unlike a formal interline arrangement — in which partner carriers book passengers across each other's flights under joint responsibility and accept re-accommodation duties on disruption — virtual interlining stitches together independently priced tickets, leaving the traveler to bear all connection risk. Platforms typically mitigate this by inserting generous self-transfer layover windows or offering missed-connection insurance as an add-on. Because no single carrier controls the end-to-end journey, passengers generally lack the EU 261/2004 re-routing entitlements that apply to through-ticketed itineraries. The model was pioneered at scale by Kiwi.com and allows travelers to access price combinations that no carrier interline product reaches.

**한국어 / Korean** — **가상 연결(Virtual Interlining)** — 가상 연결(Virtual Interlining)은 인터라인 협정 없는 항공사들의 별도 티켓을 조합하여 환승 여정을 구성하는 예약 방식이다. 제3자 플랫폼이 혼합 가격으로 조합을 제시하지만 각 티켓은 법적으로 독립되어 있어, 자기 이동(self-transfer) 후 환승편을 놓쳐도 항공사는 후속 구간에 대한 재예약 의무를 지지 않는다.

정식 인터라인 협정에서는 제휴 항공사들이 공동 책임 하에 서로의 항공편을 예약해 주고 운항 차질 시 재배정 의무를 부담하지만, 가상 연결은 독립 가격의 티켓들을 연결하므로 환승 위험을 여행자가 전부 부담한다. 플랫폼은 넉넉한 self-transfer 경유 시간 확보나 환승 지연 보험 옵션으로 이를 완화한다. 단일 항공사가 여정 전체를 통제하지 않으므로, 여행자는 일반적으로 직통 발권 여정에 적용되는 EU 261/2004 재배정 권리를 누리지 못한다. Kiwi.com이 대규모로 선도한 이 모델은 어떤 항공사 인터라인 상품도 제공하지 않는 가격 조합을 여행자에게 제공한다.

# Distinctions
- **Virtual Interlining** vs [Interline](/air/air-partner/interline.md) — Under an interline agreement carriers formally accept each other's tickets and take on re-accommodation obligations when a connecting leg is disrupted; virtual interlining uses independent tickets with no such inter-carrier obligation.
- **Virtual Interlining** vs [Codeshare](/air/air-partner/codeshare.md) — A codeshare places one carrier's code on another's flight under joint responsibility and single booking document; virtual interlining is two or more entirely separate tickets with no cross-carrier responsibility or shared flight code.
- **Virtual Interlining** vs [Misconnect](/common/disruption/misconnect.md) — A misconnect is the disruption event of missing a connecting flight; virtual interlining is the booking model that elevates misconnect risk by eliminating the onward carrier's rebooking duty.
