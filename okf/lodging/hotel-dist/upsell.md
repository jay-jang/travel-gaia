> Upsell (or upselling) is the practice of inviting a customer who has already committed to a purchase to upgrade to a higher-value product within the same category — a superior room type in a hotel, a higher cabin class on an airline, or a larger vehicle segment at car rental. It is distinct from cross-sell (offering a different product category) and is a primary ancillary revenue strategy across hospitality and travel verticals.

In hotel distribution, upselling is typically executed through three channels: (1) pre-arrival automated email or app offers sent 24–72 hours before check-in, inviting guests to upgrade for a fixed amount or using a bid model; (2) front-desk upselling at check-in, where trained agents offer room upgrades verbally; and (3) real-time dynamic upsell offers surfaced via CRS/PMS integrations with upsell platforms. Effective upsell programs are driven by availability data from the PMS, pricing intelligence (ensuring the upsell price is a meaningful value above the original but below the rack rate of the upgraded room), and guest profile data (loyalty status, past preferences) from the CRM. Industry research from HSMAI (Hospitality Sales and Marketing Association International) identifies pre-arrival upsell conversion rates typically in the 3–10 % range. Revenue management teams set upsell pricing floors and ceilings to protect yield — ensuring that accepting an upsell does not displace a higher-revenue transient guest. In airlines, structured upsell programs take the form of Bid Upgrade systems (e.g., Plusgrade, UpgradedPoints) where passengers bid for unsold premium seats, with the airline accepting bids algorithmically to maximize total revenue per flight. In car rental, upselling to a higher SIPP/ACRISS category at the rental counter is a standard front-line revenue practice. The term "upsell" is universal across travel verticals; "cross-sell" is the complementary practice of selling a product from a different category (e.g., offering insurance or a transfer to a hotel guest).

**한국어 / Korean** — **업셀(Upsell)** — 이미 구매를 결정한 고객에게 동일 카테고리 내 더 높은 가치의 상품으로 업그레이드를 제안하는 관행이다. 호텔의 상위 객실 유형, 항공사의 상위 클래스, 렌터카의 대형 차종 제안 등이 해당한다. 다른 상품 카테고리를 제안하는 크로스셀과 구분된다.

호텔 유통에서 업셀은 (1) 사전 도착 자동화 이메일/앱 오퍼, (2) 체크인 카운터 직원의 구두 제안, (3) CRS/PMS와 업셀 플랫폼 통합을 통한 실시간 오퍼 등 세 가지 채널로 실행된다. HSMAI 연구에 따르면 사전 도착 업셀 전환율은 일반적으로 3~10% 수준이다. 항공사에서는 입찰 업그레이드 시스템, 렌터카에서는 카운터 차종 업그레이드가 대표적인 업셀 사례이다.

# Distinctions
- **Upsell** vs [Bid Upgrade](/cruise/cruise/bid-upgrade.md) — A Bid Upgrade is a specific formalized upsell mechanism — typically used in airlines — where a passenger submits a monetary bid for an unsold premium seat and the airline accepts or rejects the bid algorithmically before departure; Upsell is the broader commercial practice of offering any higher-value product in the same category, spanning instant-purchase room upgrades, checkout add-ons, and car rental counter offers as well as bid-based airline programs.
