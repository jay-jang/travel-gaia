> An industry membership organization that aggregates independent or franchised travel agencies into a buying group, enabling members to negotiate preferred supplier agreements — particularly hotel preferred programs — that individual agencies could not secure alone. Consortia like Virtuoso, Signature Travel Network, Ensemble, and Internova provide members with negotiated hotel commissions, client amenity packages, and co-operative marketing under a recognized brand that hotels and suppliers use for rate and service differentiation.

A travel consortium acts as an intermediary between travel advisors and suppliers, aggregating booking volume to unlock preferential commission rates, exclusive amenity packages, and marketing support. In the hotel segment, a consortium hotel program identifies a curated set of 'preferred' properties and negotiates a standard set of benefits for guests (e.g., complimentary breakfast, property credit, room upgrade when available, early check-in/late checkout). The benefits are layered on top of the best available rate and are invisible in GDS; advisors access them by booking through a consortium-specific booking channel or by citing the consortium code when contacting the hotel directly.

Virtuoso, Signature Travel Network, Ensemble, Internova 등 컨소시엄은 회원에게 협상된 호텔 커미션, 고객 어메니티 패키지, 협력 마케팅을 제공하며, 호텔과 공급자가 요율·서비스 차별화에 활용하는 인정받은 브랜드를 보유한다.

# Distinctions
- **Travel Consortium** vs [Travel Management Company](/common/customer/travel-management-company.md) — A Travel Management Company (TMC) manages corporate travel programs and books on behalf of business travelers, often focusing on policy compliance and cost control; a travel consortium is a network that independent leisure or mixed agencies join to access preferred hotel rates and training, with a stronger advisor-to-consumer orientation.
- **Travel Consortium** vs [Hotel Loyalty Program](/lodging/hotel-dist/hotel-loyalty-program.md) — A hotel loyalty program rewards individual guests for repeat stays with points, status, and perks, fostering a direct relationship between the hotel brand and the consumer; a travel consortium creates a preferred relationship between the hotel and the travel advisor community, enabling advisors to book added-value packages for their clients regardless of the client's loyalty status.
- **Travel Consortium** vs [Rate Parity](/lodging/hotel-rate/rate-parity.md) — Rate parity requires that a hotel offer the same rate across all distribution channels; a consortium preferred booking adds non-rate benefits (breakfast, credits, upgrades) on top of the rate-parity-compliant BAR, creating a differentiated value proposition without violating parity clauses.
