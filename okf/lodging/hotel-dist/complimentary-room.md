> A hotel room provided at no charge as a commercial courtesy, operational allocation, or loyalty recognition. Hotels grant comp rooms to tour operators and meeting planners in exchange for group business (typically at a ratio of one comp per set number of paid rooms contracted), to media and travel writers for editorial coverage, to hotel partners, and to VIP guests. Complimentary rooms are recorded in the night audit at zero net revenue and are tracked against group contracts or negotiated tour-operator agreements.

Complimentary rooms (comps) are a structured element of hotel commercial practice. For tour operators, the standard industry practice (NTA/USTOA norms) is one comp per 15–20 paid rooms contracted; for meetings and incentives, ratios vary by property and market. Comps are stipulated in the Group Contract and counted as part of the total room block for purposes of attrition calculations. From a revenue management perspective, comp rooms carry zero ADR and reduce the property's effective RevPAR; they must therefore be tracked separately in the PMS and excluded from rate benchmarking reports such as the STR STAR Report. For FAM trips, comp rooms are provided to travel trade partners in exchange for familiarization and product endorsement rather than commercial payment. House-use rooms (occupied by hotel staff or set aside for operational purposes) are a related but distinct category.

**한국어 / Korean** — **무료 제공 객실(Complimentary Room)** — 상업적 예우·운영 배정·로열티 인정을 이유로 무료로 제공되는 호텔 객실. 호텔은 통상 투어 운영사·행사 기획자에게 단체 영업에 대한 보상으로(계약 유료 객실 일정 수당 1실 비율로), 미디어·여행 작가에게 편집 취재 목적으로, 호텔 파트너에게, 또는 VIP 게스트에게 comp 객실을 제공한다. 무료 제공 객실은 야간 감사(night audit) 기록에 순수익 0으로 등록되며, 단체 계약 또는 협의된 투어 운영사 계약에 따라 추적·관리된다.

무료 제공 객실(comp)은 호텔 상거래 관행의 구조화된 요소다. 투어 운영사의 경우 표준 업계 관행(NTA/USTOA 기준)은 계약 유료 객실 15–20실당 1실 comp이다. Comp 객실은 단체 계약서에 명시되어 attrition 계산에 포함된다. 수익 관리 관점에서 comp 객실은 ADR이 0이어서 PMS에서 별도 추적이 필요하다.

# Distinctions
- **Complimentary Room** vs Group Block — A group block is the contracted set of rooms reserved for a group at a negotiated rate; a complimentary room is the additional zero-charge room(s) awarded by the hotel as part of the group contract terms.
- **Complimentary Room** vs Night Audit — The night audit is the daily reconciliation process that closes the hotel's financial day; complimentary rooms appear in that process as occupied rooms at zero revenue, requiring specific tracking codes in the PMS to distinguish them from paid stays.
- **Complimentary Room** vs FAM Trip — A FAM trip is an organized familiarization visit by travel trade partners to a destination or property; complimentary rooms are the accommodation element provided during a FAM trip, but not all comp rooms are for FAM purposes.
