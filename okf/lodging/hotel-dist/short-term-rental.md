> A short-term rental (STR) is a furnished residential property — apartment, house, cottage, or room — rented to guests for a duration typically under 30 consecutive nights, primarily through online platforms such as Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com Homes. Unlike traditional hotels, STRs are operated by individual hosts or professional property managers and are subject to a rapidly evolving patchwork of local zoning, registration, and occupancy-tax regulations.

Short-term rentals occupy a distinct tier in the accommodation distribution landscape. Supply-side participants range from casual hosts renting a spare room to professional operators managing hundreds of units through dedicated Property Management Systems (PMS) purpose-built for STR workflows (e.g., Guesty, Hostaway, Lodgify). Distribution flows through OTA platforms (Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Expedia), direct-booking websites, and channel managers that synchronise availability and rates across multiple platforms. Channel managers and ARI integrations are essential to avoid double-bookings across listings. Rate Parity obligations vary: Airbnb eliminated its rate parity clause in 2014, while Booking.com enforces rate parity in most markets, creating a complex rate management environment for hosts. The STR market has grown rapidly since 2010 and now materially competes with the traditional hotel sector, particularly for leisure and group travel. Regulatory pressure has intensified globally: cities such as New York (Local Law 18), Amsterdam, Paris, Barcelona, and Tokyo impose registration requirements, night caps, and zoning restrictions.

**한국어 / Korean** — **단기 임대(Short-Term Rental, STR)** — 단기 임대(Short-Term Rental, STR)는 Airbnb·Vrbo·Booking.com Homes 등 온라인 플랫폼을 통해 주로 30박 미만으로 여행객에게 임대되는 가구가 완비된 주거용 숙소다. 전통 호텔과 달리 개인 호스트 또는 전문 자산 관리사가 운영하며, 급변하는 지역 용도 지역·등록·숙박세 규정의 적용을 받는다.

단기 임대는 숙박 유통 환경에서 독자적인 계층을 차지한다. 공급 측 참여자는 여분의 방을 임대하는 일반 호스트부터 STR 전용 PMS로 수백 개 객실을 관리하는 전문 운영사까지 다양하다. 유통은 OTA 플랫폼·직접 예약 웹사이트·채널 매니저를 통해 이루어진다. 요금 동일성 정책은 플랫폼마다 다르며, 규제 압력이 전 세계적으로 강화되고 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Short-Term Rental** vs [Rate Parity](/lodging/hotel-dist/rate-parity.md) — Rate Parity is the contractual obligation for accommodation providers to offer the same rate across all distribution channels; STR platforms have divergent parity policies — Airbnb eliminated its rate parity clause in 2014, while Booking.com still enforces parity. STR hosts therefore face a fragmented parity environment requiring platform-specific pricing strategy, unlike most hotel operators who face a more uniform rate-parity regime.
- **Short-Term Rental** vs [Channel Manager](/lodging/hotel-dist/channel-manager.md) — A Channel Manager synchronises room/unit availability (ARI) and rates across multiple distribution channels to prevent double-bookings; in the STR ecosystem this is especially critical because inventory is non-fungible (each listing is unique) and most hosts operate across two or more OTA platforms simultaneously. STR-specific channel managers also manage guest communication, smart-lock codes, and cleaning schedules alongside ARI, reflecting operational differences from hotel channel management.
