> In hotel group contracting, attrition is the contractual obligation for a group organizer to occupy a minimum percentage or number of rooms in a reserved group block, with a monetary penalty (attrition fee) charged for any shortfall below that threshold.

Attrition clauses are standard in group hotel contracts for meetings, conferences, incentive travel, and tour groups. The minimum pick-up is typically set at 80–90% of the contracted room block. If the group falls short, the hotel calculates the attrition fee by multiplying the number of unfulfilled rooms by the contracted room rate (sometimes less the hotel's estimated avoided costs). Some contracts apply a sliding scale, with the fee rate increasing as the shortfall grows. Groups can mitigate attrition exposure by: (1) negotiating a realistic room block; (2) releasing rooms back to the hotel in tranches before the cut-off date; (3) including force majeure clauses that excuse attrition fees when attendance drops due to events beyond the organizer's control.

**한국어 / Korean** — **객실 소비 의무(Attrition)** — 호텔 단체 계약에서 단체 주최자가 예약된 단체 블록의 최소 비율 또는 수의 객실을 실제 이용해야 하는 계약상 의무로, 그 기준에 미달할 경우 금전적 위약금(attrition fee)이 청구된다. Attrition 조항은 일반 재고에서 제외된 채 팔리지 않은 객실에 따른 수익 손실로부터 호텔을 보호하며, 단체 주최자는 계약상 반환(cut-off) 일자 이전에 미사용 객실을 반환함으로써 위약금을 피할 수 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Attrition** vs [Allotment](/lodging/hotel-rate/allotment.md) — An allotment is the block of rooms pre-assigned to a partner or group; attrition is the contractual minimum pick-up obligation attached to that block, specifying how many of those allotted rooms must actually be used or paid for.
- **Attrition** vs [Release Period](/lodging/hotel-rate/release-period.md) — The release period is the deadline by which unbooked allotment rooms are returned to the hotel's general inventory; attrition is the penalty owed if the group still falls short of its minimum pick-up commitment on the accounting date.
- **Attrition** vs [Hotel Walk](/lodging/hotel-dist/hotel-walk.md) — A hotel walk occurs when the hotel relocates an arriving guest due to overbooking—the hotel selling too many rooms; attrition is the opposite risk, where the group organizer does not use enough of the rooms they blocked.
