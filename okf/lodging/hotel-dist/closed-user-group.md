> A Closed User Group (CUG) is a private, access-restricted pricing tier in hotel and travel distribution in which a special rate is only visible and bookable by verified members of a defined group — typically authenticated registered users of an OTA, airline loyalty members, or hotel direct-booking programme subscribers. Because the rate is not publicly displayed, it is generally accepted as a permitted exception to standard rate parity clauses, enabling hotels to offer lower prices to specific audiences without breaching their public rate commitments.

To qualify as a genuine CUG, the rate must typically meet several conditions: membership is not automatic — users must actively opt in; access requires a password-protected login or authenticated session; the member must have a registered profile; and the discounted rate must not be accessible to anonymous visitors. Hotels leverage CUG pricing through OTAs (enabling member-only rates that technically comply with rate parity) and through their own direct-booking programmes. The CUG exception grew in importance after legal challenges to broad rate parity clauses in Europe, where competition authorities in France, Germany, and the UK imposed partial restrictions on parity obligations. Rate shopping and parity auditing tools can detect CUG rates by creating test member accounts — a practice both hotels and OTAs actively monitor.

**한국어 / Korean** — **폐쇄형 사용자 그룹(CUG)** — CUG(Closed User Group, 폐쇄형 사용자 그룹)는 호텔·여행 유통에서 사용되는 접근 제한 특별 요금 등급으로, OTA의 인증된 회원, 항공사 로열티 회원, 또는 호텔 직접 예약 프로그램 구독자 등 특정 그룹의 검증된 회원에게만 특별 요금이 표시되고 예약 가능한 구조이다. 요금이 공개적으로 표시되지 않으므로 일반적으로 표준 요율 동등(rate parity) 조항의 허용 예외로 인정되어, 호텔이 공개 요금 약정을 위반하지 않고 특정 고객층에 낮은 가격을 제공할 수 있다.

정당한 CUG로 인정받으려면 요금은 보통 다음 조건을 충족해야 한다: 회원 가입이 자동이 아닌 능동적 선택일 것; 비밀번호 보호 로그인 또는 인증된 세션이 필요할 것; 회원이 등록 프로필을 보유할 것; 할인 요금이 익명 방문자에게 접근 불가할 것. CUG 예외는 유럽에서의 요율 동등 조항 법적 분쟁 이후 더욱 중요해졌으며, 프랑스·독일·영국의 경쟁 당국이 광범위한 동등 의무에 부분 제한을 부과하였다.

# Distinctions
- **Closed User Group (CUG)** vs [Rate Parity Clause](/lodging/hotel-dist/rate-parity-clause.md) — A Rate Parity Clause is a contract provision requiring a hotel to offer the same rate across distribution channels; the CUG rate is the most widely accepted exception to that clause, because the rate is not publicly visible and is accessible only to a verified member group.
- **Closed User Group (CUG)** vs [Opaque Inventory](/lodging/hotel-dist/opaque-inventory.md) — Opaque Inventory hides the hotel brand or property name until after purchase; a CUG rate reveals the exact hotel to the member but restricts access to the rate behind a login. The discount mechanism and the information withheld are fundamentally different.
