> A hotel brand standard is the documented set of operational specifications, physical design requirements, quality benchmarks, and service protocols that a hotel property must meet and maintain in order to carry and retain a franchisor's brand flag. Brand standards are enforced through quality assurance inspections and are typically embedded in franchise agreements and management contracts, giving the brand owner the right to terminate the agreement if standards fall below defined thresholds.

Brand standards typically span multiple dimensions: physical plant (room size, bath fixtures, bedding specifications, lobby design, fitness centre, pool), technology (property management system, in-room entertainment, Wi-Fi speed), food and beverage, loyalty programme integration, safety and security, and service delivery (check-in time, guest satisfaction scores). Hotel groups such as IHG, Marriott, and Hilton publish brand standards for each of their distinct brands — a Holiday Inn Express standard differs substantially from an InterContinental standard — and update them periodically. Compliance is audited annually or biannually through unannounced or scheduled Quality Assurance (QA) inspections, and scores are typically expressed as a percentage or a numerical rating. A poor QA score can trigger a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) and, if not remedied, termination. New-build and conversion projects must meet a Brand Approval review before opening; refurbishments must follow a Property Improvement Plan (PIP) that aligns the asset with current brand standards.

**한국어 / Korean** — **호텔 브랜드 기준(Hotel Brand Standard)** — 호텔 브랜드 기준은 호텔 프랜차이저의 브랜드 마크를 사용하고 유지하기 위해 호텔 자산이 충족하고 유지해야 하는 운영 사양, 물리적 설계 요건, 품질 기준, 서비스 프로토콜에 관한 문서화된 집합이다. 브랜드 기준은 품질 보증(QA) 점검을 통해 집행되며, 일반적으로 프랜차이즈 계약 및 운영 계약에 명시되어 기준이 정해진 임계값 이하로 떨어지면 브랜드 소유자가 계약을 해지할 권리를 갖는다.

브랜드 기준은 보통 여러 차원에 걸쳐 있다: 물리적 설비(객실 크기, 욕실 비품, 침구 사양, 로비 설계, 피트니스 센터, 수영장), 기술(PMS, 객실 내 엔터테인먼트, Wi-Fi 속도), 식음료, 로열티 프로그램 연동, 안전·보안, 서비스 제공(체크인 시간, 고객 만족도 점수) 등이다. IHG, 매리어트, 힐튼 같은 호텔 그룹은 각 브랜드에 대한 브랜드 기준을 발행하며 주기적으로 업데이트한다. 준수 여부는 매년 또는 2년마다 불시 또는 예정된 QA 점검을 통해 감사된다.

# Distinctions
- **Hotel Brand Standard** vs [Soft Brand](/lodging/hotel-dist/soft-brand.md) — A brand standard defines the non-negotiable mandatory requirements a hotel must meet to carry a hard brand flag; a soft brand is a collection concept that tolerates significant property individuality while providing some shared marketing benefits — soft-brand members often face lighter or more flexible brand standards than hard-branded hotels.
- **Hotel Brand Standard** vs [Hotel Management Agreement (HMA)](/lodging/hotel-dist/hotel-management-agreement-hma.md) — A brand standard is the technical specification of what the property must physically and operationally look like; a Hotel Management Agreement (HMA) is the legal contract between the property owner and the operator or franchisor that grants the right to use the brand flag, and embeds the brand standards as contractual obligations.
