> A booking channel is a specific distribution medium through which a supplier (hotel, airline, tour operator) receives a reservation — such as brand direct (own website, mobile app, or voice centre), OTA, GDS/corporate booking tool, wholesale/bedbank, metasearch click-out, or walk-in. Revenue managers track channel mix to understand acquisition costs, guest segment profiles, and each source's contribution to total demand.

Hotels typically segment booking channels into: (1) Direct — brand.com, mobile app, voice centre, walk-in — carrying zero or minimal acquisition cost; (2) OTA — Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb — charging 15–25% commission; (3) GDS — Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport — serving corporate and TMC bookers; (4) Wholesale / Bedbank — pre-purchased allotments resold to tour operators; (5) Metasearch — Google Hotel Ads, TripAdvisor click-outs; and (6) Walk-in / Phone. Tracking channel mix enables revenue managers to balance volume, cost per booking, length of stay, and cancellation risk across segments.

**한국어 / Korean** — **예약 채널(Booking Channel)** — 예약 채널은 호텔·항공·투어 운영사 등 공급자가 예약을 접수하는 특정 유통 매체로, 브랜드 직접·OTA·GDS/기업 예약 도구·도매상/베드뱅크·메타검색·워크인 등이 있다. 수익 관리자는 채널 믹스를 추적하여 고객 확보 비용, 세그먼트 프로필, 각 소스의 기여도를 파악한다.

# Distinctions
- **Booking Channel** vs Channel Manager — A Channel Manager is the software system that distributes a hotel's rates and inventory to multiple booking channels simultaneously; a booking channel is the destination (OTA platform, direct website, GDS connection) that the channel manager feeds.
- **Booking Channel** vs Distribution Cost — Distribution Cost is the aggregate expense to generate a booking; a Booking Channel is the medium through which that booking arrives, each channel carrying its own cost structure.
