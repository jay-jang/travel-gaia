> A fully furnished self-catering apartment available on a short-to-medium term basis, offering hotel-like services (housekeeping, front-desk, Wi-Fi) alongside kitchen facilities and extra living space. It targets corporate relocations, long-stay business travellers, and extended family trips where a traditional hotel room is impractical or cost-inefficient.

Serviced apartments occupy a distinct segment between a hotel and a private short-term rental. The Association of Serviced Apartment Providers (ASAP) defines them as accommodation providing a living area and a fully equipped kitchen alongside hotel-like services such as regular housekeeping, utilities, and front-desk support. They are available by the night (often with a minimum stay of 7 or 30 nights for favourable rates) or on monthly terms. Key buyer segments include corporate relocation programmes (HR-managed, often using a Travel Management Company or direct supplier agreements), extended project-team deployments, and leisure travellers on multi-week trips. Serviced apartments are distributed via corporate housing platforms (e.g., CHBO, Blueground), specialist aggregators (HRS, SilverDoor, Hotelbeds Corporate), OTAs (Booking.com, Airbnb), and direct channel. Revenue is measured by ADR and REVPAR similarly to hotels, but longer average lengths of stay and lower distribution costs often result in higher profitability per available unit. Unlike short-term rentals (STR) on peer-to-peer platforms, serviced apartments are operated professionally and must comply with local planning, licensing, and safety regulations.

**한국어 / Korean** — **서비스 아파트(Serviced Apartment)** — 단기~중기로 제공되는 완전 가구 갖춤·자취형 아파트로, 하우스키핑·프런트 데스크·Wi-Fi 등 호텔식 서비스와 함께 주방 시설 및 추가 생활 공간을 갖춘다. 기업 이주, 장기 출장, 호텔 객실이 비실용적이거나 비용 효율이 낮은 장기 가족 여행을 대상으로 한다.

서비스 아파트는 호텔과 개인 단기 임대의 중간에 위치한 별개의 세그먼트다. ASAP는 정기 하우스키핑, 유틸리티, 프런트 데스크 지원 등 호텔식 서비스와 함께 거실 및 완비된 주방을 제공하는 숙소로 정의한다. 야간 단위로 이용하거나 월 단위로 계약할 수 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Serviced Apartment** vs [Short-Term Rental](/lodging/hotel-dist/short-term-rental.md) — A Short-Term Rental (STR) is typically a privately owned home let on a peer-to-peer platform (Airbnb, Vrbo) with variable service levels; a serviced apartment is a professionally operated, purpose-built or converted property with consistent hotel-like services, longer minimum stays, and corporate compliance standards.
