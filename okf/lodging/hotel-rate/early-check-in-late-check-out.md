> Early check-in (ECI) is a hotel service that allows a guest to access their room before the property's standard check-in time (typically 15:00). Late check-out (LCO) allows a guest to retain the room past the standard departure time (typically 11:00–12:00). Both services are subject to room availability, may carry a fee or hourly rate, and are frequently offered as a complimentary benefit to loyalty programme elite members or guests booking directly. They represent an ancillary revenue opportunity for properties and a significant guest satisfaction driver.

Standard check-in and check-out windows exist because housekeeping needs a gap of 3–5 hours to clean and turn over a room. Early check-in compresses this window from the arrival end; late check-out compresses it from the departure end. Fee structures vary: a flat fee (USD 25–75) for up to 4 hours; a half-day rate (50% of ADR) for a 6-hour extension; or a full night if the guest departs 6+ hours after stated check-out time. In hotel loyalty programmes, guaranteed late check-out is among the most valued elite benefits. Revenue management systems increasingly integrate ECI/LCO as yield-managed ancillary products, dynamically priced by occupancy pressure.

**한국어 / Korean** — **조기 체크인·지연 체크아웃(ECI/LCO)** — 조기 체크인(ECI)은 표준 체크인 시각(통상 15:00) 이전에 객실을 이용하는 서비스이고, 지연 체크아웃(LCO)은 표준 퇴실 시각(통상 11:00~12:00) 이후에도 객실을 유지하는 서비스이다. 모두 객실 가용 상황에 따르며 추가 요금이 부과될 수 있다. 로열티 프로그램 엘리트 혜택으로 무료 제공되며, 수익 관리 시스템에서 점유율 기반 동적 가격의 부가 상품으로 통합된다.

# Distinctions
- **Early Check-In / Late Check-Out** vs [Day Use](/lodging/hotel-rate/day-use.md) — Day use is a standalone booking for daytime-only room access without an overnight stay, typically at 40–70% of BAR. ECI/LCO extends an existing overnight reservation, not a new booking type.
- **Early Check-In / Late Check-Out** vs [Guaranteed Reservation](/lodging/hotel-dist/guaranteed-reservation.md) — A guaranteed reservation secures the room against a no-show regardless of arrival time; ECI/LCO adjusts the time window of an already-confirmed booking.
