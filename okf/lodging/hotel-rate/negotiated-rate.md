> A hotel room rate contracted in advance between a hotel or hotel chain and a specific corporate account, travel management company (TMC), or consortia, guaranteeing preferential pricing for qualifying travelers in exchange for a committed volume or preferred-supplier designation. Negotiated rates are loaded against a rate code in the hotel's CRS/PMS and accessed via agreed channels for a defined contract period, typically one calendar year.

Negotiated rates are the cornerstone of corporate hotel programs. A company (or its TMC on its behalf) solicits proposals from hotels—often via the annual RFP (Request for Proposal) cycle—specifying estimated room nights and preferred markets. Hotels respond with net or commissionable rate offers, and the agreed rate is assigned a Rate Access Code (RAC) so it appears only in authorized booking channels. Local Negotiated Rates (LNRs) are property-specific agreements, while consortium rates leverage collective volume across multiple client companies. Unlike BAR, which varies with demand, a negotiated rate is fixed for the contract year, giving travelers and travel managers predictable cost and simplified T&E reporting.

**한국어 / Korean** — **협약 요금(Negotiated Rate / LNR)** — 호텔 또는 호텔 체인이 특정 기업 계정, 여행 관리 회사(TMC), 또는 컨소시엄과 사전에 계약하여 일정 수요량 또는 우선 공급자 지정을 조건으로 적격 여행자에게 우대 요금을 보장하는 객실 요금이다. 협약 요금은 통상 1년 단위의 계약 기간 동안 호텔의 CRS/PMS에 레이트 코드로 등록되어 합의된 채널을 통해 접근된다.

협약 요금은 기업 호텔 프로그램의 근간이다. 기업(또는 그 TMC)은 연간 RFP(제안 요청) 사이클을 통해 호텔에 예상 숙박 야수와 선호 시장을 명시한 제안을 요청한다. 합의된 요금은 승인된 예약 채널에서만 표시되도록 Rate Access Code(RAC)에 할당된다. LNR(Local Negotiated Rate)은 개별 숙박 시설별 협약이며, 컨소시엄 요금은 여러 고객사의 집합 물량을 활용한다. 수요에 따라 변동하는 BAR와 달리 협약 요금은 계약 기간 동안 고정되어 예측 가능한 비용과 간편한 보고를 제공한다.

# Distinctions
- **Negotiated Rate** vs [BAR](/lodging/hotel-rate/bar.md) — BAR (Best Available Rate) is the lowest publicly bookable rate at a given time and fluctuates with demand; a negotiated rate is a fixed, privately contracted rate for a specific account, invisible to the general public.
- **Negotiated Rate** vs [Rate Parity](/lodging/hotel-dist/rate-parity.md) — Rate parity requires that a hotel offer the same rate across all public distribution channels; a negotiated rate is exempt from parity obligations because it is a private, account-specific contract rate, not a public rate.
- **Negotiated Rate** vs [Rate Fence](/lodging/hotel-rate/rate-fence.md) — A rate fence is a rule that restricts who can access a public fare or rate; a negotiated rate is controlled by account credentials (Rate Access Code) rather than a public fence condition.
