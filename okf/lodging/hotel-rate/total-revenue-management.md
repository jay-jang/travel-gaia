> A hotel revenue optimisation strategy that extends revenue management principles beyond guest rooms to all revenue-generating departments — food and beverage, meetings and events, spa, parking, retail, and ancillary services — with the goal of maximising total hotel revenue and profit per available space or time unit (expressed through TRevPAR and GOPPAR metrics).

Traditional hotel revenue management (RM) focuses almost exclusively on optimising room revenue — setting rates, managing availability, and controlling overbooking for guest rooms. Total Revenue Management (TRM) recognises that a hotel generates significant income from non-room sources (restaurants, bars, function space, spa, golf, parking, in-room services) and applies the same demand-forecasting and pricing disciplines across all these streams. HSMAI defines TRM as "the discipline of maximising the profitability of all revenue streams from a hotel." In practice, this requires cross-departmental collaboration, shared demand data, and integrated revenue management systems (RMS) capable of handling multi-department optimisation. TRM performance is tracked through TRevPAR (total revenue per available room) and GOPPAR (gross operating profit per available room), rather than RevPAR alone.

**한국어 / Korean** — **총수익 관리(Total Revenue Management, TRM)** — 호텔의 수익 최적화 전략을 객실을 넘어 식음료·회의 및 이벤트·스파·주차·소매·부가 서비스 등 모든 수익 창출 부서로 확장하는 접근법으로, 가용 공간 또는 시간 단위당 총수익과 이익을 극대화하는 것을 목표로 한다.

전통적인 객실 수익 관리와 달리, TRM은 모든 비객실 수익 부문에 수요 예측·가격 책정 원칙을 적용한다. 성과는 RevPAR만이 아니라 TRevPAR과 GOPPAR로 측정된다.

# Distinctions
- **Total Revenue Management** vs [Revenue Management](/air/air-shop/revenue-management.md) — Revenue Management in the air/hotel context traditionally focuses on a single inventory type (seat or room) to maximise revenue per unit; Total Revenue Management expands this scope to all hotel revenue departments and aims to maximise total profit, not just room revenue.
- **Total Revenue Management** vs [TRevPAR](/lodging/hotel-rate/trevpar.md) — TRevPAR is the KPI that measures total revenue divided by available rooms; Total Revenue Management is the organisational strategy and discipline that drives TRevPAR improvement.
