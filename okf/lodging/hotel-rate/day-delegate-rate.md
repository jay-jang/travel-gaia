> An all-inclusive, per-person daily meeting package offered by a hotel or conference venue that bundles the main meeting room hire, audio-visual equipment, tea/coffee breaks, and a working lunch into a single quoted price per delegate per day — the standard pricing unit in the MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) industry.

The DDR simplifies event budgeting by bundling what would otherwise be separately negotiated cost items — venue hire, catering, AV, and stationery — into a single headline number that scales linearly with headcount. A typical DDR package covers: main meeting room (full-day); two tea/coffee breaks; a working lunch (two courses); projector, screen, and flipchart; Wi-Fi; and delegate stationery. Items excluded from DDR vary by property but commonly include overnight accommodation, parking at city-centre venues, alcohol, and room hire for break-out rooms. An extended variant — the 24-Hour Delegate Rate (24HDR) — adds one night of accommodation and dinner. In the UK, DDRs are benchmarked by the Meetings Industry Association (MIA) and included in hotel group rate cards; similar packages are sold globally under terms such as Day Meeting Package (DMP) or Conference Day Rate.

**한국어 / Korean** — **일일 참가자 요금(DDR, Day Delegate Rate)** — 호텔이나 컨퍼런스 시설이 회의·이벤트 고객에게 제공하는 1인당 1일 통합 패키지 요금으로, 회의실 임대·음향영상 장비·티/커피 브레이크·업무용 점심을 단일 가격으로 묶는다. MICE(회의·인센티브·컨퍼런스·전시) 업계의 표준 가격 단위이다.

DDR은 시설 임대·케이터링·음향영상·문구류 등 개별적으로 협상해야 하는 비용 항목들을 단일 가격으로 묶어 이벤트 예산 편성을 간소화하며, 참가자 수에 따라 선형적으로 확장된다. 일반적인 DDR 패키지에 포함되는 항목: 메인 회의실(하루 종일), 티/커피 브레이크 2회, 2코스 업무용 점심, 프로젝터·스크린·플립차트, Wi-Fi, 참가자 문구류. DDR에서 제외되는 항목은 시설에 따라 다르지만, 일반적으로 숙박·도심 시설 주차·주류·별도 회의실 임대가 포함된다. 확장 형태인 24시간 참가자 요금(24HDR)은 1박 숙박과 저녁 식사를 추가한다.

# Distinctions
- **Day Delegate Rate (DDR)** vs [ADR](/lodging/hotel-rate/adr.md) — ADR (Average Daily Rate) is a backward-looking hotel revenue metric measuring the average price achieved per occupied room; DDR is a forward-facing per-person package price for meetings and events that bundles room hire with catering and AV into a single contracted rate.
- **Day Delegate Rate (DDR)** vs [Group Block](/lodging/hotel-rate/group-block.md) — A group block is a reserved allocation of guestrooms (sleeping accommodation) for a group at a negotiated rate; a DDR is the meeting-space and catering package for the same group during their conference day — the two are often quoted together but cover different hospitality cost elements.
- **Day Delegate Rate (DDR)** vs [MICE](/common/customer/mice.md) — MICE is the industry segment encompassing Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions; DDR is one of the primary pricing formats through which hotels sell their meeting facilities and catering services to the MICE segment.
