> A city tax (also called tourist tax or accommodation tax) is a per-person, per-night levy imposed by a city, municipality, or regional authority on guests staying in paid accommodation — hotels, vacation rentals, hostels, or serviced apartments. It is separate from national VAT/GST, typically collected by the property and remitted to the local authority, and is frequently excluded from the quoted room rate.

City taxes vary widely by jurisdiction in rate, basis of calculation, and collection method. Common approaches include a flat fee per person per night (e.g., Paris taxe de séjour, tiered by accommodation category from €1 to €15+ per night), a percentage of room rate (e.g., Rome or Venice), or a combination. Some cities exempt children, long-stay guests, or business travelers on official duty. The tax is frequently shown as a separate line item on the final hotel bill rather than included in quoted rates, which can create surprises for guests. Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) and GDS systems may show the rate excluding city tax, with the tax listed under "taxes and fees"; some platforms collect the tax at the time of booking while others leave collection to the property. Hotels must register with local authorities and remit collected taxes according to local filing schedules. Amsterdam, Barcelona, Berlin, Paris, Rome, and Venice are among the cities with significant, publicly documented city-tax schemes.

**한국어 / Korean** — **시티 택스(도시세/관광세)** — 시티 택스(도시세·관광세·숙박세)는 호텔·휴가용 렌탈·호스텔·서비스드 아파트 등 유료 숙박 시설 투숙객에게 시·자치단체·지역 당국이 부과하는 인당·박당 징수금이다. 국가 단위 부가가치세(VAT/GST)와 별개이며, 통상 숙박 시설이 징수하여 지방 당국에 납부한다. 공시 객실 요금에서 제외되는 경우가 많다.

시티 택스는 세율, 산정 기준, 징수 방식에서 관할권마다 크게 다르다. 일반적인 방식은 인당·박당 정액, 객실 요금의 비율, 또는 혼합 방식이다. 일부 도시는 어린이, 장기 투숙객, 또는 공무 출장 비즈니스 여행자를 면제한다. 공시 요금에 포함되지 않고 최종 숙박 청구서의 별도 항목으로 표시되는 경우가 많다.

# Distinctions
- **City Tax** vs [Rack Rate](/lodging/hotel-rate/rack-rate.md) — Rack rate is the hotel's highest published undiscounted room rate; a city tax is an additional municipal levy on top of any room rate that is excluded from the rate itself and collected separately. A guest pays both: the room rate plus the city tax.
- **City Tax** vs [Rate Plan](/lodging/hotel-rate/rate-plan.md) — A rate plan is the pricing construct that defines the room rate, inclusions, and conditions; a city tax is an externally mandated government levy applied on top of the rate plan regardless of its structure.
