> The United Nations specialized agency responsible for the promotion of responsible, sustainable, and universally accessible tourism. UNWTO provides leadership in the global tourism sector, sets international tourism classification standards, and publishes authoritative tourism statistics. Rebranded publicly as "UN Tourism" in 2023.

UNWTO (headquartered in Madrid) counts 160 member states plus territories and associate members drawn from private sector and academia. Its core functions include: publishing the International Recommendations for Tourism Statistics (IRTS 2008), which defines inbound/outbound/domestic tourism classifications used in national accounts; producing the annual World Tourism Barometer tracking international arrivals and receipts; running the Tourism Sustainability Group; and convening the biennial General Assembly. UNWTO data underpin national tourism satellite accounts and WTTC economic-impact modelling. The 'One Planet' programme, launched jointly with UN Environment Programme and WTTC, sets sustainability benchmarks aligned with the UN SDGs.

**한국어 / Korean** — **세계관광기구(UNWTO)** — 책임 있고 지속 가능하며 모든 사람이 이용할 수 있는 관광의 증진을 담당하는 UN 전문 기구. 1975년 설립되어 2003년 UN 전문 기구로 지정되었으며, 글로벌 관광 부문의 리더십을 제공하고, 국제 관광 분류 기준과 관광 정책을 발전시키며, 권위 있는 관광 통계 데이터베이스를 발행한다.

UNWTO는 국제관광통계권고(IRTS 2008) 발행, 연간 세계관광 풍향계(World Tourism Barometer) 발행, 관광 지속가능성 그룹 운영, 격년제 총회 소집 등을 주요 기능으로 한다. UNWTO 데이터는 국가 관광 위성계정(TSA)과 WTTC 경제 영향 모델링의 근거가 된다.

**Aliases:** `UNWTO`, `UN Tourism`, `United Nations World Tourism Organization`, `World Tourism Organisation`

# Distinctions
- **World Tourism Organization** vs [GSTC Criteria](/common/sustainability/gstc-criteria.md) — UNWTO is the intergovernmental body that sets international tourism classification standards and policy; the GSTC Criteria is a sustainability standard developed by a separate multi-stakeholder council (though UNWTO and GSTC collaborate on sustainable tourism goals).
- **World Tourism Organization** vs [Destination Management Organization](/tours/tour-ops/destination-management-organization.md) — A DMO is a local or regional entity that markets and manages a tourism destination; UNWTO is the global intergovernmental authority that establishes tourism statistics methodology, classification, and policy frameworks that DMOs apply at the destination level.
