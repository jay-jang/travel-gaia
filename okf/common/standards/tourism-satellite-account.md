> A statistical framework developed jointly by UNWTO, the UN Statistics Division (UNSD), Eurostat, and OECD to measure the economic contribution of tourism in a manner fully consistent with the System of National Accounts (SNA). The TSA extracts tourism-related output, value added, employment, and investment from the broader economy using a set of ten standard tables, enabling cross-country comparisons of tourism's GDP contribution, employment, and capital formation.

A Tourism Satellite Account is called a "satellite" account because it sits alongside, rather than inside, the national accounts — it reorganises existing national accounts data around the tourism demand concept without creating a new accounting system. The recommended framework (TSA:RMF 2008, published by the UN Statistics Division) defines ten standard tables: T1 Inbound Tourism Expenditure; T2 Domestic Tourism Expenditure; T3 Outbound Tourism Expenditure; T4 Internal Tourism Consumption; T5 Production Accounts of Tourism Industries; T6 Domestic Supply and Internal Tourism Consumption; T7 Employment in Tourism Industries; T8 Tourism Gross Fixed Capital Formation; T9 Tourism Collective Consumption; T10 Non-Monetary Tourism Indicators. Pre-COVID, UNWTO reported that tourism directly accounted for approximately 3.3% of global GDP (10.4% including indirect and induced effects). OECD publishes annual tourism statistics using TSA methodology.

**한국어 / Korean** — **관광 위성계정(Tourism Satellite Account)** — UNWTO, UN 통계국(UNSD), Eurostat, OECD가 공동 개발한 통계 프레임워크로, 국민계정 체계(SNA)와 완전히 일치하는 방식으로 관광의 경제적 기여를 측정한다. 10개 표준 표를 사용하여 관광 관련 산출물, 부가가치, 고용, 투자를 추출하여 국가 간 비교를 가능하게 한다.

# Distinctions
- **Tourism Satellite Account** vs [Inbound Tourism](/tours/tour-product/inbound-tourism.md) — Inbound Tourism is the activity of non-resident visitors traveling to a given country; the Tourism Satellite Account is the statistical framework that measures the economic value of all tourism types (inbound, domestic, outbound) using national-accounts methodology to produce GDP and employment figures.
