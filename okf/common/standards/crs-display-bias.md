> The former practice by which the airline that owned or controlled a computerised reservation system (CRS) manipulated the primary display of flight options to place its own flights ahead of competitors' flights, regardless of price, schedule, or routing quality. CRS display bias is now prohibited by regulation in the EU (Regulation (EC) No 80/2009) and the US (DOT CRS rules), which require neutral, non-discriminatory display of flight and fare information.

In the 1970s and 1980s, major US and European airlines developed and owned proprietary CRS platforms — Sabre (American Airlines), Apollo (United), Galileo (British Airways, KLM et al.), Amadeus (Air France, Iberia, Lufthansa, SAS). These systems were distributed to travel agencies, which depended on the CRS primary display to select and book flights. Owning airlines adjusted display algorithms to favor their own flights in the "halo" position (the first screen shown), which dramatically increased their booking share at the expense of competitors. In 1984 the US Civil Aeronautics Board (CAB) issued the first CRS rules prohibiting display bias, subsequently updated by the DOT. The EU enacted its first CRS Code of Conduct (Regulation (EEC) No 2299/89) in 1989 and updated it with Regulation (EC) No 80/2009, which imposes display-neutrality obligations on all system vendors operating in the EU. Airlines subsequently divested from CRS ownership, and all major GDS systems are now legally required to rank flights by objective criteria without discrimination by carrier identity.

**한국어 / Korean** — **CRS 화면 편향(CRS Display Bias)** — 컴퓨터 예약 시스템(CRS)을 소유 또는 통제하는 항공사가 가격·스케줄·경로 품질에 관계없이 자사 항공편을 경쟁사보다 앞에 배치하도록 1차 항공편 표시를 조작하던 과거의 관행. EU(Regulation (EC) No 80/2009) 및 미국(DOT CRS 규정)의 규제에 의해 현재는 금지되어 있다.

1970~80년대에 주요 미국·유럽 항공사들은 자체 CRS 플랫폼을 개발·소유했다. 여행사는 CRS 1차 표시에 의존해 항공편을 선택하고 예약했으며, 소유 항공사들은 "halo" 포지션에 자사 항공편이 오도록 알고리즘을 조정해 예약 점유율을 크게 높였다. EU와 미국의 규제로 이 관행은 금지되었고, 항공사들은 CRS 소유에서 철수했다. 현재 모든 주요 GDS 시스템은 항공사 신원에 의한 차별 없이 객관적 기준에 따라 항공편을 나열하도록 법적으로 요구된다.

# Distinctions
