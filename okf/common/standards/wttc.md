> A non-profit membership organisation whose members are the CEOs and Chairpersons of the world's leading travel and tourism companies, representing the private sector globally. WTTC is the primary authority on the economic and social contribution of the Travel & Tourism sector, producing annual Economic Impact Research covering 185 economies and the widely cited global Travel & Tourism GDP share figure.

Founded in 1990, the WTTC conducts its flagship annual Economic Impact Research (EIR) using a Tourism Satellite Account (TSA) methodology aligned with UNWTO and UN standards, estimating travel and tourism's total contribution to GDP (direct, indirect, and induced), employment, capital investment, and export earnings for each of 185 economies. The 2024 EIR estimated global Travel & Tourism GDP at approximately USD 11.1 trillion (around 10% of global GDP) and 348 million jobs. Beyond research, WTTC operates the Safe Travels stamp programme, Hotel Sustainability Basics (with GSTC), a Crisis Communication Centre, and publishes playbooks on aviation recovery, sustainable tourism, and technology adoption. WTTC convenes the annual WTTC Global Summit and advocates with governments for open-border and visa-facilitation policies.

**한국어 / Korean** — **세계 여행 관광 협의회(WTTC)** — WTTC는 세계 주요 여행·관광 기업의 CEO와 회장들로 구성된 비영리 회원 조직으로, 민간 부문을 전 세계적으로 대표한다. 여행·관광 분야의 경제적 기여에 관한 주요 권위 기관으로, 185개 경제권을 대상으로 연간 경제 영향 연구를 발표하며 전 세계적으로 주목받는 GDP 기여도 수치를 산출한다.

# Distinctions
- **WTTC (World Travel & Tourism Council)** vs [World Tourism Organization](/common/standards/unwto.md) — The World Tourism Organization (UNWTO) is a United Nations specialized agency that sets intergovernmental tourism norms and publishes official statistics; the WTTC is a private-sector CEO membership body that focuses on economic impact research and corporate advocacy for the travel sector, without regulatory authority.
- **WTTC (World Travel & Tourism Council)** vs [Tourism Satellite Account](/common/standards/tourism-satellite-account.md) — A Tourism Satellite Account (TSA) is the statistical framework for measuring tourism's economic contribution; the WTTC uses TSA methodology as the basis of its annual Economic Impact Research, applying it across 185 economies to produce its widely cited global figures.
