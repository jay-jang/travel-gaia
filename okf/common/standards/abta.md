> ABTA (formerly the Association of British Travel Agents) is the UK's largest travel trade association, representing travel agents and tour operators and providing consumer protection through a mandatory Code of Conduct and a financial protection scheme for non-air package holidays. ABTA membership is a widely recognised quality mark in UK travel, and works alongside the statutory ATOL scheme to cover the majority of UK holiday purchases.

ABTA was founded in 1950 and rebranded to ABTA — The Travel Association in 2007. It currently represents around 4,000 travel businesses across the UK. Core functions: (1) Code of Conduct: ABTA's Code governs member behaviour on advertising accuracy, fair terms of trade, changes to bookings, complaint handling, and repatriation obligations. Breaches can result in fines, suspension, or expulsion. (2) Financial protection for non-air packages: for land- or sea-based package holidays sold by ABTA members, consumers' money is protected against the member's insolvency; ABTA provides a replacement holiday or refund in the event of member failure. (3) Dispute resolution: ABTA operates a consumer affairs team and a low-cost arbitration scheme (administered by CEDR or ICAS) for unresolved complaints. (4) Government liaison: ABTA lobbies the UK government on travel regulations, Package Travel Regulations compliance, and consumer rights. ABTA does not cover air-inclusive package holidays sold by operators whose flights are ATOL-bonded; those are protected under ATOL. In practice, many ABTA members are also ATOL holders, providing a dual layer of protection.

법정 ATOL 제도와 함께 운영되어 영국 휴가 구매의 대부분을 보장하며, ATOL이 항공 패키지를 보장하는 반면 ABTA는 비항공 패키지와 행동 강령 준수를 담당한다.

# Distinctions
- **ABTA — The Travel Association** vs [Air Travel Organiser's Licence](/common/insurance/atol.md) — ATOL (Air Travel Organiser's Licence) is a statutory financial protection scheme administered by the UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) that specifically covers consumers who book air-inclusive package holidays; it is a legal requirement for operators selling such packages in the UK. ABTA is a voluntary trade association providing its own Code of Conduct and financial protection for non-air package holidays (land and sea packages without a flight), and also provides member services including lobbying and dispute resolution. The two schemes are complementary: many UK travel companies hold both ABTA membership and ATOL licences.
- **ABTA — The Travel Association** vs [American Society of Travel Advisors](/common/standards/asta.md) — ASTA (American Society of Travel Advisors) is the US trade body representing travel advisors and agencies, focused on professional standards, government advocacy, and certification (VTA). ABTA is the UK trade body with a stronger consumer-protection mandate — its Code of Conduct has binding financial consequences for members, and it operates an insolvency-protection fund for non-air packages, giving it a quasi-regulatory character that ASTA does not have.
