> The IATA Environmental Assessment (IEnvA) is a voluntary two-stage certification program operated by IATA that independently assesses and recognises the environmental management performance of aviation stakeholders — including airlines, airports, ground handlers, caterers, MROs, and freight forwarders. It is built on the ISO 14001 environmental management system standard and the framework of the IATA Operational Safety Audit (IOSA), adapted for environmental impact measurement and reduction.

IEnvA operates through two progressive stages: **Stage 1** assesses whether an organisation has established a sound environmental management system (EMS) — policies, objectives, procedures, and management reviews aligned with ISO 14001 — verified by an independent third-party auditor. **Stage 2**, the highest level of IEnvA compliance, requires the organisation to have identified its significant environmental impacts, to be demonstrating measurable operational performance improvements, and to have set verified reduction targets across key areas (fuel/energy, waste, water, noise, air quality).

Participating organisations appear on the public IEnvA Registry published by IATA. IEnvA is distinct from CORSIA (which governs CO₂ offsetting for international flights) and from Science-Based Targets (which set company-level decarbonisation pathways); IEnvA addresses the breadth of an organisation's environmental management system, not just carbon emissions.

**한국어 / Korean** — **IATA 환경평가 프로그램(IEnvA)** — IATA 환경평가(IEnvA)는 IATA가 운영하는 자발적 2단계 인증 프로그램으로, 항공사·공항·지상 조업사·기내식 업체·MRO·화물 운송 업체 등 항공 이해관계자의 환경 관리 성과를 독립적으로 평가·인정한다. ISO 14001 환경경영 시스템 표준과 IATA IOSA 체계를 기반으로 한다.

1단계는 조직의 환경경영 시스템(EMS) 수립 여부를 검증하고, 2단계(최고 수준)는 중요한 환경 영향 파악, 운영 성과 개선 시연, 감축 목표 수립을 요구한다.

# Distinctions
- **IEnvA** vs [CORSIA (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation)](/common/sustainability/corsia.md) — CORSIA is an ICAO market-based measure that requires airlines to offset CO₂ growth from international aviation above a 2019 baseline; IEnvA is an IATA certification program that assesses the breadth of an organisation's environmental management system across fuel, waste, water, noise and air quality — not limited to CO₂ compliance.
- **IEnvA** vs [Carbon Neutral](/common/sustainability/carbon-neutral.md) — Carbon Neutral is a declared state where an organisation's net CO₂ emissions are zero, achieved through reduction and offsetting; IEnvA is a process-based certification that verifies an organisation has a systematic environmental management approach — an IEnvA Stage 2 organisation is not necessarily carbon neutral.
