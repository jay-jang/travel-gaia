> A joint methodology published by the International Tourism Partnership (ITP) and the World Travel & Tourism Council (WTTC) that defines a standardized approach for calculating the carbon footprint of a hotel stay, expressed as kg CO2e per occupied room night, enabling corporate travel buyers to aggregate accommodation emissions in their Scope 3 Category 6 inventories.

HCMI calculates the carbon intensity of a hotel by summing Scope 1 (on-site combustion) and Scope 2 (purchased electricity) emissions, dividing by the number of occupied room nights over the measurement period, and expressing the result in kg CO2e per occupied room night. The metric is property-specific and enables comparisons across hotels and chains. HCMI data is supplied to GDS platforms, online booking tools (OBTs), and travel management companies (TMCs) so that bookers can see and compare the carbon intensity of accommodation options at the point of booking. Corporate buyers use HCMI-based data to quantify hotel stays in their GHG Protocol Scope 3 Category 6 reports and to support decarbonization of their travel programs.

**한국어 / Korean** — **호텔 탄소 측정 이니셔티브(HCMI)** — 국제관광 파트너십(ITP)과 세계여행관광협의회(WTTC)가 발표한 공동 방법론으로, 숙박의 탄소발자국을 "점유 객실 1박당 kg CO2e" 단위로 산정하는 표준화된 접근법을 정의하며, 기업 여행 구매자가 Scope 3 카테고리 6 인벤토리에 숙박 배출량을 집계할 수 있게 한다.

HCMI는 Scope 1(현장 연소) 및 Scope 2(구입 전력) 배출량의 합계를 측정 기간의 점유 객실 박수로 나누어, "점유 객실 1박당 kg CO2e"로 표현해 호텔의 탄소 집약도를 산정한다. 이 지표는 부동산별 특성이 반영되며 호텔 및 체인 간 비교를 가능하게 한다. HCMI 데이터는 GDS 플랫폼, 온라인 예약 도구(OBT), 여행 관리 회사(TMC)에 제공되어 예약자가 예약 시점에 숙박 옵션의 탄소 집약도를 확인·비교할 수 있다. 기업 구매자는 HCMI 기반 데이터를 사용해 GHG 프로토콜 Scope 3 카테고리 6 보고서에 호텔 숙박을 정량화하고 여행 프로그램의 탈탄소화를 지원한다.

# Distinctions
- **Hotel Carbon Measurement Initiative (HCMI)** vs [GHG Protocol Scope 3 (Business Travel)](/common/sustainability/ghg-protocol-scope-3-business-travel.md) — GHG Protocol Scope 3 Category 6 is the corporate accounting framework that tells a company how to measure and report its travel emissions; HCMI is a supply-side methodology that tells a hotel how to calculate and publish its own carbon intensity figure so that corporate buyers can use it in their Scope 3 inventories.
- **Hotel Carbon Measurement Initiative (HCMI)** vs [Green Key Certification](/common/sustainability/green-key-certification.md) — Green Key is an eco-label certifying that a hotel meets environmental criteria across a broad range of operational areas; HCMI is specifically a carbon-measurement methodology that quantifies one number — CO2e per occupied room night — for use in buyer reporting, not a certification scheme.
