> A corporate climate strategy in which an organisation invests in greenhouse gas emission reductions, removals, or avoided deforestation within its own value chain and supply chain — for example, funding agroforestry among its food suppliers, restoring reefs at cruise destinations, or supporting regenerative land management by the farmers who supply its lodges — as distinct from carbon offsetting, which purchases credits from unrelated third-party projects outside the company's supply chain. Insetting keeps the environmental and community benefit directly connected to the commercial activity generating the emissions.

The International Platform for Insetting (IPI) defines carbon insetting as "the actions taken by an organisation to fight climate change within its own value chain in a manner which generates multiple positive sustainable impacts." The core logic is that an emitting company's most credible path to decarbonisation is to fund climate action among the suppliers, farmers, landowners, and communities already embedded in its own commercial relationships — not to buy credits from entirely unrelated projects. In the travel and tourism sector, insetting is increasingly favoured over external offsetting because tour operators, cruise lines, and hotel chains often have deep, long-term relationships with destination communities and supply-chain partners who can deliver verifiable land-use and climate co-benefits. Practical examples include: a tour operator funding agroforestry training for the communities supplying food to its camps; a cruise line restoring mangroves in its ports of call; a hotel brand supporting solar installation for its local food suppliers. The WTTC's Net Zero Roadmap identifies insetting as the primary decarbonisation strategy for travel and tourism because it reduces Scope 3 emissions at source. Insetting projects can generate carbon credits under Plan Vivo, Gold Standard, or VCS standards, but IPI requires demonstrated supply-chain linkage and community co-benefits as mandatory criteria. ICROA, which governs voluntary offset quality, does not currently accredit insetting — the two concepts are complementary but distinct.

**한국어 / Korean** — **인세팅(Insetting)** — 탄소 상쇄(Offsetting)와 달리 기업이 자체 가치사슬 및 공급망 내에서 온실가스 배출 감축·제거·삼림 벌채 방지에 투자하는 기업 기후 전략이다. 예를 들어 식음료 공급업체의 혼농임업 자금 지원, 크루즈 기항지 암초 복원, 롯지에 식재료를 공급하는 농가의 재생 농업 지원 등이 이에 해당한다.

IPI는 탄소 인세팅을 "다양한 긍정적 지속가능 영향을 창출하는 방식으로 자체 가치사슬 내에서 기후변화에 대응하는 조직의 행동"으로 정의한다. 인세팅은 이미 자체 상업적 관계에 포함된 공급업체·농부·토지 소유자·지역사회에서 기후 행동에 자금을 지원하는 것으로, 공급망 외부의 무관한 프로젝트 크레딧을 구매하는 상쇄와 구별된다. WTTC 넷제로 로드맵은 인세팅을 여행·관광의 주요 탈탄소화 전략으로 제시한다.

# Distinctions
- **Insetting** vs [Carbon Offset](/common/sustainability/carbon-offset.md) — Carbon offsetting purchases credits from projects that are geographically and commercially unconnected to the buyer's own supply chain. Insetting invests in climate action among the buyer's own suppliers, communities, and operational partners. Insetting is supply-chain-internal; offsetting is supply-chain-external. Both can generate carbon credits, but only insetting integrates the climate benefit into the company's commercial relationships.
- **Insetting** vs [Regenerative Tourism](/common/sustainability/regenerative-tourism.md) — Regenerative Tourism is the broad philosophy and practice of tourism that restores and improves natural and social systems in destinations beyond simply reducing harm; Insetting is a specific financial and carbon-accounting mechanism for investing climate action within the supply chain. A regenerative tourism approach may incorporate insetting as one of its tools, but insetting is more narrowly defined by supply-chain linkage and GHG accounting criteria.
