> Flygskam (Swedish: flight shame) is a social movement that discourages air travel on the grounds of its outsized contribution to greenhouse gas emissions and climate change. Originating in Sweden in 2018 and amplified globally by climate activist Greta Thunberg, the movement prompted measurable shifts in travel behaviour in Scandinavia and influenced policy debates, airline sustainability strategies, and the accelerated adoption of sustainable aviation fuel commitments worldwide.

The term existed in Swedish before 2018, but it gained social force when Swedish athlete Björn Ferry, opera singer Malena Ernman, and others publicly discussed guilt about flying for environmental reasons in spring 2018. Greta Thunberg's refusal to fly — travelling instead by sailing boat to climate conferences — attracted global media coverage. In Sweden, domestic air passenger numbers declined noticeably in 2018–2019, and surveys showed growing "flight shame" sentiment among younger travellers. The companion concept *Tågskryt* (train brag — pride in choosing rail over air) emerged alongside it. The aviation industry responded with accelerated commitments: IATA's Fly Net Zero 2050 goal, airline net-zero pledges, SAF mandates (ReFuelEU Aviation), and carbon labelling schemes such as the Flight Emissions Label.

**한국어 / Korean** — **플뤼그스캄(비행 부끄러움)** — 플뤼그스캄(Flygskam, 스웨덴어: 비행 부끄러움)은 항공 여행이 온실가스 배출과 기후 변화에 과도하게 기여한다는 이유로 비행을 자제하도록 촉구하는 사회 운동이다. 2018년 스웨덴에서 시작되어 기후 활동가 그레타 툰베리를 통해 전 세계에 확산된 이 운동은 스칸디나비아 지역에서 여행 행동의 측정 가능한 변화를 이끌었으며, 항공사 지속 가능성 전략과 정책 논의에 영향을 미쳤다.

동반 개념인 *Tågskryt*(기차 자랑)—항공 대신 철도를 선택하는 것에 대한 자부심—이 함께 등장했다. 항공 산업은 IATA의 Fly Net Zero 2050 목표, SAF 의무화(ReFuelEU Aviation), 비행 배출 라벨 등으로 응답하였다.

# Distinctions
- **Flygskam (Flight Shame)** vs [Sustainable Tourism](/common/sustainability/sustainable-tourism.md) — Sustainable tourism is an industry management framework guiding how destinations and operators minimise harm; flygskam is a grassroots behavioural movement placing the moral burden of flight avoidance on the individual traveller rather than on industry systems. The two are complementary but operate at different levels.
- **Flygskam (Flight Shame)** vs [Fly Net Zero 2050](/common/sustainability/fly-net-zero-2050.md) — Fly Net Zero 2050 is the IATA-led industry commitment for commercial aviation to achieve net-zero by 2050 through SAF and new technology — a future supply-side decarbonisation roadmap; flygskam is a consumer behaviour movement encouraging individuals to fly less due to present-day aviation emissions.
