> A collaborative framework — co-founded by CDP, the UN Global Compact, World Resources Institute, and WWF — that defines methodologies for companies to set greenhouse-gas reduction targets aligned with limiting global warming to 1.5 °C; in travel, airlines, hotel groups, and OTAs commit to SBTi-validated targets as the anchor for their corporate net-zero roadmaps.

To receive SBTi validation, a company submits its proposed emission-reduction targets; SBTi scientists independently assess whether the targets are consistent with a 1.5 °C science-aligned pathway across Scope 1, 2, and material Scope 3 emissions. Validated companies appear on the public SBTi target dashboard and commit to annual progress disclosure. SBTi publishes sector-specific guidance: the Transport sector guidance (2021) is directly applicable to airlines, the Buildings and Real Estate guidance covers hotels. Major travel companies with SBTi-committed or validated targets include IAG, easyJet, Air France-KLM, Marriott International, IHG Hotels & Resorts, and Booking Holdings. Corporate travel buyers increasingly require supply-chain partners to hold SBTi commitments as part of Scope 3 Category 6 emission management.

**한국어 / Korean** — **과학기반 감축목표 이니셔티브(SBTi)** — CDP, UN 글로벌콤팩트, 세계자원연구소(WRI), WWF가 공동 설립한 협력 프레임워크로, 기업이 지구 온난화를 산업화 이전 대비 1.5°C로 제한하는 데 부합하는 온실가스 감축 목표를 설정하는 방법론을 정의한다. 여행업에서는 항공사·호텔 그룹·OTA가 SBTi 검증 목표를 기업 넷제로 로드맵의 근간으로 삼는다.

SBTi 검증을 받으려면 기업이 제안한 감축 목표를 제출하고, SBTi 과학자들이 해당 목표가 Scope 1·2 및 중요한 Scope 3 배출 전반에 걸쳐 1.5°C 과학 기반 경로에 부합하는지 독립적으로 평가한다. 검증된 기업은 SBTi 공개 목표 대시보드에 등재되고 연간 진행 공시 의무를 진다. SBTi는 부문별 지침을 발표하며, 교통 부문 지침(2021)은 항공사에 직접 적용되고, 건물·부동산 지침은 호텔을 다룬다. SBTi 약속 또는 검증 목표를 보유한 주요 여행 기업으로는 IAG, easyJet, Air France-KLM, 메리어트 인터내셔널, IHG 호텔 & 리조트, Booking Holdings 등이 있다. 기업 여행 구매자들은 Scope 3 카테고리 6 배출 관리의 일환으로 공급망 파트너에게 SBTi 약속 보유를 점점 더 요구한다.

# Distinctions
- **Science-Based Targets initiative (SBTi)** vs [CORSIA (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation)](/common/sustainability/corsia.md) — CORSIA is a binding intergovernmental offsetting obligation on airlines for growth in international-flight CO2; SBTi is a voluntary corporate framework that validates emission-reduction targets across all scopes for any company — airlines, hotels, or OTAs — rather than obligating operators to buy offset credits.
- **Science-Based Targets initiative (SBTi)** vs [Fly Net Zero 2050](/common/sustainability/fly-net-zero-2050.md) — Fly Net Zero 2050 is IATA's collective industry pledge for aviation to reach net-zero CO2 by 2050; an SBTi target is company-level, independently validated, and must demonstrate alignment to a defined 1.5 °C science pathway rather than simply declaring a target year.
- **Science-Based Targets initiative (SBTi)** vs [GHG Protocol Scope 3 (Business Travel)](/common/sustainability/ghg-protocol-scope-3-business-travel.md) — GHG Protocol Scope 3 Category 6 is the accounting methodology that quantifies a company's business-travel emissions; SBTi uses that emission inventory to assess whether the company's targets are declining fast enough to remain on a 1.5 °C pathway.
