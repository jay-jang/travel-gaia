> An approach to tourism that goes beyond reducing negative impacts to actively restoring and improving the ecological, social, and cultural health of a destination, aiming for a net-positive outcome for host communities and ecosystems.

Regenerative tourism is an evolution beyond the "do no harm" framing of sustainable tourism. Where sustainability seeks to maintain a destination in its current state, regeneration explicitly aims to leave it better off — richer biodiversity, stronger local economies, revitalised cultural traditions. Practically this means channelling tourism spend into local supply chains, involving host communities in product design and governance, restoring degraded habitats (e.g., coral-reef restoration dives, reforestation walks), and measuring impact not just through carbon footprint but through community well-being indicators. As of 2026 there is no single globally recognised certification or standard for "regenerative tourism"; the GSTC Criteria remain the primary verifiable baseline, and many regenerative operators seek GSTC-recognised certification as a foundation. The concept draws criticism for lack of measurable definition, but it has significant momentum with organisations such as the Regenerative Travel network and is referenced in GSTC guidance materials.

**한국어 / Korean** — **재생 관광(Regenerative Tourism)** — 관광이 목적지의 생태적·사회적·문화적 건강을 단순히 보전하는 데 그치지 않고 실질적으로 회복·향상시켜 순긍정적(net-positive) 결과를 이끌어내는 접근 방식. 재생 농업과 시스템 사고에서 착안해, 목적지를 관광 실천을 통해 회복될 수 있는 살아있는 시스템으로 바라본다.

재생 관광은 지속가능 관광의 "해를 끼치지 않는다"는 틀을 넘어선 진화형이다. 지속가능성이 목적지를 현 상태로 유지하려 한다면, 재생은 명시적으로 더 나은 상태—더 풍부한 생물 다양성, 강화된 지역 경제, 되살아난 문화 전통—를 지향한다. 실천적으로는 관광 지출을 지역 공급망으로 유입시키고, 호스트 커뮤니티가 상품 설계와 거버넌스에 참여하며, 훼손된 서식지(예: 산호초 복원 다이빙, 산림 복원 트레킹)를 회복하고, 영향을 탄소 발자국뿐 아니라 커뮤니티 웰빙 지표로도 측정한다. 2026년 현재 "재생 관광"에 대한 국제적으로 공인된 인증·표준은 없으며, GSTC 기준이 여전히 주요 검증 가능한 기준선이다.

# Distinctions
- **Regenerative Tourism** vs [GSTC Criteria](/common/sustainability/gstc-criteria.md) — GSTC Criteria are the internationally recognised baseline standards for what constitutes sustainable tourism practice, verifiable through third-party certification; Regenerative Tourism is a broader aspirational concept that builds on those standards, aiming for net-positive rather than merely net-neutral impact, and currently lacks an equivalent verifiable certification framework.
- **Regenerative Tourism** vs [Overtourism](/common/sustainability/overtourism.md) — Overtourism describes the negative impact on destinations when visitor numbers exceed sustainable capacity; Regenerative Tourism is a response philosophy that seeks to address and reverse such damage through redesigned tourism flows and community benefit.
