> Irregular Operations (IRROPS) is the airline industry term for any situation in which an airline's scheduled flights deviate materially from the published timetable due to unforeseeable or uncontrollable events—including severe weather, mechanical or technical failures, air traffic control constraints, crew unavailability, security incidents, or airport closures.

IRROPS encompasses a spectrum of disruptions: cancellations (flight does not operate at all), diversions (flight lands at an unplanned alternate airport), significant delays (typically 3+ hours), and ground stops imposed by ATC. Airlines distinguish between controllable IRROPS (carrier-caused, e.g., a maintenance issue) and uncontrollable IRROPS (extraordinary circumstances such as severe weather), because passenger-rights legislation—notably EU Regulation 261/2004, APPR in Canada, and DOT rules in the US—links compensation obligations to whether the disruption was controllable. The term is also used by ground handlers, airports, and travel management companies as a signal to activate IRROPS protocols: proactive rebooking, lounge access, hotel and meal vouchers, and real-time traveler communications.

**한국어 / Korean** — **비정상 운항(IRROPS)** — 항공사의 정기 항공편이 악천후, 기체 기술 결함, 항공 교통 관제 제한, 승무원 부족, 보안 사건, 공항 폐쇄 등 예측 불가능하거나 통제 불가능한 사건으로 인해 게재된 시간표에서 실질적으로 벗어나는 모든 상황을 가리키는 항공 업계 용어이다.

IRROPS는 취소(항공편 미운항), 회항, 상당한 지연, ATC 부과 그라운드 스톱 등 다양한 장애 유형을 포괄한다. 항공사는 조종 가능한 IRROPS(항공사 책임)와 조종 불가능한 IRROPS(특이한 상황)를 구분하는데, EU Regulation 261/2004, 캐나다 APPR, 미국 DOT 규정 등이 보상 지급 의무를 장애 통제 가능 여부에 연계하기 때문이다.

# Distinctions
- **Irregular Operations (IRROPS)** vs [Force Majeure](/common/disruption/force-majeure.md) — Force majeure is a legal concept that excuses a party from contractual obligations due to extraordinary events beyond their control; IRROPS is the operational term describing the disruption event itself, regardless of whether legal force majeure applies.
- **Irregular Operations (IRROPS)** vs [Schedule Change](/common/disruption/schedule-change.md) — A schedule change is typically a planned, advance modification to a flight's departure time, route, or equipment; IRROPS refers to unplanned, real-time disruptions that occur on or close to the day of operation.
- **Irregular Operations (IRROPS)** vs [Re-accommodation](/common/disruption/re-accommodation.md) — IRROPS is the triggering disruption event; re-accommodation is the carrier action taken in response to it—moving affected passengers to the next available flight or alternative transport.
