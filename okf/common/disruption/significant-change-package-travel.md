> A material alteration to any of the main characteristics of a package travel arrangement — such as destination, departure point, timing, standard of accommodation, or a price increase exceeding 8% — that obligates the organiser to notify the traveller and gives the traveller the right to accept the change, choose an alternative package, or terminate the contract and receive a full refund, as defined in Article 11 of EU Directive 2015/2302.

Under Article 11 of Directive 2015/2302, if the organiser is constrained to alter significantly any of the main characteristics of the travel services before the start of the package, the traveller may, within a reasonable period set by the organiser, (a) accept the proposed change or (b) terminate the contract without paying a termination fee and receive a full refund. The organiser must offer an alternative package of equivalent or higher quality where possible. Price increases of more than 8% of the total package price are treated as a significant change and trigger the same rights. 'Significant' is assessed in context — the Directive lists main characteristics as destination, itinerary, times of departure or return, duration, frequency, departure point, type or class of transport, accommodation, meals, and excursions included. Minor administrative adjustments do not qualify. In the UK, equivalent rights apply under The Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018 (SI 2018/634). The concept is operationally distinct from Extraordinary Circumstances, which allows the traveller (not the organiser) to terminate when the destination is significantly affected by unavoidable events.

**한국어 / Korean** — **중대한 변경(Significant Change, 패키지 여행)** — EU 지침 2015/2302(패키지 여행 지침) 제11조에서 규정하는 개념으로, 목적지·출발지·일정·숙박 등급 또는 총 가격의 8% 초과 인상 등 패키지 여행의 주요 특성에 대한 중요한 변경. 주최자는 여행자에게 통보해야 하며, 여행자는 변경 수락, 대안 패키지 선택, 또는 위약금 없는 전액 환급 중 하나를 선택할 권리를 갖는다.

EU 지침 2015/2302 제11조에 따라, 주최자가 패키지 출발 전 여행 서비스의 주요 특성을 중대하게 변경해야 하는 경우, 여행자는 주최자가 설정한 합리적인 기간 내에 (a) 제안된 변경을 수락하거나 (b) 위약금 없이 계약을 해지하고 전액 환급을 받을 수 있다. 주최자는 가능한 경우 동급 이상의 대안 패키지를 제시해야 한다. 총 패키지 가격의 8% 초과 가격 인상도 중대한 변경으로 간주되며 동일한 권리를 발생시킨다. '중대한' 여부는 맥락에 따라 판단하며, 지침은 주요 특성으로 목적지·여정·출발/귀환 시각·기간·빈도·출발지·운송 수단의 종류·등급·숙박·식사·포함된 투어를 열거한다. 소소한 행정 조정은 해당하지 않는다. 영국에서는 Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018(SI 2018/634)에 따라 동등한 권리가 적용된다.

# Distinctions
- **Significant Change (Package Travel)** vs [Extraordinary Circumstances](/common/disruption/extraordinary-circumstances.md) — A Significant Change is a unilateral alteration made by the organiser to the package before departure, giving the traveller the right to terminate; Extraordinary Circumstances is an event at or near the destination (war, natural disaster) that allows the traveller to cancel before departure without penalty — in that case neither party is responsible for the disruption.
- **Significant Change (Package Travel)** vs [Package Travel Directive](/common/disruption/package-travel-directive.md) — The Package Travel Directive is the overarching EU regulation governing package holidays; Significant Change is one specific right-triggering concept defined within Article 11 of that Directive, dealing with pre-departure alterations the organiser makes to the package.
