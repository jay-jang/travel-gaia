> Bleisure (a portmanteau of "business" and "leisure") describes the practice of combining corporate travel with personal leisure time — typically by extending a business trip for sightseeing, recreation, or family visits before or after the work obligation. Recognised by GBTA (Global Business Travel Association) as a significant and growing travel segment, bleisure reflects the blurring of work-life boundaries in the modern workforce and presents a cross-selling opportunity for airlines, hotels, and destination marketers.

Bleisure travellers represent a high-value cross-segment for multiple travel suppliers. Airlines benefit from leisure-fare extensions tacked on to business-itinerary base tickets; hotels capture longer average stays and additional leisure spend (spa, restaurant, local tours) from guests already on property under a corporate rate. GBTA research (2018) found that a substantial proportion of business travellers extend at least some trips for personal leisure, particularly on international itineraries. Indicators of a bleisure trip include: (a) the traveller brings a partner, spouse, or family member; (b) the stay length exceeds the minimum required by the business obligation; (c) leisure activities or extra hotel nights are paid personally rather than expensed. The trend intensified post-2020 as remote-work policies increased schedule flexibility and the concept of the "workcation" — working remotely from a vacation destination — emerged as an adjacent behaviour.

**한국어 / Korean** — **블레저(Bleisure)** — 블레저(Bleisure)는 "business"와 "leisure"의 합성어로, 법인 출장에 개인 여가 시간을 결합하는 관행을 뜻한다. 일반적으로 업무 일정 전후로 출장지에서 관광·레크리에이션·가족 방문을 위해 체류를 연장하는 형태다. GBTA가 중요한 성장 세그먼트로 인정한 블레저는 현대 직장에서 일과 생활의 경계가 흐려지는 추세를 반영하며, 항공사·호텔·목적지 마케터에게 교차 판매 기회를 제공한다.

블레저 여행자는 복수의 여행 공급자에게 고가치 교차 세그먼트다. 2020년 이후 원격 근무 정책으로 일정 유연성이 높아지면서 이 추세가 심화됐으며, 휴가지에서 원격 근무하는 "워케이션" 개념도 인접 행태로 등장했다.

# Distinctions
- **Bleisure** vs [Corporate Profile](/common/customer/corporate-profile.md) — A Corporate Profile records a traveller's corporate preferences, policies, and identifiers in the booking system; bleisure is the travel behaviour of extending that corporate trip for personal leisure — a behaviour pattern rather than a data record.
- **Bleisure** vs [Travel Management Company](/common/customer/travel-management-company.md) — A TMC manages corporate travel bookings and policy compliance on behalf of a company; bleisure trips often blur TMC oversight — the business segment may be booked through the TMC under a corporate policy, but leisure extensions are typically arranged independently and paid from personal funds.
