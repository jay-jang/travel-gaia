> A formal corporate or institutional document that establishes the rules, booking channels, preferred suppliers, authorization workflows, spending limits, and reimbursement criteria for employee business travel. It is enforced through the configuration of online booking tools, travel management company briefings, and expense management systems, and directly governs negotiated rate access, Duty of Care obligations, and data capture for travel spend reporting.

A travel policy typically covers: who may travel on company account, which booking channels are permitted (OBT, TMC, direct), preferred airline and hotel programs, cabin/room type entitlements by trip length or seniority, advance purchase requirements, per diem allowances, and exception approval workflows. GBTA research consistently shows that well-designed travel policies achieve 10–20% cost savings versus unmanaged travel through preferred-supplier leverage and booking compliance. Compliance monitoring is embedded in the OBT — out-of-policy bookings trigger warnings or require manager approval — and TMC data feeds produce management information (MI) reports showing policy adherence by traveler and department. Travel policies are increasingly incorporating sustainability requirements (preferred carriers with SAF commitments, carbon budgets per trip) and Duty of Care provisions (location tracking, crisis communication protocols).

**한국어 / Korean** — **출장 정책(Travel Policy)** — 임직원의 비즈니스 출장에 관한 규정·예약 채널·선호 공급자·승인 절차·지출 한도·비용 정산 기준을 규정한 공식 기업 또는 기관 문서. 온라인 예약 도구(OBT) 설정, 여행 관리 회사(TMC) 브리핑, 경비 관리 시스템을 통해 집행되며, 협의 요금 접근권·안전 배려 의무(Duty of Care)·출장비 데이터 보고에 직접적인 영향을 미친다.

출장 정책은 통상 출장 자격자, 허용 예약 채널, 선호 항공사·호텔 프로그램, 직급별 좌석/객실 등급 기준, 조기 구매 요건, 일비 수당, 예외 승인 절차 등을 규정한다. GBTA 연구에 따르면 잘 설계된 출장 정책은 선호 공급자 활용과 예약 준수를 통해 비관리 출장 대비 10–20%의 비용 절감을 달성한다. OBT에 준수 여부 모니터링이 내장되어 정책 외 예약은 경고를 유발하거나 상위자 승인을 요구한다.

# Distinctions
- **Travel Policy** vs Duty of Care — Duty of Care is the legal and ethical obligation owed to traveling employees; a travel policy is the operational document through which Duty of Care and other obligations are implemented, documented, and monitored.
- **Travel Policy** vs Per Diem — Per diem is a specific financial allowance; a travel policy is the comprehensive governance document that sets the per diem amount and all other rules governing corporate travel.
- **Travel Policy** vs Online Booking Tool — An OBT is the technology platform used to book travel; a travel policy is the rule set loaded into (and enforced by) the OBT to ensure compliant bookings.
