> A Digital Nomad Visa (also called a Remote Work Visa) is a special-category long-stay permit that allows remote workers and self-employed individuals to reside legally in a host country for an extended period — typically one to two years, often renewable — while working exclusively for employers or clients based outside that country. It is distinct from a tourist visa (which prohibits work) and from a conventional work visa (which ties the holder to a local employer).

As remote work proliferated post-2020, more than 65 countries launched dedicated digital nomad or remote-work visa programs by 2025. Prominent examples include Estonia's Digital Nomad Visa (launched 2020), Portugal's D8 Visa (2022), Spain's Digital Nomad Visa (2023), Thailand's Long-Term Resident (LTR) Visa, Barbados's Welcome Stamp, and Croatia's Digital Nomad Residence Permit. Common eligibility criteria include proof of remote employment or self-employment, minimum income thresholds (typically USD 2,000–3,500 per month), health insurance coverage, and a criminal background check.

For travel professionals and tourism authorities, the digital nomad visa class represents a distinct and economically significant traveler segment: nomads typically stay longer than tourists, spend more per visit, and often choose secondary destinations outside peak seasons.

**한국어 / Korean** — **디지털 노마드 비자(Digital Nomad Visa)** — 디지털 노마드 비자(원격 근무 비자라고도 함)는 원격 근로자 및 자영업자가 해당 국가 이외의 고용주나 고객을 위해서만 근무하면서 입국국에서 장기간(일반적으로 1~2년, 갱신 가능) 합법적으로 거주할 수 있도록 허용하는 특수 장기체류 허가증이다.

2020년 이후 원격 근무의 확산으로 2025년까지 65개 이상의 국가가 디지털 노마드 또는 원격 근무 전용 비자 프로그램을 도입했다. 주요 사례: 에스토니아 디지털 노마드 비자(2020), 포르투갈 D8 비자(2022), 스페인 디지털 노마드 비자(2023), 태국 장기거주자(LTR) 비자, 바베이도스 Welcome Stamp, 크로아티아 디지털 노마드 거주 허가증.

# Distinctions
- **Digital Nomad Visa** vs [eVisa](/common/customer/e-visa.md) — An eVisa is a short-stay electronic travel authorisation typically valid for 30–90 days and issued for tourism or business; a Digital Nomad Visa is a longer-stay permit specifically for remote workers, usually valid for one to two years, and allows the holder to work while residing in the country.
- **Digital Nomad Visa** vs [ETIAS](/common/customer/etias.md) — ETIAS is a pre-travel security screening authorisation for visa-exempt travellers entering the Schengen Area, with no entitlement to work or extended residency; a Digital Nomad Visa is a dedicated residency permit that explicitly authorises long-stay remote work and is processed via a full visa application, not an automated pre-screen.
