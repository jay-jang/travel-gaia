> A visa on arrival (VoA) is a visa issued by immigration officers at the port of entry — typically an international airport — to qualifying foreign nationals upon their physical arrival, without requiring advance application through a consulate or embassy. The traveler pays a fee and presents their passport and supporting documents at the immigration counter; if approved, a visa sticker or stamp is placed in the passport granting the permitted stay.

Visa-on-arrival policies are sovereign decisions; eligibility varies by destination country and traveler nationality, and conditions (permitted stay duration, permitted activities, fees, and eligible ports of entry) are set unilaterally by the issuing state. Countries offering widely used visa-on-arrival programs include Thailand, Maldives, Nepal, Indonesia, and Egypt, among others. A visa on arrival differs from an eVisa in that it is issued physically at the border rather than digitally before travel; it differs from visa-free entry in that it requires payment of a fee and documentary review at the counter. IATA TIMATIC is the authoritative tool used by airlines and travel advisors to determine whether a given nationality requires a visa in advance or is eligible for a visa on arrival at the specific destination. Because eligibility can change without notice and arrival-counter processing takes time, airlines and travel advisors recommend confirming TIMATIC data close to travel and arriving at the airport with sufficient time before the flight.

**한국어 / Korean** — **도착 비자(Visa on Arrival)** — 도착 비자(Visa on Arrival, VoA)는 외국 국민이 영사관이나 대사관을 통한 사전 신청 없이 도착하는 국제 공항 등 입국 항구에서 출입국 심사관이 발급하는 비자다. 여행객은 수수료를 납부하고 여권과 지원 서류를 출입국 심사 카운터에 제시하며, 승인 시 허가된 체류를 부여하는 비자 스티커나 스탬프가 여권에 부착된다.

도착 비자 정책은 주권적 결정이며, 자격은 목적지 국가와 여행자 국적에 따라 다르다. IATA TIMATIC은 항공사와 여행 어드바이저가 특정 국적의 여행자가 목적지에서 사전 비자가 필요한지 또는 도착 비자 자격이 있는지 확인하는 데 사용하는 권위 있는 도구다.

# Distinctions
- **Visa on Arrival** vs [eVisa](/common/customer/e-visa.md) — An eVisa is issued electronically by the destination government before the traveler departs — the traveler applies online, receives a digital authorization, and arrives with permission already granted; a visa on arrival is issued at the border upon arrival, requiring the traveler to queue at an immigration counter and pay a fee on the spot. Both result in a visa, but the timing, channel, and process differ. Some governments are replacing visa-on-arrival programs with eVisa systems to pre-screen travelers before they board.
- **Visa on Arrival** vs [TIMATIC](/common/customer/timatic.md) — TIMATIC is the IATA database that airlines and agents consult to verify whether a traveler's nationality requires a visa in advance, qualifies for a visa on arrival, or may enter visa-free for a given destination; a visa on arrival is the specific entry authorization mechanism that TIMATIC may indicate as applicable.
