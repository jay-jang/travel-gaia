> A fixed daily allowance provided by a company, government agency, or institution to cover an employee's lodging, meal, and incidental expenses while traveling on business. Per diem rates are set by reference to official government tables (such as US GSA rates or UK HMRC scale rates) or internal corporate travel policy, and are used by travel management companies and online booking tools to enforce spending limits.

Per diem (Latin: "per day") is the cornerstone of corporate expense management. It may cover lodging only, meals and incidentals only (M&IE), or both. In the United States, the GSA publishes per diem rates by location for federal employees; private-sector employers often adopt GSA rates as benchmarks. In the EU, individual member-state governments and the European Commission publish their own per diem scales. GBTA research indicates that per diem policies directly influence traveler behavior, booking pace, and overall managed travel compliance. When a per diem rate is set below market accommodation cost it may drive travelers outside the preferred booking channel, creating leakage and reducing Duty of Care visibility. Per diem differs from actual-expense reimbursement in that it is a flat allowance regardless of actual spend, simplifying accounting and reducing administrative overhead.

**한국어 / Korean** — **출장 일비(Per Diem)** — 비즈니스 출장 중 직원의 숙박·식사·잡비를 충당하기 위해 회사·정부기관·기관이 지급하는 일 정액 수당. 미국 GSA 요율이나 영국 HMRC 기준금액 등 공식 정부 요율표 또는 내부 출장 정책에 따라 요율이 정해지며, 여행 관리 회사(TMC)와 온라인 예약 도구(OBT)가 지출 한도를 집행하는 데 활용된다.

Per diem(라틴어: "하루당")은 기업 비용 관리의 핵심이다. 일비는 숙박비만, 식비 및 잡비(M&IE)만, 또는 둘 다를 포함할 수 있다. 미국 GSA가 연방 직원을 위해 지역별 요율을 공시하며, 민간 기업들은 흔히 이를 기준으로 삼는다. 일비가 시장 숙박비보다 낮게 설정되면 여행자가 선호 예약 채널 밖에서 예약하도록 유도하여 Duty of Care 가시성을 저하시킬 수 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Per Diem** vs Travel Policy — A travel policy is the governing document that defines all rules for business travel, including per diem limits; per diem is a specific allowance mechanism that a travel policy typically quantifies and mandates.
- **Per Diem** vs Duty of Care — Duty of Care is the employer's legal and ethical obligation to ensure traveler safety; per diem is a financial instrument within the travel policy. Under-funded per diem rates can undermine Duty of Care by pushing travelers to unapproved accommodation outside safety-vetted channels.
