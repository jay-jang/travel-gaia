> Car hire excess insurance is a third-party insurance product that reimburses the excess (deductible) a traveler would otherwise pay out of pocket when making a damage or theft claim under a rental car CDW or LDW policy. Sold by specialist brokers and travel insurers independently of the rental counter, it is typically far cheaper than the excess-waiver product offered by the car hire company and can cover a policyholder across multiple rentals during a trip or annual policy period.

When a traveler rents a car, the standard CDW/LDW reduces or eliminates liability for vehicle damage but almost always retains an excess (a first-loss amount, typically £1,000–£3,000 in Europe). The rental company sells an excess waiver at the counter that eliminates this excess, but counter prices are high (£10–£25/day is common). Car hire excess insurance — sold in advance online by specialists such as iCarhireinsurance, RentalCover, and Questor Insurance — steps in to pay that excess after the traveler has paid it to the rental company. Because the product is activated after the rental company settles, it does not change the rental agreement; the traveler still pays the excess to the rental company and then claims it back from the insurer. Annual multi-trip policies covering unlimited rentals are widely available and especially cost-effective for frequent travelers.

**한국어 / Korean** — **렌터카 초과 보험(Car Hire Excess Insurance)** — 렌터카 초과 보험은 렌터카 CDW 또는 LDW 보험으로 손상·도난 청구를 할 때 여행자가 직접 부담해야 하는 초과 공제액을 보상하는 제3자 보험 상품이다. 렌터카 카운터에서 판매하는 면제 상품과 무관하게 전문 브로커나 여행 보험사가 별도로 판매하며, 통상 렌터카 회사의 면제 상품보다 훨씬 저렴하다.

# Distinctions
- **Car Hire Excess Insurance** vs [Excess Waiver](/ground/car/excess-waiver.md) — An excess waiver is a product sold by the car rental company at the counter that eliminates the excess on its own CDW/LDW policy; car hire excess insurance is a third-party insurance product purchased independently that reimburses the excess after the traveler has paid it. The waiver changes the rental agreement; the insurance does not.
- **Car Hire Excess Insurance** vs [Collision Damage Waiver](/ground/car/collision-damage-waiver.md) — CDW is the primary damage product on the rental contract that reduces the traveler's liability for vehicle damage (but retains an excess); car hire excess insurance is a supplementary third-party product that covers that retained excess.
