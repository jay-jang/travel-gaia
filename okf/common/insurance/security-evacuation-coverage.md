> Security evacuation coverage is a travel-insurance benefit that pays for emergency transportation to a safe location when a traveler's destination becomes unsafe due to political unrest, civil disturbance, civil war, coup d'état, riots, terrorism, or a government-issued evacuation order. It is distinct from emergency medical evacuation, which responds to health emergencies, not security or political threats.

Security evacuation coverage (also called political evacuation, political risk evacuation, or security assistance) is triggered by a formal security event rather than a medical one. The insurer or an affiliated assistance company maintains a 24-hour Security Operations Center that monitors government travel advisories (such as those issued by the US Department of State or the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office), intelligence alerts, and on-the-ground conditions. When the threat level reaches the policy threshold — typically a Level 3 or Level 4 US State Department Advisory, or an equivalent formal evacuation order — the benefit is activated and the insurer arranges and pays for the traveler's transport out of the affected area to the nearest safe haven or home country. Coverage limits and triggers vary widely by policy; some policies require that the government of the traveler's home country issue a formal evacuation order, while others activate on insurer discretion or named-peril definitions.

안전 대피 보장(정치 대피, 정치 위험 대피 또는 보안 지원이라고도 함)은 의료 상황이 아닌 공식 보안 사건에 의해 발동된다. 보험사 또는 제휴 지원 회사는 24시간 보안 운영 센터를 운영하여 정부 여행 경보(미국 국무부, 영국 FCDO 등 발행), 정보 경고, 현지 상황을 모니터링한다. 위험 등급이 약관 기준점에 도달하면(일반적으로 미국 국무부 레벨 3 또는 4, 또는 이에 상응하는 공식 대피 명령), 보장이 발동되어 보험사가 여행자의 영향 지역 이탈 및 가장 가까운 안전한 장소나 본국으로의 교통편을 수배·지급한다. 보장 한도와 발동 요건은 약관마다 크게 다르며, 일부 약관은 여행자의 본국 정부 공식 대피 명령을 요구하고, 다른 약관은 보험사 재량이나 열거 위험 정의에 따라 발동된다.

# Distinctions
- **Security Evacuation Coverage** vs [Emergency Medical Evacuation](/common/insurance/emergency-medical-evacuation.md) — Emergency medical evacuation is triggered by a sudden and unforeseen medical illness or injury requiring transportation to an appropriate medical facility; security evacuation coverage is triggered by political, civil, or security threats to the traveler's safety — the two are entirely separate perils addressing different risks, and most insurers price and include them as distinct benefits.
- **Security Evacuation Coverage** vs [Travel Advisory](/common/disruption/travel-advisory.md) — A travel advisory is a government-issued risk assessment warning travellers about conditions in a destination; security evacuation coverage is the insurance benefit that may be activated by a sufficiently severe advisory — the advisory is the trigger condition, not the benefit itself.
- **Security Evacuation Coverage** vs [Comprehensive Travel Insurance](/common/insurance/comprehensive-travel-insurance.md) — Comprehensive travel insurance is a multi-benefit policy bundling trip cancellation, medical, baggage, and often evacuation coverages; security evacuation coverage is one specific benefit within such a policy, and may also be purchased as a standalone endorsement or through dedicated security assistance services.
