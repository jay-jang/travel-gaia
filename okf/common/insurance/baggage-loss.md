> A travel-insurance benefit that compensates a traveller for checked or carry-on baggage that is permanently lost, stolen, or irreparably damaged in the custody of a common carrier or while otherwise in transit during a covered trip. Unlike Baggage Delay — which reimburses the cost of essential replacement items during a temporary absence — Baggage Loss pays an indemnity against the permanent deprivation of the bags themselves, subject to the policy's overall limit and any per-item or per-category sub-limits.

Coverage typically includes all personal belongings inside the bags except commonly excluded items such as cash, passports, jewellery, electronics, sporting equipment, and items already insured elsewhere. Claims require documentation: a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) filed with the carrier at the airport, the carrier's written confirmation of permanent loss (usually issued 21–30 days after a delay), a police report if theft is alleged, and an itemised inventory with estimated values and purchase dates. Airline liability is capped under the Montreal Convention 1999 (Articles 17 and 22) at an SDR-denominated limit per passenger; travel insurance baggage-loss coverage is typically secondary and pays the excess above what the carrier has already settled. Sub-limits often apply by item category: electronics at one cap, jewellery and watches at another, pairs and sets of items may count as a single item. High-value items frequently require scheduled-item riders for adequate coverage. The IATA WorldTracer system tracks delayed bags; once WorldTracer status shifts to "lost", it typically triggers the conversion from a Baggage Delay claim to a Baggage Loss claim.

**한국어 / Korean** — **수하물 분실 보장(Baggage Loss)** — 위탁 수하물 또는 휴대 수하물이 운송 중 운송인의 관리 하에 또는 여행 중 영구적으로 분실·도난·수리 불가능하게 파손된 경우 여행자에게 보상하는 여행보험 보장이다. 수하물이 일시적으로 없는 동안 필수 대체품 구입비를 보상하는 수하물 지연(Baggage Delay)과 달리, 수하물 분실은 가방 자체의 영구적 박탈에 대해 보험증권 전체 한도 및 물품별·분류별 하한도 범위 내에서 보상금을 지급한다.

Baggage Loss 보장은 현금·여권·귀금속·전자기기·스포츠 장비 등 통상 면책되는 물품을 제외한 수하물 내 모든 개인 소지품을 포함한다. 클레임 제기에는 공항에서 운송인에게 접수한 PIR(Property Irregularity Report), 운송인의 영구 분실 확인서(보통 지연 후 21~30일 이내 발행), 도난 주장 시 경찰 신고서, 추정 가치 및 구입일이 기재된 물품 목록이 필요하다. 항공사 책임은 몬트리올 협약 1999(제17조·제22조)에 따라 승객당 SDR 한도로 제한되며, 여행보험의 수하물 분실 보장은 보통 secondary(2차)로 운송인 정산액 초과분만 지급한다. IATA WorldTracer 시스템이 수하물 상태를 "lost(분실)"로 변경하면 통상 수하물 지연 클레임에서 수하물 분실 클레임으로 전환된다.

# Distinctions
- **Baggage Loss** vs [Baggage Delay](/common/insurance/baggage-delay.md) — Baggage Delay reimburses the cost of essential items (clothing, toiletries) purchased while your bags are temporarily missing and still in transit; Baggage Loss pays compensation for bags that are permanently gone — stolen, destroyed, or never recovered. Most policies convert a Delay claim into a Loss claim once the carrier formally declares the bag permanently lost, typically 21 days or more after the mismatch date.
- **Baggage Loss** vs [Property Irregularity Report](/common/disruption/property-irregularity-report.md) — A Property Irregularity Report (PIR) is the carrier's official record of the mishandled baggage incident filed at the airport; it is the required supporting document for a Baggage Loss insurance claim, not the insurance benefit itself.
- **Baggage Loss** vs [Trip Cancellation](/common/insurance/trip-cancellation.md) — Trip Cancellation reimburses pre-paid, non-refundable trip costs when a covered reason prevents you from departing; Baggage Loss compensates for tangible property destroyed or permanently lost during travel. They are triggered by entirely different events.
