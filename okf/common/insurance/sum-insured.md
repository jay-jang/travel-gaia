> The maximum amount an insurer will pay for losses covered under a travel insurance policy, per benefit category. No claim can exceed the stated limit for its category regardless of the actual loss incurred. US markets use "benefit amount" or "coverage limit"; UK and international markets prefer "sum insured".

The sum insured is fixed at purchase and stated on the policy schedule. Most comprehensive travel insurance products carry multiple sums insured — the emergency medical expenses limit is commonly the largest (e.g. USD 500,000 to USD 5,000,000, or "unlimited" on premium products), while trip cancellation, trip delay, and baggage limits are typically much lower. The insurer pays claims up to but not exceeding the applicable per-category limit; any loss above the limit falls on the policyholder. Many travel policies also carry per-item sublimits within a benefit (e.g. electronics capped at USD 500 within an overall USD 2,000 baggage limit), further constraining payouts on individual high-value items.

**한국어 / Korean** — **보험 가입 금액(Sum Insured)** — 여행자보험 증권 하에 보험사가 보장 카테고리별로 지급할 최대 금액. 실제 손실 규모에 관계없이 각 카테고리의 명시 한도를 초과하여 청구할 수 없다. 미국 시장은 "benefit amount" 또는 "coverage limit"을, 영국·국제 시장은 "sum insured"를 선호한다.

보험 가입 금액은 가입 시 확정되어 보험증권 일람표에 명시된다. 비상 의료비 한도가 가장 크고(USD 50만~500만 또는 무제한), 여행 취소·지연·수하물 한도는 훨씬 낮다. 보험사는 카테고리별 한도까지만 지급하며, 초과 손실은 피보험자가 부담한다.

# Distinctions
- **Sum Insured** vs [Comprehensive Travel Insurance](/common/insurance/comprehensive-travel-insurance.md) — Comprehensive travel insurance is the product — a bundle of multiple benefit categories sold as a single policy; the sum insured is a per-category parameter within that product defining the maximum payout for each individual benefit.
