> Curtailment is a travel insurance benefit that reimburses a policyholder for the unused, non-refundable portion of a prepaid trip plus any additional transport costs incurred to return home earlier than planned, when the early return is caused by a covered emergency such as the serious illness or death of the insured or a close relative, a home emergency, or another listed peril. It is the predominant UK and EU term for what North American policies call trip interruption.

A curtailment claim arises after the trip has begun — the traveler must actually have departed before the early return occurs. Covered reasons typically include: the serious injury or illness of the insured or a traveling companion requiring return home; the death of a close relative; a burglary or serious damage to the insured's home; or a summons for jury service. The benefit pays proportionally for the unused portion of accommodation, excursions, and other pre-booked non-refundable elements, and additionally covers the cost of alternative return transport if the original ticket cannot be used. UK and EU insurers are required to describe curtailment cover in the Insurance Product Information Document (IPID) in plain language under FCA / Insurance Distribution Directive rules. The term "curtailment" is used in British English; North American and global travel-insurance policies use "trip interruption" for essentially the same benefit.

# Distinctions
- **Curtailment** vs [Trip Interruption](/common/insurance/trip-interruption.md) — Curtailment and trip interruption describe the same core benefit — reimbursement for early return from a trip due to a covered emergency — but in different markets: curtailment is the standard UK/EU term; trip interruption is the standard North American term. Some policy wordings use both, or use trip interruption globally, while UK-specific policies invariably use curtailment.
- **Curtailment** vs [Trip Cancellation](/common/insurance/trip-cancellation.md) — Trip cancellation applies before the trip starts — the traveler never departs and claims for the full non-refundable trip cost; curtailment applies after the trip has started, covering only the unused portion plus extra return travel costs. The triggering peril can be the same, but the timing and claim calculation differ fundamentally.
- **Curtailment** vs [Emergency Assistance](/common/insurance/emergency-assistance.md) — Emergency assistance is a service — the 24/7 helpline and logistical support (evacuation, hospital location, repatriation coordination) — that a travel insurer or assistance company provides; curtailment is an insurance benefit — a cash indemnity for the costs the traveler bears when returning early. They are complementary: the assistance service helps arrange the return, and the curtailment benefit reimburses the cost.
