> The European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) is a free, standardised card issued by the public health or social security authority of each EU/EEA member state and Switzerland that entitles the cardholder to access state-provided medical treatment under the same conditions and at the same cost as residents, when travelling or temporarily residing in another participating country. It covers medically necessary care but does not substitute for private travel insurance, does not cover repatriation, and does not apply to private healthcare providers.

The EHIC was introduced across the EU in 2004 (replacing the E111 form), standardising the administrative procedures for accessing cross-border healthcare. It is valid in all 27 EU member states, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and Switzerland. Eligibility: any person insured under or covered by a social security or public health insurance system of a participating country. The EHIC is issued free of charge by the national health authority (e.g., CPAM in France, Krankenkassen in Germany) and is valid for 2–5 years. Coverage scope: medically necessary treatment during a temporary stay (including for pre-existing conditions and pregnancy); the traveller pays the same co-payments as local residents; emergency treatment and routine care for chronic conditions. The EHIC does not cover planned (elective) treatment abroad, private healthcare, repatriation costs, or costs exceeding what local residents would pay. Following Brexit, UK residents apply instead for the UK Global Health Insurance Card (GHIC), which provides equivalent rights in EU countries. The EHIC celebrated its 20th anniversary in December 2024, having been used by hundreds of millions of European travellers.

**한국어 / Korean** — **유럽 건강 보험 카드(EHIC)** — EHIC는 EU/EEA 회원국 및 스위스의 공공 건강 당국이 발급하는 무료 표준화 카드로, 소지자가 다른 참여 국가에서 현지 주민과 동일한 조건과 비용으로 국가 제공 의료 서비스를 이용할 수 있는 권리를 부여한다. 2004년 도입되어 EU 27개 회원국, 아이슬란드, 리히텐슈타인, 노르웨이, 스위스에서 유효하다.

민간 여행자 보험을 대체하지 않으며, 송환이나 민간 의료 기관에는 적용되지 않는다. Brexit 이후 영국 거주자는 EU 국가에서 동등한 권리를 제공하는 영국 GHIC(Global Health Insurance Card)를 신청한다. 2024년 12월 20주년을 맞이했다.

# Distinctions
- **European Health Insurance Card** vs [Travel Medical Insurance](/common/insurance/travel-medical-insurance.md) — Travel Medical Insurance is a private insurance product purchased for a fee that covers emergency and sometimes routine medical expenses abroad, typically including repatriation, private hospital treatment, and costs exceeding what state systems cover. The EHIC is a free public entitlement card that only covers access to state-provided healthcare at local-resident rates; it does not cover repatriation, private care, or costs that locals would pay personally. Travel insurers universally advise carrying an EHIC in addition to a travel insurance policy, not as a replacement.
- **European Health Insurance Card** vs [Comprehensive Travel Insurance](/common/insurance/comprehensive-travel-insurance.md) — Comprehensive Travel Insurance is a commercial product covering a wide set of risks including trip cancellation, baggage loss, flight delay, medical emergencies, repatriation, and personal liability. The EHIC covers only state healthcare access in EU/EEA/Switzerland at no additional cost; it does not cover any of the other travel risks a comprehensive policy addresses, and its medical coverage is limited to what local residents receive through public systems.
