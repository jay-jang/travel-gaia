> A structured visit by a professional travel buyer — travel agent, tour operator, DMC, or corporate travel manager — to inspect accommodation, destination infrastructure, or supplier services near the point of contracting, allowing the buyer to verify quality and suitability before committing to purchase or contract. Also called a site inspection or site visit.

Unlike a FAM trip — which is a broader, group-based familiarisation experience designed to promote a destination to a wider trade audience — an inspection trip is a focused visit arranged by a specific supplier or DMC for a buyer who is in active contracting discussions. The buyer evaluates bedrooms, meeting rooms, food and beverage outlets, accessibility, transfer logistics, and overall product suitability with the specific intent of deciding whether to recommend, contract, or include the property in an itinerary. The cost may be borne partly or wholly by the host supplier. Fáilte Ireland and other national tourism organisations define site visits/inspections as the mechanism for Professional Conference Organisers (PCOs), tour operators, and corporate buyers to inspect potential venues and suppliers prior to committing. Site inspection reports are often formalised in a written evaluation shared with sales managers.

**한국어 / Korean** — **인스펙션 트립(Inspection Trip)** — 여행사, 투어 오퍼레이터, DMC 또는 법인 여행 담당자 등 전문 구매자가 계약 체결 전에 숙박 시설, 목적지 인프라, 공급업체 서비스를 직접 방문하여 품질과 적합성을 검증하는 구조화된 현장 방문. 현장 조사(Site Inspection) 또는 현장 방문(Site Visit)이라고도 한다.

포괄적이고 그룹 중심으로 목적지를 더 넓은 여행업 관계자에게 홍보하는 팸 트립과 달리, 인스펙션 트립은 계약 논의가 진행 중인 특정 구매자를 위해 특정 공급업체나 DMC가 주선하는 집중적인 방문이다. 구매자는 객실·회의실·식음료 시설·접근성·이동 물류 및 전반적인 상품 적합성을 평가하며, 해당 시설을 추천하거나 계약하거나 일정에 포함할지 여부를 결정하기 위한 목적으로 진행된다. 비용은 호스트 공급업체가 부분 또는 전액 부담하는 경우가 많다.

# Distinctions
- **Inspection Trip** vs [FAM Trip](/tours/tour-ops/fam-trip.md) — A FAM trip is a complimentary or discounted group trip designed to familiarise a broad group of travel advisors or journalists with a destination; an inspection trip is a targeted, buyer-initiated visit to a specific property or supplier — typically one-on-one or very small group — conducted because a purchase or contract decision is imminent.
- **Inspection Trip** vs [Receptive Tour Operator](/tours/tour-ops/receptive-tour-operator.md) — A receptive tour operator is the in-destination supplier that receives and services incoming groups; an inspection trip is the pre-contract process by which a buying tour operator or travel manager visits and evaluates that receptive operator before signing a contract.
