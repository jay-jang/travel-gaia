> A Series Tour is a multi-day tour itinerary that a tour operator schedules and repeats on multiple fixed departure dates throughout a season or year. Each departure follows the same predetermined route, uses the same hotels and services, and is sold as a packaged product with a fixed price. Series tours are the backbone of mass-market coach touring and enable tour operators to achieve economies of scale in purchasing and contracting.

A series tour is distinguished from a one-off custom or chartered tour by its repeating schedule: the operator contracts with hotels and local suppliers for a season's worth of departures (e.g., "every Saturday from April to October"), securing block space and confidential rates based on volume. Individual passengers book onto a departure date within the series, and the operator fills each date independently. When a departure date fails to attract enough passengers to cover costs, the operator may cancel it or "double up" passengers into adjacent dates. When a departure date meets the minimum passenger requirement, it becomes a Guaranteed Departure. Series tours are distributed through travel agents and direct channels, typically appearing in a printed or digital brochure cataloguing all departure dates, prices by date (seasonal pricing), and the inclusions.

**한국어 / Korean** — **시리즈 투어(고정 출발 일정 패키지)** — 시리즈 투어(Series Tour)는 투어 운영사가 시즌 또는 연중 여러 고정 출발 날짜에 반복 운행하도록 일정을 잡은 다일제 투어 상품이다. 각 출발편은 동일한 미리 정해진 경로를 따르고, 동일한 호텔과 서비스를 이용하며, 고정 요금의 패키지 상품으로 판매된다.

시리즈 투어는 반복되는 일정으로 인해 일회성 맞춤형 또는 차터 투어와 구분된다. 운영사는 시즌 단위 출발편을 위해 호텔 및 현지 공급업체와 계약을 체결하고, 물량 기반의 블록 객실 및 기밀 요금을 확보한다. 개별 승객은 시리즈 내 특정 출발 날짜에 예약하며, 운영사는 각 날짜를 독립적으로 채운다. 최소 승객 요건을 충족한 출발편은 확정 출발(Guaranteed Departure)이 된다. 시리즈 투어는 여행사 및 직판 채널을 통해 유통되며, 일반적으로 모든 출발 날짜·날짜별 요금·포함 사항을 목록화한 브로슈어에 등장한다.

**Aliases:** `Series Departure`, `Scheduled Series`, `Brochure Tour`, `Fixed-Departure Tour`, `Catalogued Tour`

# Distinctions
- **Series Tour** vs [Guaranteed Departure](/tours/tour-ops/guaranteed-departure.md) — A Guaranteed Departure is a departure date within a series that has reached its minimum passenger threshold and is confirmed to operate; a Series Tour is the broader repeating-itinerary product that produces those departure dates. Not all departure dates in a series become guaranteed — some may be cancelled or consolidated.
- **Series Tour** vs [Escorted Tour](/tours/tour-product/escorted-tour.md) — An Escorted Tour is a tour type where a dedicated tour director accompanies the group throughout the journey; a Series Tour describes the scheduling model (repeating departures on fixed dates). A series tour may or may not be escorted — many series are escorted, but small-group or self-guided series exist as well.
