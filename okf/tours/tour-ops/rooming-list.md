> A rooming list is the formal document prepared by a tour operator, travel agent, or group organiser and delivered to a hotel (or series of hotels) that specifies the name, room type, arrival/departure dates, and any special requirements for each guest in a group booking. It converts a group block or allotment into individual room assignments and serves as the operational handover between the seller and the accommodation supplier.

A rooming list is typically submitted to the hotel by a contractual deadline (often 30–7 days before arrival) stipulated in the group contract; late submission may result in the hotel releasing unconfirmed rooms or charging cancellation fees on a per-room basis. A complete rooming list includes: full guest names (matching passport or ID), room type per guest (single, double, twin, triple, suite), bed configuration preference, check-in and check-out dates (especially for staggered group arrivals), meal-plan inclusions (if applicable), loyalty programme numbers, and any special requests (disability access, high floor, connecting rooms, cots, dietary needs). In tour operations, the rooming list is one of the critical pre-tour documents alongside the manifest, driver or guide briefings, and vouchers issued to passengers. It flows from the tour operator's booking and group-management system to the hotel's PMS (Property Management System). Errors in rooming lists — wrong names, wrong room counts, unspecified meal plans — are a primary cause of at-property complaints and compensation claims in group travel.

루밍 리스트는 일반적으로 계약상 마감일(통상 도착 30~7일 전)까지 호텔에 제출해야 하며, 지연 제출 시 호텔이 미확인 객실을 해제하거나 객실별 취소 수수료를 부과할 수 있다. 완전한 루밍 리스트에는 여권이나 신분증과 일치하는 투숙객 전체 이름, 투숙객별 객실 유형, 침대 구성 선호도, 체크인·체크아웃 일자, 식사 플랜 포함 사항, 로열티 프로그램 번호, 특별 요청 사항이 포함된다. 투어 운영에서 루밍 리스트는 매니페스트, 드라이버 또는 가이드 브리핑, 바우처와 함께 핵심 출발 전 문서 중 하나다.

# Distinctions
- **Rooming List** vs [Group Block](/lodging/hotel-rate/group-block.md) — A Group Block is the hotel's initial commitment to hold a quantity of rooms for a group at agreed terms; the rooming list is the subsequent operational document that populates that block with individual guest names and room assignments.
- **Rooming List** vs [Manifest](/tours/tour-ops/manifest.md) — A Manifest lists all passengers on a transport service (coach, vessel, aircraft); a rooming list is the accommodation-specific document that specifies how those same passengers are distributed among hotel rooms at a destination.
- **Rooming List** vs [Allotment](/lodging/hotel-rate/allotment.md) — An Allotment is a block of rooms (or other inventory) pre-committed to a tour operator or agent under agreed commercial terms; the rooming list is the specific operational document the operator sends to a hotel to assign guest names to the rooms within that allotment.
