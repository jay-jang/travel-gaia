> An optional excursion is a sightseeing trip, activity, or cultural experience offered to guests on an escorted or group tour package that is not included in the base price of the tour and must be purchased separately, typically on the day or in advance with the tour director. Optional excursions allow operators to keep the core package price competitive while providing guests with flexibility to select premium or specialized experiences at additional cost.

Optional excursions are a key ancillary revenue component for tour operators, offered beyond the included programme. They are typically presented by the tour director at an early "options meeting" or pre-sold online, and are capacity-limited. The operator adds a mark-up to the DMC's net cost; guests participate under the local activity operator's terms, with the tour operator acting as agent. Purchased optional excursions appear on the manifest for logistics coordination. They differ from free-time activities guests arrange independently and from pre/post-tour extensions.

**한국어 / Korean** — **선택 관광(Optional Excursion)** — 선택 관광은 에스코트 또는 그룹 패키지 투어에서 기본 가격에 포함되지 않아 별도로 구매해야 하는 관광·액티비티·문화 체험이다. 보통 투어 디렉터를 통해 당일 또는 사전에 신청하며 추가 비용이 발생한다. 선택 관광은 운영사가 핵심 패키지 가격을 경쟁력 있게 유지하면서 게스트에게 유연성을 제공한다.

선택 관광은 포함 프로그램 외에 제공되는 투어 운영사의 중요한 부가 수익 원천이다. 투어 초반 투어 디렉터 주관의 "옵션 미팅"에서 소개되거나 사전 온라인 판매되며, 정원이 제한된다. 운영사는 DMC의 순비용에 마크업을 추가하고, 게스트는 현지 액티비티 운영사의 약관에 따라 참여하며 투어 운영사는 대리인으로 행동한다.

# Distinctions
- **Optional Excursion** vs [Shore Excursion](/cruise/cruise/shore-excursion.md) — A shore excursion is sold by a cruise line at a port of call (cruise-industry concept); an optional excursion is the land-tour equivalent sold by an escorted-tour operator at a stop on a land itinerary. Both are add-on activities at extra cost, but the context (cruise vs. land tour) and distribution (cruise line vs. tour operator/DMC) differ.
- **Optional Excursion** vs [Inclusions](/tours/tour-product/inclusions.md) — Inclusions are services bundled into the base package price that every guest receives; optional excursions are extras that guests must additionally pay for. The included vs. optional split is a core pricing and product-design decision for tour operators.
