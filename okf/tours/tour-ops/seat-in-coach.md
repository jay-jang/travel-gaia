> A tour and ground-transfer configuration in which individual travellers purchase available seats on a scheduled shared-coach (bus) departure alongside other independently booked passengers, receiving guided or chauffeured service over a fixed itinerary at a lower cost than a private tour or exclusive vehicle.

Seat-in-Coach (SIC) is the most economical form of guided touring and shared ground transportation in the inbound and destination-management sectors. An operator runs a coach on a fixed schedule or circuit and sells individual seats to travellers sourced from multiple channels — hotels, cruise lines, FIT itineraries, or group blocks — who share the vehicle and guide. Because costs are divided across all passengers, SIC pricing is significantly lower than a private vehicle or exclusive tour. SIC transfers (airport ↔ hotel ↔ port) and SIC sightseeing tours are standard product types in DMC and receptive-operator catalogues, and the abbreviation appears universally in GDS ground segments, manifest systems, and OTA product feeds. The trade-off is reduced schedule flexibility: SIC runs on the operator's timetable and may include multiple pick-up stops.

**한국어 / Korean** — **좌석 공유 투어(Seat-in-Coach, SIC)** — 개별 여행자들이 다른 독립 예약 승객들과 함께 일정한 일정에 맞춰 운행되는 공유 코치(버스)의 좌석을 구매하는 투어·지상 이동 형태로, 프라이빗 투어나 전세 차량보다 낮은 비용으로 가이드 또는 기사 서비스를 제공받는다.

SIC는 인바운드 및 DMC 부문에서 가장 경제적인 공유 지상 이동 형태다. 운영자는 고정 일정·노선에 따라 코치를 운행하며, 호텔·크루즈 선사·FIT 일정·그룹 블록 등 여러 채널에서 온 여행자들에게 개별 좌석을 판매한다. 비용이 전체 승객에게 분산되므로 SIC 요금은 전세 차량이나 프라이빗 투어보다 현저히 낮다. 단점은 일정 유연성이 낮다는 점으로, SIC는 운영사의 시간표에 따라 운행되며 복수의 픽업 정류장을 포함할 수 있다.

**Aliases:** `SIC Tour`, `SIC Transfer`, `Seat in Coach`, `Shared Transfer`, `Shared Coach Tour`

# Distinctions
- **Seat-in-Coach** vs [Private Tour](/tours/tour-product/private-tour.md) — A Private Tour reserves the entire vehicle and guide for one group or family, offering full itinerary flexibility; Seat-in-Coach mixes independently travelling passengers on a shared vehicle and guide, reducing per-person cost but constraining departure times and routing.
- **Seat-in-Coach** vs [Small-Group Tour](/tours/tour-product/small-group-tour.md) — A Small-Group Tour caps total passengers (typically 12–16) to guarantee an intimate experience; Seat-in-Coach is defined by the shared-seat commercial model regardless of group size, and a SIC coach can carry significantly more passengers.
- **Seat-in-Coach** vs [Transfer](/tours/tour-ops/transfer.md) — A Transfer is point-to-point transportation with no sightseeing content; a Seat-in-Coach may be a simple shared transfer or a full guided sightseeing tour using the same shared-seat pricing model.
