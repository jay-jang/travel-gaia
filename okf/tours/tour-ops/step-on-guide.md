> A local, licensed professional guide who joins a motorcoach tour group or cruise shore-excursion vehicle at a specific destination — "stepping on" the vehicle at an agreed pickup point — to provide expert narration and on-site facilitation for a defined area or attraction, then disembarks before the group continues. Distinguished from a tour director, who accompanies the group throughout the entire multi-day itinerary.

Step-on guides are contracted by tour operators, DMCs, and cruise lines for their deep, destination-specific expertise — local history, culture, geography, and site-level logistics — that a travelling tour director cannot replicate. The guide boards the vehicle at an agreed point, narrates the segment, manages timing, handles site permits, and hands the group back at segment end. In many jurisdictions, a licensed local guide is legally required at world-heritage sites, national museums, or national parks. Commonly used for cruise shore excursions, motorcoach city days, and multi-destination itineraries requiring regional specialists.

**한국어 / Korean** — **스텝온 가이드(Step-On Guide)** — 특정 목적지에서 모터코치 그룹이나 기항지 투어 차량에 합류하여 해당 구역 전문 해설을 제공하고 하차하는 현지 면허 전문 가이드. 전체 일정을 동행하는 투어 디렉터와 구별된다. 세계문화유산 명소 등 많은 지역에서 현지 면허 가이드 이용이 법적 의무다.

# Distinctions
- **Step-On Guide** vs Tour Director — A tour director travels with the group for the entire multi-day itinerary managing all logistics; a step-on guide joins only for one destination segment, providing local expertise the tour director does not possess, then disembarks.
- **Step-On Guide** vs Guided Tour — A guided tour is the product (an escorted experience); a step-on guide is the professional role delivering guiding for one segment of a larger motorcoach or cruise itinerary.
