> A trained and often licensed professional who leads individuals or groups through destinations, attractions, or experiences, providing historical and cultural interpretation, logistical assistance, and safety awareness. Many jurisdictions require tour guides to hold a national or regional license; the World Federation of Tourist Guide Associations (WFTGA) sets international standards for training and professional certification. Distinct from a Tour Director (who manages overall group tour logistics across multiple days) and a Step-On Guide (who joins a moving tour for a single site or city).

Tour guides operate at specific sites (museum, archaeological site, city center) and are the primary interface between visitors and the destination's heritage, culture, and natural environment. Unlike the Tour Director, who manages group logistics throughout a multi-day tour, the tour guide's expertise is site-specific and often legally regulated: France, Italy, Greece, and many other countries require licensed guides for state museums, archaeological sites, and certain city zones. WFTGA defines the profession's competency framework, covering languages, local history, first aid, and group management. For OTA and tour operator distribution, the guide's language and license status are product attributes that OTAs and booking systems must capture to match travelers with the correct guide. Guide fees are typically included in the tour product price or disclosed as a separate line in the inclusions; tipping practices vary by culture and destination.

**한국어 / Korean** — **투어 가이드(Tour Guide)** — 목적지·관광명소·체험을 개인 또는 그룹에게 안내하며 역사·문화적 해설, 물류 지원, 안전 인식을 제공하는 훈련되고 종종 면허를 보유한 전문가. 많은 국가·지역에서 국가 또는 지역 면허 취득이 요건으로, 세계관광가이드연맹(WFTGA)이 훈련과 전문 자격에 관한 국제 기준을 수립하고 있다. 전체 그룹 투어 운영 물류를 다일정에 걸쳐 담당하는 투어 디렉터, 이동 중인 투어에 특정 명소·도시에서만 합류하는 스텝온 가이드와 구별된다.

투어 가이드는 특정 장소(박물관, 고고학 유적지, 도심 등)에서 활동하며 방문객과 목적지의 유산·문화·자연 환경 사이의 주요 접점 역할을 한다. 투어 디렉터와 달리 투어 가이드의 전문성은 특정 장소에 특화되며 법적으로 규제받는 경우가 많다. OTA 및 투어 운영사 유통에서 가이드의 언어와 면허 상태는 여행자와 적합한 가이드를 매칭하기 위해 반드시 수집해야 하는 상품 속성이다.

# Distinctions
- **Tour Guide** vs Tour Director — A Tour Director (or tour manager) accompanies a group for the entire duration of a multi-day tour, handling logistics, transport, and group cohesion throughout; a tour guide is a site-specific expert engaged for a particular attraction or city visit, often for a few hours.
- **Tour Guide** vs Step-On Guide — A Step-On Guide boards a moving tour bus or joins a group in progress for a specific site or city, typically for a partial day; a tour guide is a broader role designation that includes all on-site interpretive professionals, of which step-on guiding is one delivery format.
- **Tour Guide** vs Guided Tour — A guided tour is the product — a structured experience with a guide — while a tour guide is the professional who delivers it.
