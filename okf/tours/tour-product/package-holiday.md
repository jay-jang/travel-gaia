> A pre-arranged combination of at least two different types of travel services sold by a single organiser at an inclusive price for a trip exceeding 24 hours or including at least one overnight stay, with statutory consumer protections applying.

The legal definition under EU Directive 2015/2302 distinguishes a package from a linked travel arrangement (LTA): in a package all components are booked under a single contract with one organiser; in an LTA components are booked separately but through facilitated links (e.g., a hotel URL shown after booking a flight). The organiser bears full liability for all components; if a supplier fails, the organiser must remedy the issue or offer a refund. Insolvency protection (a bond or insurance) is mandatory for organisers selling packages in the EU/EEA. The minimum service combination for a package includes: (a) transport + accommodation; (b) transport + a tourist service accounting for ≥25% of package value; (c) accommodation + a tourist service; (d) transport + accommodation + tourist service. The 2018 revision (applicable from 1 July 2018) expanded the definition to capture online dynamic combinations and click-through packages. In the UK, the ATOL (Air Travel Organiser's Licence) scheme protects package holidays that include a flight booked through a UK-licensed organiser.

**한국어 / Korean** — **패키지 여행(Package Holiday)** — 단일 주최자가 최소 두 가지 이상의 서로 다른 여행 서비스를 통합 가격으로 제공하며, 여행 기간이 24시간을 초과하거나 최소 1박 이상 포함된 사전 기획 여행 상품. EU 지침 2015/2302(Package Travel Directive) 및 각국 동등 법률에 따라 법정 소비자 보호가 적용된다.

EU 지침 2015/2302의 법적 정의는 패키지와 연계 여행 편의(LTA)를 구분한다. 패키지는 모든 구성 요소가 단일 주최자와의 단일 계약하에 예약되고, LTA는 개별적으로 예약되지만 촉진 연결을 통해 이루어진다. 주최자는 모든 구성 요소에 대해 완전한 책임을 지며, 공급자 부도 시 해결 또는 환불을 제공해야 한다.

# Distinctions
- **Package Holiday** vs [Land Package](/tours/tour-ops/land-package.md) — A Land Package is the ground-only component excluding international flights; a Package Holiday is the legally defined combined product that typically includes transport and triggers full Package Travel Directive consumer protections.
- **Package Holiday** vs [FIT](/tours/tour-ops/fit.md) — FIT is a trip where each component is booked separately under individual contracts; a Package Holiday is a single-contract product assembled and sold by an organiser, triggering statutory protections FIT bookings do not receive.
- **Package Holiday** vs [Dynamic Packaging](/common/standards/dynamic-packaging.md) — Dynamic Packaging is a distribution technology that assembles components in real time; whether the resulting booking qualifies as a Package Holiday depends on whether it is sold under a single contract by a single organiser — many dynamic packages do qualify.
