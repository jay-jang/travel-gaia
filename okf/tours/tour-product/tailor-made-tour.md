> A travel itinerary built from scratch by a tour operator, travel consultant, or Destination Management Company specifically to match the unique requirements, preferences, dates, budget, pace, and interests of an individual client or private group. Every element — flights, accommodation selection, ground transport, private guides, activities, dining, and day-by-day pacing — is individually chosen rather than drawn from a pre-scheduled, fixed-departure group programme. No two tailor-made itineraries are identical; the product does not exist until it is constructed for that specific client.

Tailor-made touring (also called bespoke touring or custom touring) is today the primary product category of specialist luxury and adventure tour operators, as well as travel management company (TMC) premium leisure desks. The tour operator or travel agent conducts a detailed consultation — interests, travel style, accommodation preferences, activity levels, dietary requirements, special occasions — before constructing day-by-day itineraries with pre-booked accommodation, private transfers, locally assigned expert guides, and curated activities. Pricing is always per-trip rather than per-seat, and the itinerary is adjusted through an iterative process until the client confirms. Under UK law (Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018), a tailor-made package retains full Package Travel Regulations protection when it combines two or more travel services (e.g., flight + accommodation) sold through a single seller, even though the itinerary is unique to the client. ABTA explicitly distinguishes "tailor-made packages" from "ready-made packages" — tailor-made are "customised to suit each customer's preferences and budget" while the latter are pre-constructed by the operator and sold to multiple clients from fixed inventory. Tailor-made is the fastest-growing product segment in premium leisure travel, driven by demand for unique, personalised experiences and specialist operators who can design remote or complex multi-country itineraries.

맞춤형 여행(비스포크 투어라고도 함)은 럭셔리·어드벤처 전문 투어 운영사의 주요 상품 범주이다. 관심사·여행 스타일·숙박 선호도·활동 수준 등을 상세히 상담한 후 일별 일정을 구성하고, 고객이 확인할 때까지 반복적으로 조율한다. 가격은 항상 좌석 단위가 아닌 여행 단위로 책정된다. ABTA는 "맞춤형 패키지"를 "기성 패키지"와 명확히 구분하며, 두 형태 모두 동등한 법적 보호를 받는다.

# Distinctions
- **Tailor-Made Tour** vs [FIT](/tours/tour-ops/fit.md) — FIT (Free/Fully Independent Travel) is the broad booking category for any non-group, individually ticketed travel. Tailor-made touring is a subset of FIT in which a professional operator assembles and manages all logistics on the client's behalf according to a custom design. A traveller doing their own Booking.com + budget airline booking is also FIT, but not tailor-made.
- **Tailor-Made Tour** vs [Escorted Tour](/tours/tour-product/escorted-tour.md) — An Escorted Tour is a pre-designed group product with fixed departure dates, a fixed route, and a fixed roster of accommodation and activities; strangers join as a group and travel with a tour escort. A tailor-made tour is privately designed for one party, starts on the client's chosen dates, follows a custom route, and typically uses private guides at each destination.
- **Tailor-Made Tour** vs [Private Tour](/tours/tour-product/private-tour.md) — A Private Tour is a single activity or day-excursion reserved exclusively for one party with no strangers joining. A tailor-made tour is a multi-day, multi-element journey where every component is custom-selected; it may incorporate several private day-tours, but the concept is qualitatively broader.
