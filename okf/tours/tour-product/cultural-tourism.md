> Cultural tourism is travel motivated by the desire to learn, discover, experience, and consume the tangible and intangible cultural attractions and products of a destination — encompassing arts, architecture, historical heritage, culinary heritage, literature, music, creative industries, and living cultures. It accounts for an estimated 40% of global tourism and is one of the fastest-growing segments of the industry.

UNWTO's operational definition from its 22nd General Assembly (2017) reads: "Cultural tourism is a type of tourism activity in which the visitor's essential motivation is to learn, discover, experience and consume the tangible and intangible cultural attractions/products in a tourism destination. These attractions/products relate to a set of distinctive material, intellectual, spiritual and emotional features of a society that encompasses arts and architecture, historical and cultural heritage, culinary heritage, literature, music, creative industries and the living cultures with their lifestyles, value systems, beliefs and traditions." In practice, cultural tourism encompasses museum and gallery visits, heritage site tours, performing arts and festival attendance, religious and pilgrimage travel, language-immersion programmes, artisan workshops, and architectural itineraries. Destination Management Organisations (DMOs) increasingly treat cultural tourism as a resilience strategy, diversifying demand beyond mass-beach and urban-leisure segments. Subsets include Heritage Tourism (tangible and intangible heritage sites), Culinary Tourism (food and beverage culture), Dark Tourism (sites of tragedy and mortality), and Religious Tourism, each sharing the cultural motivation but differing in primary experience.

**한국어 / Korean** — **문화 관광(Cultural Tourism)** — 문화 관광은 목적지의 유형·무형 문화 어트랙션과 상품을 배우고 발견하며 체험하고 소비하려는 동기에서 비롯된 여행으로, 예술, 건축, 역사 유산, 음식 유산, 문학, 음악, 창의 산업, 생활문화 등을 아우른다. UNWTO 제22차 총회(2017)에서 주요 관광 활동 유형으로 정의하며, 전 세계 관광의 약 40%를 차지하고 가장 빠르게 성장하는 분야 중 하나이다.

UNWTO 운영 정의에 따르면 "문화 관광은 방문자의 핵심 동기가 목적지의 유형·무형 문화 어트랙션/상품을 배우고 발견하며 체험하고 소비하는 것인 관광 활동 유형"이다. 실무적으로는 박물관·갤러리 방문, 유산 사이트 투어, 공연예술·축제 참가, 종교·순례 여행, 어학 연수 등을 포함한다. 하위 유형으로는 Heritage Tourism, Culinary Tourism, Dark Tourism 등이 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Cultural Tourism** vs [Heritage Tourism](/tours/tour-product/heritage-tourism.md) — Heritage Tourism is a subset of Cultural Tourism specifically focused on tangible sites (historic monuments, archaeological parks, UNESCO World Heritage sites) and intangible manifestations (traditional crafts, oral traditions, living heritage). Cultural Tourism is the broader category encompassing heritage alongside arts, creative industries, performing arts, and contemporary living cultures.
- **Cultural Tourism** vs [Ecotourism](/tours/tour-product/ecotourism.md) — Ecotourism centres on experiencing and conserving natural environments and biodiversity; Cultural Tourism centres on a destination's human-created or culturally significant heritage, arts, and living cultures. Agritourism and indigenous-community tourism are the zones where natural and cultural motivations legitimately overlap.
