> A packaged travel product spanning two or more consecutive days, combining overnight accommodation with transport, guided activities, and typically some meals into a single purchasable itinerary.

Multi-day tours are structurally more complex than day tours: they require accommodation contracting, multi-night logistics, day-by-day programming, and crew (drivers, guides, assistants) management across the full run. Commercial variants range from escorted coach tours (a fixed itinerary with set departure dates and a Tour Director throughout) and small-group adventure tours (e.g., 8–15 pax, camping or lodges) to luxury private journeys and self-guided cycling/hiking routes (accommodation pre-booked but no guide). Revenue comes from a single all-inclusive or semi-inclusive price per person. The Guaranteed Departure model — where a specific departure runs even at minimum pax — is the standard commitment mechanism for scheduled multi-day products. Distribution reaches consumers via OTAs (Viator, GetYourGuide, Klook), specialist adventure OTAs (Intrepid, G Adventures own channels), and traditional travel agents/tour operators.

**한국어 / Korean** — **다박 투어(Multi-Day Tour)** — 2일 이상에 걸쳐 숙박을 포함하고, 교통·가이드 동행 액티비티·일부 식사를 단일 구매 가능한 일정으로 묶은 패키지 여행 상품. 전문 투어 오퍼레이터의 핵심 상품 세그먼트이며, 당일 액티비티·입장권·자유 여행(FIT)과 구별된다.

다박 투어는 당일 투어보다 구조적으로 복잡하다. 숙박 계약, 여러 박에 걸친 물류, 일정별 프로그램 기획, 전 여정에 걸친 승무원(운전기사·가이드·보조 스태프) 관리가 필요하다. 상업적 형태는 고정 일정의 에스코티드 코치 투어(전 일정에 Tour Director 동반), 소그룹 어드벤처 투어(예: 8–15명, 캠핑 또는 로지), 럭셔리 프라이빗 여정, 셀프 가이드 사이클링/하이킹 루트(숙박 선예약, 가이드 없음)까지 다양하다. 수익은 1인당 단일 올인클루시브 또는 세미인클루시브 가격에서 나온다. 특정 출발일이 최소 인원에서도 운영되도록 보장하는 Guaranteed Departure 모델이 정기 다박 상품의 표준 약정 방식이다.

**Aliases:** `Multi-Night Tour`, `Extended Tour`, `Multi-Day Experience`, `Multi-Day Trip`

# Distinctions
- **Multi-Day Tour** vs [FIT](/tours/tour-ops/fit.md) — FIT is an independently assembled trip where the traveller books each component separately; a Multi-Day Tour is pre-packaged by an operator and sold as a single product at an inclusive per-person price.
- **Multi-Day Tour** vs [Guided Tour](/tours/tour-product/guided-tour.md) — A Guided Tour is typically a single-session or single-day activity with a guide; a Multi-Day Tour spans two or more overnights and includes accommodation and sequential daily programming.
- **Multi-Day Tour** vs [Escorted Tour](/tours/tour-product/escorted-tour.md) — An Escorted Tour is a specific style of multi-day travel (coach-based, fixed group, professional Tour Director throughout); Multi-Day Tour is the broader product category that encompasses escorted tours as well as small-group, self-guided, and private formats.
