> Medical tourism is the practice of travelling outside one's country of residence specifically to receive planned medical, dental, or surgical treatment — often in pursuit of lower costs, shorter waiting times, higher quality care unavailable at home, or access to procedures not approved in the home country. UNWTO's Exploring Health Tourism (2018) distinguishes medical tourism from wellness tourism by the clinical nature of the intervention involved.

Medical tourism has historically flowed from lower-income to higher-income countries for access to advanced technology; increasingly it flows from higher-income countries to destinations such as Thailand, India, Mexico, South Korea, Turkey, and Hungary, where prices for elective procedures (dental implants, hip replacements, cardiac surgery, IVF, bariatric surgery, cosmetic surgery) can be 50–80% lower than in North America or Western Europe. Key quality and safety considerations include accreditation (Joint Commission International / JCI is the primary international accreditation body for hospitals serving medical tourists), medical record portability, aftercare continuity, and medical liability jurisdiction. The Medical Tourism Association (MTA) publishes Global Healthcare Accreditation standards and the annual World Medical Tourism Congress. Risks include infection from different microbial environments, complications requiring follow-up care at home, and limited legal recourse when procedures go wrong abroad. UNWTO's Exploring Health Tourism (2018) defines a health tourism visitor as "a visitor whose primary purpose of the visit is to improve their health" and sub-classifies into medical tourism (clinical interventions) and wellness tourism (preventive/restorative experiences).

**한국어 / Korean** — **의료 관광(Medical Tourism)** — 의료 관광은 계획된 의료·치과·외과 치료를 받기 위해 거주 국가 밖으로 여행하는 행위로, 흔히 더 낮은 비용, 짧은 대기 시간, 국내에서 이용할 수 없는 고품질 의료 서비스, 또는 본국에서 승인되지 않은 시술에 대한 접근을 위해 이루어진다.

의료 관광은 역사적으로 저소득국에서 고소득국으로의 이동 흐름이었으나, 점점 더 고소득국에서 태국, 인도, 멕시코, 한국, 터키, 헝가리 등 선택적 시술 비용이 50~80% 저렴한 목적지로의 이동이 증가하고 있다. 주요 품질 고려 사항으로는 JCI 인증, 의무 기록 이식성, 사후 관리 연속성 등이 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Medical Tourism** vs [Wellness Tourism](/tours/tour-product/wellness-tourism.md) — Wellness Tourism is travel for preventive, restorative, and lifestyle-enhancement experiences (spa, yoga, meditation, fitness retreats) without a clinical medical procedure as the primary purpose. Medical Tourism involves a planned clinical intervention — surgery, dental treatment, diagnostic testing, or medical consultation — and the traveller is in a patient role rather than a leisure-wellness role. The two may be combined in "medical wellness" itineraries but the primary motivation and regulatory context differ fundamentally.
