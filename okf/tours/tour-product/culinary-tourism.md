> Culinary tourism (also called food tourism or gastronomic tourism) is travel for which the primary motivation is to experience the food and beverage culture of a destination — through restaurant visits, cooking classes, food and drink tours, market visits, farm and winery experiences, and culinary festivals. Defined by the World Food Travel Association (WFTA) as a recognised subset of cultural tourism, it is classified by UNWTO as a key driver of destination competitiveness and sustainable tourism development.

Erik Wolf, founder of the World Food Travel Association (WFTA, established 2001), introduced and popularised the concept of "food tourism" as "the pursuit and enjoyment of unique and memorable food and drink experiences." UNWTO's Global Report on Food Tourism (2012) and Second Global Report on Gastronomy Tourism (2017) documented food tourism as one of the fastest-growing travel segments, accounting for a significant share of tourism expenditure at many destinations and closely tied to destination brand identity. Culinary tourism encompasses a wide product spectrum — from Michelin-starred restaurant pilgrimages and chef's-table experiences to street food tours, cooking schools, agritourism (farm visits, harvest festivals, olive oil or wine tasting), and specialty food trails (cheese routes, whisky trails). It overlaps with cultural heritage tourism when food is treated as intangible cultural heritage under UNESCO frameworks. Destination management organisations (DMOs) increasingly structure formal culinary tourism strategies, and UNWTO and WFTA have published guidance for destinations on designing and marketing culinary tourism products.

**한국어 / Korean** — **음식 관광(Culinary Tourism)** — 음식 관광(Culinary Tourism)은 목적지의 음식·음료 문화를 체험하는 것을 주된 동기로 하는 여행으로, 레스토랑 방문, 요리 클래스, 음식·음료 투어, 시장 방문, 농장·와이너리 체험, 음식 축제 등이 포함된다. WFTA가 문화 관광의 공인 하위 분야로 정의하며, UNWTO는 목적지 경쟁력과 지속가능 관광 발전의 핵심 동력으로 분류한다.

WFTA 창립자 Erik Wolf는 음식 관광을 "독특하고 기억에 남는 음식·음료 체험을 추구하고 즐기는 것"으로 정의했다. UNWTO의 《제2차 미식 관광 글로벌 보고서》(2017)는 이 분야를 빠르게 성장하는 관광 세그먼트로 기록하며, 미슐랭 스타 레스토랑부터 길거리 음식 투어, 농촌 관광, 특산물 트레일까지 광범위한 상품 스펙트럼을 다루고 있다.

# Distinctions
- **Culinary Tourism** vs [Wellness Tourism](/tours/tour-product/wellness-tourism.md) — Wellness Tourism is motivated by personal health improvement — physical, mental, or spiritual restoration; Culinary Tourism is motivated by cultural and gustatory experience of a destination's food heritage. The two may overlap in healthy-cuisine retreats or mindful-eating programmes, but serve fundamentally different primary purposes.
- **Culinary Tourism** vs [Ecotourism](/tours/tour-product/ecotourism.md) — Ecotourism centres on experiencing and conserving natural environments and biodiversity; Culinary Tourism centres on experiencing a destination's food and beverage culture. Agritourism — farm visits, harvest participation, and nature-based food sourcing — is the zone where both legitimately overlap.
- **Culinary Tourism** vs [Dark Tourism](/tours/tour-product/dark-tourism.md) — Dark Tourism is travel to sites of death, tragedy, or historical atrocity motivated by education, empathy, or contemplation; Culinary Tourism is travel motivated by positive sensory and cultural engagement with food heritage. Both are special-interest subsets of cultural tourism but serve entirely different experiential and motivational purposes.
