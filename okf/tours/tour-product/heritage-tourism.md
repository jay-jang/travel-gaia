> Heritage tourism is travel motivated by visiting places and participating in activities that authentically represent the stories, people, and history of a destination — spanning tangible heritage (historic monuments, archaeological sites, UNESCO World Heritage properties, historic districts) and intangible heritage (traditional crafts, oral traditions, performing arts, festivals, and living customs).

Heritage tourism encompasses two broad domains. (1) Tangible heritage tourism: visits to UNESCO World Heritage Sites (numbering over 1,200 as of 2024), national monuments, historic city centres, archaeological parks, historic railways, industrial heritage sites, and battlefield memorials. Properties inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List act as significant tourism anchors, with the designation itself functioning as a quality signal and demand generator. (2) Intangible heritage tourism: participation in traditional festivals, artisan workshops (pottery, weaving, glassblowing), indigenous community experiences, oral storytelling traditions, and living heritage expressions inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Heritage management bodies must balance conservation obligations with tourism access, a tension framed in ICOMOS and UNESCO guidance documents. Heritage tourism is a primary revenue source for many historic cities and archaeological sites, but overtourism risk — carrying-capacity breaches at vulnerable monuments — is a growing concern requiring visitor management planning.

**한국어 / Korean** — **유산 관광(Heritage Tourism)** — 유산 관광은 목적지의 이야기, 인물, 역사를 진정성 있게 나타내는 장소를 방문하고 활동에 참여하는 것을 동기로 하는 여행으로, 유형 유산(역사적 기념물, 고고학 유적, UNESCO 세계유산, 역사 지구)과 무형 유산(전통 공예, 구전 전통, 공연예술, 축제, 생활 관습)을 아우른다.

유산 관광은 유형 유산(UNESCO 세계유산 사이트, 국가 기념물, 역사 도심, 고고학 공원, 전적지)과 무형 유산(전통 축제, 장인 워크숍, 원주민 공동체 체험, 구전 전통) 두 영역을 포괄한다. 유산 관리 기관은 보존 의무와 관광 접근성 사이의 균형을 유지해야 하며, 오버투어리즘 위험이 점점 커지고 있어 방문자 관리 계획이 중요하다.

# Distinctions
- **Heritage Tourism** vs [Cultural Tourism](/tours/tour-product/cultural-tourism.md) — Cultural Tourism is the broader category covering all travel motivated by cultural attractions — arts, architecture, music, creative industries, and living cultures as well as heritage. Heritage Tourism is a specific subset focused primarily on tangible historical sites and intangible living heritage expressions, and does not necessarily encompass contemporary arts or creative-industry experiences.
- **Heritage Tourism** vs [Ecotourism](/tours/tour-product/ecotourism.md) — Ecotourism is travel to natural areas that conserves the environment and supports local communities; Heritage Tourism is travel to cultural or historical sites and experiences. The two overlap when indigenous communities blend natural-landscape stewardship with living cultural heritage — for example, in national parks co-managed with First Nations or Aboriginal groups.
