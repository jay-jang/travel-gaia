> Accessible tourism (also "Tourism for All") is the ongoing practice of designing and delivering travel destinations, products, and services so that people with disabilities, elderly travellers, families with young children, and others with access needs can participate in tourism on an equal basis with other travellers. UNWTO defines it as ensuring access to the physical environment, transportation, information, communications, and a wide range of public facilities and services. It is integrated into the GSTC Criteria as a baseline social-sustainability standard.

Accessible tourism encompasses three dimensions: (1) physical access — barrier-free environments, wheelchair-accessible venues, adapted transport; (2) information access — WCAG-compliant websites, audio guides, Braille materials, sign-language video content; (3) attitudinal/service access — disability-trained staff, mobility equipment availability, and tour formats adapted for cognitive or sensory needs. The UNWTO "Recommendations on Accessible Tourism for All" (2013) provide the global reference framework; the EU European Accessibility Act (Directive 2019/882) creates binding rules for tourism digital services from June 2025. The market is estimated to cover 1.3 billion people with disabilities worldwide (WHO) plus a much larger population of elderly and temporarily mobility-restricted travellers.

**한국어 / Korean** — **무장벽 관광(접근 가능 관광 / 모두를 위한 관광)** — 무장벽 관광은 장애인, 고령자, 영유아 동반 가족 등 접근성 필요가 있는 여행자가 다른 여행자와 동등하게 관광에 참여할 수 있도록 여행 목적지·상품·서비스를 설계·제공하는 지속적인 실천이다. UNWTO 2013년 권고안이 글로벌 기준 프레임워크를 제공하며, EU 유럽 접근성법은 2025년 6월부터 관광 디지털 서비스에 구속력 있는 규칙을 적용한다.

# Distinctions
- **Accessible Tourism** vs [PRM](/common/customer/prm.md) — PRM governs disability assistance in air/airport operations; accessible tourism covers the full travel experience—destinations, activities, accommodation—beyond the aircraft and terminal.
- **Accessible Tourism** vs [Ecotourism](/tours/tour-product/ecotourism.md) — Ecotourism focuses on environmental sustainability; accessible tourism focuses on social inclusivity. The two are complementary and can be combined.
- **Accessible Tourism** vs [Wellness Tourism](/tours/tour-product/wellness-tourism.md) — Wellness tourism is a travel motivation; accessible tourism is a design standard that should apply to all tourism products including wellness.
