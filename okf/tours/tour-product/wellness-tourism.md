> Travel in which the primary motivation is to maintain or enhance personal wellbeing — physical, mental, or spiritual — through activities such as spa treatments, fitness retreats, mindfulness programmes, or therapeutic natural environments. Distinct from medical tourism in that wellness travellers are typically already healthy and seek enhancement or prevention rather than treatment of illness.

The Global Wellness Institute (GWI) defines wellness tourism as "travel associated with the pursuit of maintaining or enhancing personal wellbeing." It is one of the fastest-growing travel segments, generating an estimated USD 651 billion in expenditure in 2017 and projected to grow to over USD 1 trillion by 2027. Wellness tourism overlaps significantly with spa travel, retreat travel, nature-based tourism, and cultural heritage tourism. The UNWTO classifies wellness as a key motivational category alongside adventure, culture, and business travel. Product formats range from dedicated wellness resorts (medi-spas, yoga retreats, Ayurveda centres) to wellness add-ons within mainstream hotels (fitness facilities, healthy menus, sleep programmes). A core distinction is that wellness tourists travel to feel better, whereas medical tourists travel to receive clinical treatment.

**한국어 / Korean** — **웰니스 관광(Wellness Tourism)** — 신체적·정신적·영적 건강을 유지하거나 향상하는 것을 주된 동기로 하는 여행으로, 스파 트리트먼트·피트니스 리트리트·마음챙김 프로그램·치유 자연환경 등의 활동을 포함한다. 웰니스 여행자들은 일반적으로 이미 건강한 상태에서 증진·예방을 추구한다는 점에서 의료 관광과 구별된다.

GWI는 웰니스 관광을 "개인 웰빙을 유지하거나 향상하려는 추구와 관련된 여행"으로 정의한다. 2017년 지출액이 약 6,510억 달러에 달하며 2027년까지 1조 달러를 초과할 것으로 전망되는 고성장 세그먼트다. 상품 형태는 전용 웰니스 리조트(메디스파·요가 리트리트·아유르베다 센터)부터 일반 호텔의 웰니스 부가 서비스(피트니스 시설·건강 메뉴·수면 프로그램)까지 다양하다.

# Distinctions
- **Wellness Tourism** vs [Ecotourism](/tours/tour-product/ecotourism.md) — Ecotourism is motivated by experiencing and conserving natural environments; Wellness Tourism is motivated by personal health improvement. The two frequently overlap (nature-based wellness retreats), but have distinct primary purposes.
- **Wellness Tourism** vs [Adventure Tourism](/tours/tour-product/adventure-tourism.md) — Adventure Tourism centres on physical challenge and excitement in natural or remote settings; Wellness Tourism centres on restoration, healing, and wellbeing enhancement, though active wellness (yoga treks, fitness camps) can overlap with adventure.
