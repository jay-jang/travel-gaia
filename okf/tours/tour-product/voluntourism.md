> Voluntourism is a form of tourism in which travelers combine leisure travel to a destination with organized volunteer work — such as conservation, community construction, education, or care — typically facilitated by an NGO, social enterprise, or specialist tour operator. Participants pay program fees that cover accommodation, in-country logistics, and a contribution to the host project. Voluntourism sits at the intersection of sustainable tourism and experiential travel, and is recognized by the UNWTO as a niche tourism segment with significant growth.

Voluntourism programs range from short stays of one to two weeks to multi-month placements, and span sectors including wildlife conservation, habitat restoration, orphanage support, medical assistance, disaster relief, and language teaching. The industry is served by specialist organizations such as Projects Abroad, Earthwatch Institute, and Global Vision International. Critics note risks of "voluntourist dependency", displacement of local workers, and projects designed to meet traveler preferences rather than genuine community needs. Best-practice guidelines from organizations such as Learning Service and the GSTC emphasize long-term community partnerships, skills matching, and transparent fee allocation. Voluntourism is a sub-segment of responsible tourism and is increasingly framed within regenerative travel.

**한국어 / Korean** — **자원봉사 관광(볼런투어리즘)** — 볼런투어리즘은 여행자가 목적지로의 여가 여행과 NGO·사회적 기업 또는 전문 여행사가 주선하는 자원봉사 활동을 결합하는 관광 형태다. 참가자는 숙박, 현지 물류, 주관 프로젝트에 대한 기여금을 포함하는 프로그램 비용을 지불한다. 볼런투어리즘은 UNWTO가 상당한 성장세를 보이는 틈새 관광 세그먼트로 인정한다.

# Distinctions
- **Voluntourism** vs [Ecotourism](/tours/tour-product/ecotourism.md) — Ecotourism focuses on experiencing and conserving natural environments with minimal impact; voluntourism involves active contribution of labor or skills to a project (environmental or social), making the volunteer work central rather than incidental to the trip.
- **Voluntourism** vs [Regenerative Tourism](/tours/tour-product/regenerative-tourism.md) — Regenerative tourism is a broad philosophy of travel that leaves destinations better than found, encompassing choices by any traveler; voluntourism is a specific form of travel product organized around structured volunteer placements, usually via a facilitating organization.
