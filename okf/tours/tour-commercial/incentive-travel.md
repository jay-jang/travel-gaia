> A form of motivational group travel fully funded by a sponsoring company to reward employees, channel partners, or dealers for achieving defined performance targets. It is one of the four core segments of MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) and is characterised by aspirational destinations, bespoke programming, and full cost underwriting by the sponsoring organisation.

Incentive travel is distinguished from ordinary corporate travel by its experiential and motivational character: the trip itself is the reward. The industry body SITE (Society for Incentive Travel Excellence) defines incentive travel as "a global management tool that uses an exceptional travel experience to motivate and/or recognise participants for increased levels of performance in support of organisational goals." Programmes are designed to be aspirational — destinations, activities, and hospitality levels that participants would not typically purchase themselves — and are wholly funded by the sponsoring company. Buyers are typically corporate incentive managers and Travel Management Companies (TMCs) collaborating with Destination Management Companies (DMCs) and specialist incentive agencies. Budget per head commonly ranges from a few hundred to several thousand US dollars. The segment is sensitive to economic cycles and corporate governance regulations, particularly rules capping entertainment or gift expenditure.

**한국어 / Korean** — **인센티브 여행(Incentive Travel)** — 성과 목표를 달성한 임직원, 채널 파트너 또는 딜러를 보상하기 위해 후원 기업이 전액 비용을 부담하는 동기 부여형 단체 여행. MICE(회의·인센티브·컨퍼런스·전시)의 핵심 네 가지 세그먼트 중 하나로, 열망적인 목적지, 맞춤형 프로그램, 후원 기업의 전액 경비 지원을 특징으로 한다.

인센티브 여행은 체험적·동기부여적 성격에서 일반 출장과 구별된다. 여행 자체가 보상이다. 업계 단체인 SITE(Society for Incentive Travel Excellence)는 인센티브 여행을 "조직의 목표 달성을 위해 참가자의 성과 수준을 동기부여하고/또는 인정하는 글로벌 경영 도구로서 탁월한 여행 경험을 활용하는 것"으로 정의한다. 프로그램은 열망적으로 설계된다. 즉 참가자가 스스로 구매하지 않을 목적지, 액티비티, 수준 높은 환대를 제공하며 후원 기업이 전액 지불한다. 구매자는 일반적으로 기업 인센티브 담당자이며, DMC 및 전문 인센티브 에이전시와 협력하는 TMC와 함께 일한다.

# Distinctions
- **Incentive Travel** vs [MICE](/lodging/hotel-dist/mice.md) — MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions) is the collective category; incentive travel is specifically the motivational reward-travel segment within it, distinct from conferences, meetings, and trade exhibitions.
- **Incentive Travel** vs [FIT](/tours/tour-ops/fit.md) — FIT (Fully Independent Travel) is individually arranged travel where the traveller self-funds and self-plans; incentive travel is group-based, company-funded, and planned by the sponsoring organisation.
