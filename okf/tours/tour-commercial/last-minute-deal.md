> A discounted price offered for inventory remaining unsold close to the departure date; used to fill capacity, recover contribution margin, and reduce spoilage — the temporal mirror of the Early Bird Discount.

Last-minute deals typically appear within 0–30 days of departure, though the window varies by product type: tour activities and day trips may offer discounts from 48–72 hours out, while ocean cruises may drop prices 30–90 days prior to sailing. The discount mechanism ranges from a percentage reduction on the standard retail rate to a lower net rate offered to resellers. Platforms such as Viator, GetYourGuide, and Tiqets surface last-minute tours under dedicated filter or promotional sections. Key tension: aggressive last-minute discounting can train price-sensitive consumers to delay purchase, cannibalising early bookings and eroding yield; operators therefore usually manage the depth and timing of last-minute discounts carefully, sometimes gating them to specific channels (app-only deals) or loyalty members.

**한국어 / Korean** — **막판 할인(Last-Minute Deal)** — 출발일이 임박해 미판매로 남은 재고에 대해 제공하는 할인 가격. 잔여 정원을 채우고, 좌석 공백(spoilage)을 줄이기 위해 활용되며, 얼리버드 할인의 시간적 반대 개념이다.

막판 할인은 일반적으로 출발 0~30일 전에 나타나지만, 상품 유형에 따라 창이 다르다. 투어 액티비티·당일 투어는 출발 48~72시간 전부터, 크루즈는 출항 30~90일 전에 가격을 내리기도 한다. Viator·GetYourGuide·Tiqets 등 플랫폼은 전용 필터에서 막판 투어를 노출한다. 공격적인 막판 할인은 가격 민감 소비자에게 구매를 미루도록 훈련시켜 얼리 예약을 잠식할 수 있어, 오퍼레이터는 할인의 폭과 시기를 신중히 관리한다.

# Distinctions
- **Last-Minute Deal** vs [Early Bird Discount](/tours/tour-commercial/early-bird-discount.md) — An Early Bird Discount rewards travellers who book well in advance, incentivising forward commitment; a Last-Minute Deal discounts close to departure to clear unsold inventory. Together they represent the two ends of a time-based pricing spectrum.
- **Last-Minute Deal** vs [Demand-Based Pricing](/tours/tour-commercial/demand-based-pricing.md) — Demand-Based Pricing is the dynamic pricing strategy that adjusts rates across the booking window in response to demand signals; a Last-Minute Deal is one specific tactical outcome of that strategy applied when close-in demand is insufficient to fill remaining inventory.
