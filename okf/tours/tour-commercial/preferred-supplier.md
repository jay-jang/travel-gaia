> A Preferred Supplier is a supplier — hotel, airline, car rental company, tour operator, or activity provider — with whom a travel agency or tour operator has established a formal commercial relationship that grants enhanced commission rates, override incentives, or reduced booking fees in exchange for volume commitments and preferred placement in sales recommendations or marketing materials.

Preferred supplier programs are a cornerstone of travel agency and tour operator commercial strategy. By concentrating bookings with a smaller set of vendors, agents and operators earn elevated commissions (often tiered by volume), marketing support, and co-op funds. In return, the preferred supplier gains predictable booking volume, priority access in agent GDS displays or booking tools, and inclusion in agents' training and promotional materials. Preferred supplier agreements typically specify: a minimum annual booking volume or revenue commitment, enhanced base commission rates above standard, override commission thresholds (e.g., additional 2–5% above base when quarterly volume targets are met), marketing development funds (MDF), and exclusivity or preferred placement rules. In the tour distribution context, a preferred supplier may be a DMC or ground handler whose services a tour operator features exclusively in a destination, or a hotel chain with a chain-wide preferred relationship with a travel consortium (e.g., Virtuoso, Signature Travel Network).

**한국어 / Korean** — **우선 공급업체(프리퍼드 서플라이어)** — 우선 공급업체(Preferred Supplier)는 여행사 또는 투어 운영사가 판매 권고 또는 마케팅 자료에서 물량 약속 및 우선 배치를 조건으로 강화된 커미션 요율, 오버라이드 인센티브 또는 낮은 예약 수수료를 제공하는 공식 상업 관계를 체결한 공급업체를 말한다.

우선 공급업체 프로그램은 여행사 및 투어 운영사 상업 전략의 핵심 요소다. 더 적은 수의 공급업체에 예약을 집중함으로써 여행사와 운영사는 상향된 커미션·마케팅 지원·공동 협력 자금을 받는다. 이에 대해 우선 공급업체는 예측 가능한 예약 물량, GDS 디스플레이 또는 예약 도구에서의 우선 노출, 여행사의 교육 및 프로모션 자료 포함 등의 혜택을 얻는다. 우선 공급업체 계약에는 최소 연간 예약 물량·강화된 커미션 요율·오버라이드 커미션 기준·마케팅 개발 자금(MDF)·독점 또는 우선 배치 규칙 등이 명시된다.

# Distinctions
- **Preferred Supplier** vs [Override Commission](/tours/tour-commercial/override-commission.md) — An Override Commission is the additional commission earned above the standard rate when volume targets are met — it is one financial mechanism within a preferred supplier agreement. A Preferred Supplier relationship is the broader commercial arrangement that may include override commissions but also encompasses marketing support, priority placement, and co-op funding.
