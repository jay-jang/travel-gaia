> A retrospective volume-based payment from a tour supplier to a distribution partner, calculated and paid after a performance period based on achieved booking targets. Unlike commission (deducted per transaction) or override commission (a higher ongoing rate), a rebate is a lump sum paid after targets are audited — not per booking.

Rebates are a demand-stimulus and partner-loyalty instrument in the B2B tours-and-activities supply chain. A supplier sets tiered booking-volume or gross-value targets in a distribution agreement (e.g. USD 100,000 in confirmed bookings earns a 2% rebate; USD 250,000 earns 3%), and the distributor receives a lump-sum payment after the period closes and targets are audited. Because rebates are retrospective and contingent, they do not alter per-booking economics in real time — the agent books at the contracted agent rate throughout the year — but the prospect of a year-end payment incentivizes the distributor to consolidate volumes toward preferred suppliers rather than splitting them across competitors.

**한국어 / Korean** — **리베이트(Rebate)** — 투어·액티비티 공급자가 성과 기간 이후 달성된 예약 볼륨 또는 총액 기준에 따라 유통 파트너에게 소급 지급하는 볼륨 기반 장려금. 커미션(건별 공제)이나 override commission(높은 지속 비율)과 달리, 리베이트는 목표 달성 여부 확인 후 지급되는 일시금이다.

공급자는 유통 계약에 단계별 예약 볼륨 목표를 설정하고, 기간 종료 후 목표 달성 여부 검증 뒤 일시금을 지급한다. 리베이트는 소급·조건부이므로 건별 예약 경제성에는 영향을 미치지 않지만, 볼륨을 선호 공급자에게 집중하도록 유도한다.

# Distinctions
- **Rebate** vs [Override Commission](/tours/tour-commercial/override-commission.md) — An override commission is a higher ongoing commission rate applied to every booking once a performance target is met; a rebate is a separate lump-sum payment made after the period closes based on total volume achieved.
- **Rebate** vs [Marketplace Commission](/tours/tour-commercial/marketplace-commission.md) — Marketplace commission is deducted from each booking transaction (a per-booking cost for the supplier); a rebate flows in the opposite direction — from supplier to distributor — as a reward for aggregate performance.
