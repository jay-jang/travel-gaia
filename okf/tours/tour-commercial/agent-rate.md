> A negotiated wholesale or discounted rate offered by a tour operator to travel agents, OTAs, and trade distribution partners, enabling the reseller to earn a margin through markup or commission while remaining price-competitive on consumer channels.

Agent rates are the backbone of the B2B distribution layer in tours and activities. They are typically quoted as a net rate (exclusive of any commission) or as a retail rate with a negotiated commission percentage. The gap between the agent rate and the retail rate is the reseller's gross margin; the operator controls this margin through its confidential tariff and minimum selling price (MSP). Agent rates are commonly structured in tiers: platinum/gold/silver tiers based on volume, or differentiated by channel type (traditional travel agent, OTA, DMC). They are usually governed by a contract specifying payment terms, cancellation policy, marketing restrictions, and rate confidentiality obligations (preventing the reseller from advertising below a floor price). Agent rates are distinct from group rates, which are size-triggered rather than channel-triggered.

**한국어 / Korean** — **에이전트 요금(Agent Rate)** — 투어 운영자가 여행사·OTA·무역 유통 파트너에게 제공하는 협상 기반의 도매 또는 할인 요금으로, 리셀러가 마크업 또는 커미션을 통해 마진을 확보하면서도 소비자 채널에서 가격 경쟁력을 유지할 수 있게 한다.

에이전트 요금은 투어·액티비티 B2B 유통 계층의 근간이다. 통상 순 요금(커미션 미포함)이나 합의된 커미션 비율이 적용된 소매 요금으로 견적된다. 에이전트 요금과 소매 요금의 차이가 리셀러의 총마진이며, 운영자는 기밀 관세율표(confidential tariff)와 최소 판매 가격(MSP)을 통해 이 마진을 관리한다. 에이전트 요금은 볼륨 기반의 플래티넘·골드·실버 등급, 또는 채널 유형(일반 여행사·OTA·DMC)에 따라 계층화되는 경우가 많다. 결제 조건·취소 정책·마케팅 제한·요금 기밀 유지 의무를 명시한 계약서에 의거하는 것이 일반적이다. 에이전트 요금은 채널 기반이 아닌 규모 기반인 단체 요금과 구별된다.

**Aliases:** `Trade Rate`, `Wholesale Rate`, `B2B Rate`, `Reseller Rate`

# Distinctions
- **Agent Rate** vs [Confidential Tariff](/tours/tour-commercial/confidential-tariff.md) — A confidential tariff is the document or rate sheet listing agent rates and the terms under which they apply; the agent rate is the specific price point extracted from that tariff for a given product. The tariff governs the rate; the rate is the value.
- **Agent Rate** vs [Group Rate](/tours/tour-commercial/group-rate.md) — An agent rate is triggered by who is buying (a trade partner); a group rate is triggered by how many are buying (minimum pax count). A trade partner booking a group may be eligible for both simultaneously.
