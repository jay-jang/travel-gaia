> The per-person tariff for ground arrangements only — accommodation, transfers, guiding, meals, entrance fees, and other surface services — in a package tour, explicitly excluding air or sea transportation. Operators use it to quote modular pricing to agents who have their own air contracts.

In tour-operator pricing the land rate is the cost of all destination-side services netted out of the full package price. Tour wholesalers publish land rates in tariff sheets for travel agencies that prefer to book air separately (often on their preferred carrier or using frequent-flyer seats). The land rate includes the net cost of hotels (at contracted rates), in-destination transfers, guiding, sightseeing admissions, and sometimes meals, plus the operator's margin. When airlines have their own holiday divisions the term "land-only supplement" describes the extra charge above the air-only seat price. ABTA and ATOL regulation treat land-only bookings differently from air-inclusive packages in terms of financial protection requirements.

**한국어 / Korean** — **랜드 요금(Land Rate)** — 패키지 투어에서 항공·해상 교통을 제외한 지상 서비스(숙박·이동·가이드·식사·입장료 등)만에 대한 1인당 요금. "랜드 온리(land-only)" 가격이라고도 한다.

투어 오퍼레이터 가격 책정에서 랜드 요금은 전체 패키지 가격에서 도착지 측 서비스 원가 전부를 분리한 것이다. 랜드 요금에는 협약 요율로 계약된 호텔 원가, 현지 이동, 가이드, 관광 입장료, 일부 식사, 그리고 오퍼레이터 마진이 포함된다.

**Aliases:** `Land-Only Rate`, `Land-Only Price`, `Land-Only Supplement`, `Ground-Only Rate`

# Distinctions
- **Land Rate** vs [Land Package](/tours/tour-ops/land-package.md) — A land package is the bundled collection of ground services sold as a unit; the land rate is the monetary price assigned to that bundle per person, which may be expressed at net (wholesale) or retail level.
- **Land Rate** vs [Retail Rate](/tours/tour-commercial/retail-rate.md) — The retail rate is the selling price presented to the end consumer inclusive of the operator's margin; the land rate may be quoted at either net or retail level — the key distinction is that the land rate excludes any air or sea transport component.
