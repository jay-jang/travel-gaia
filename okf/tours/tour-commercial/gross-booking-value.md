> The total customer-facing retail value of all bookings transacted through a platform or marketplace in a given period, before deducting any commissions, refunds, or platform fees; GBV is the headline volume metric used by OTAs, experience marketplaces, and tour operators to measure distribution scale and market share.

GBV represents the total value of transactions flowing through a platform as seen by the consumer, regardless of how much of that value the platform retains. For an experiences marketplace, GBV equals the sum of all activity and attraction-ticket prices paid by customers; the platform's own revenue is then the GBV multiplied by the take rate (marketplace commission percentage). GBV is widely used in OTA investor reporting (Booking Holdings, Expedia Group, TripAdvisor/Viator) and in market-sizing research (e.g., Arival estimates the global tours and activities sector's GBV). A key distinction is that GBV is a gross metric and can be large relative to revenue; a marketplace reporting strong GBV growth may still have thin margins depending on take rates, refund volumes, and cancellations.

**한국어 / Korean** — **총 예약 금액(GBV)** — 특정 기간 동안 플랫폼이나 마켓플레이스를 통해 거래된 모든 예약의 고객 대면 소매 총액으로, 커미션·환불·플랫폼 수수료 차감 전 금액. GBV는 OTA, 익스피리언스 마켓플레이스, 투어 운영사가 유통 규모와 시장 점유율을 측정하는 데 사용하는 핵심 볼륨 지표이다.

GBV는 플랫폼이 얼마나 많은 금액을 보유하는지와 무관하게 소비자가 보는 플랫폼 거래 총액을 나타낸다. 익스피리언스 마켓플레이스의 경우 GBV는 고객이 지불한 모든 액티비티·어트랙션 티켓 가격의 합이며, 플랫폼 자체 수익은 GBV에 테이크율(마켓플레이스 커미션 비율)을 곱한 금액이다. GBV는 OTA 투자자 보고(Booking Holdings, Expedia Group, TripAdvisor/Viator)와 시장 규모 연구에서 광범위하게 사용된다. GBV는 그로스(총액) 지표이며 수익 대비 규모가 클 수 있다는 점이 핵심 특징이다.

# Distinctions
- **Gross Booking Value (GBV)** vs [Marketplace Commission](/tours/tour-commercial/marketplace-commission.md) — GBV is the total transaction value flowing through the marketplace; marketplace commission is the percentage of GBV that the marketplace retains as its revenue. Multiplying GBV by the take rate gives the marketplace's net revenue.
- **Gross Booking Value (GBV)** vs [Operator Payout](/tours/tour-commercial/operator-payout.md) — GBV is the full price charged to the consumer; operator payout is what remains after the marketplace deducts its commission and any payment-processing fees — typically GBV minus the commission percentage.
- **Gross Booking Value (GBV)** vs [Net Rate](/lodging/hotel-rate/net-rate.md) — A net rate is the wholesale price the operator offers to a reseller to mark up; GBV is the aggregate sum of all retail prices paid across a platform's entire book of business, applicable to both net-rate and commission models.
